Game Evening

Curated session lineups wrapping existing COSYgames titles into structured party night formats with seamless URL handoff.

🎲 Live Facilitator Session Guide

Host a seamless 60–75 minute Game Evening using this structured party framework:

⏱️ Phase 1: Icebreaker (15 mins)

Launch Game 1 (e.g. Hot Seat) to warm up vocabulary and build low-pressure group energy.

🔥 Phase 2: Main Event (30 mins)

Play Games 2 & 3 (e.g. Battle of Wits / Story Chain) in rounds with small teams or pairs.

☕ Phase 3: Break Point (10 mins)

Informal open chatter and score recap before the final celebratory round.

🏆 Phase 4: Finale (15 mins)

Conclude with Game 4 (e.g. Lucky Numbers or 100 Questions) and award fun non-competitive prizes.

💡 Facilitator Tip: Clicking any "Launch Game" button automatically opens the standalone COSYgames engine with pre-configured language, level, and topic parameters. Game Evening does not host or reimplement game mechanics.
