🟢 Round 2 : Deep Speculation: Agree or Disagree? (10 Statements)▲
Debate
Marketing has successfully convinced society that physical rest is the only valid reward for hard work, encouraging a sedentary existence.
★ Do you agree that sitting down feels like a reward, or do you view movement as true relaxation?
Overcoming mental inertia is far harder today than a century ago because digital entertainment is designed to keep us motionless.
★ Is it easier for you to start a workout or to continue binge-watching a show?
The endless pursuit of convenience reduces human resilience and makes us overly dependent on corporate services.
★ Would you survive a week without any food delivery or digital navigation services?
A seductive advertising campaign can make sitting on a couch feel like an act of self-care rather than passive laziness.
★ How do you distinguish between genuine self-care and avoiding responsibility?
We should actively resist the current normality where physical inactivity is encouraged by urban infrastructure.
★ Do you walk or bike when possible, or rely on motor vehicles for short distances?
Unchecked consumerism thrives when citizens are sedentary because inactive people consume more digital media and delivered goods.
★ Do you notice yourself buying more online items when you spend the day indoors?
The ubiquitous presence of smartphones ensures that even when we walk, our minds remain sedentary in virtual spaces.
★ Can you go for a 30-minute walk without checking your phone once?
Having a subscription for every possible home service has replaced active travel as the ultimate status symbol.
★ What subscription service do you value most in your daily routine?
Subtle psychological conditioning by ad agencies has re-engineered our brains to equate physical exertion with suffering.
★ Did you grow up viewing physical exercise as fun or as a punishment?
True vitality can only be restored when we consciously reject artificial conveniences and reconnect with active living.
★ What is one habit you could change tomorrow to bring more active movement into your life?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
Marketing makes us to be sedentary. → Marketing makes us sedentary. (Grammar check: 'make' as a causative verb takes an object and bare adjective or base verb without 'to be')
We are used to sit all day. → We are used to sitting all day. (Grammar check: 'be used to' is followed by a gerund `-ing` form)
