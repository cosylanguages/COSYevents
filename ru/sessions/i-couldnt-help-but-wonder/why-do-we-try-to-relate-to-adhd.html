⚡ Grammar Practice : Adverbs of Degree & Mitigators▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Use adverbs of degree (virtually, barely, slightly, highly, somewhat, mildly) to express precise levels of intensity and carefully qualify psychological claims.

  4. The student felt _____ distracted, but managed to complete the exam successfully.
  5. Psychologists are _____ critical of self-diagnoses made on social media platforms.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I think everyone have ADHD these days. → I think everyone has ADHD these days. (Subject-verb agreement: 'Everyone' is singular and requires 'has'.)
We discussed about mental health topics. → We discussed mental health topics. (The verb 'discuss' is transitive and does not take the preposition 'about'.)
He is highly sensitive about distractions. → He is highly sensitive to distractions. (Preposition check: the adjective 'sensitive' is followed by 'to'.)
