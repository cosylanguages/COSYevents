⚡ Grammar Practice : Passive Reporting Verbs▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Upper-Intermediate (B2))

Use passive reporting verbs (it is said that, is believed to, is thought to be, is considered to) to talk about general beliefs, customs, and traditional expectations without mentioning a specific subject.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I am agree with keeping old heirlooms. → I agree with keeping old heirlooms. (Verb usage: 'agree' is a verb, not an adjective, so do not use 'am'.)
It is believed that traditions to provide community comfort. → It is believed that traditions provide community comfort. (Passive Reporting: When starting with "It is believed that", use a full finite clause instead of an infinitive.)
Traditions are passed down since centuries. → Traditions have been passed down for centuries. (Preposition choice: use 'for' to indicate the duration of an action, and use present perfect passive.)
