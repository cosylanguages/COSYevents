Keeping Up with Science

Real science news, made discussable. Each session unpacks a recent discovery or trend across various scientific areas — tailored for A2, B1-B2, and C1 levels. No lab coat required, just curiosity.

🔬 About Keeping Up with Science & Preparation Guidelines

Who it's for: This club is designed for A2, B1-B2, and C1 language levels. It is especially useful for university students, researchers, and professors — though anyone curious to test whether a session is easy or challenging for them is always welcome!

Obligatory Pre-Session Preparation: Before attending a session, it is mandatory to read the assigned research article or read/listen to our attached summary on the official Cosyscience Podcast:

🎙️ Mave Stream 🍏 Apple Podcasts 🎧 Spotify 📺 YouTube Playlist

Make sure to check the session vocabulary and engage in a quick pre-reflection before joining the call.

💡 Golden Rule: No translations, try to explain! If you find explaining concepts without translating difficult, check out our blog post Stop Translating.
