🎙️ Audio Briefing Transcription / Science Digest ▼

Can a computer algorithm speak with perfect grammatical fluency yet be completely out of touch with reality? Large language models can generate eloquent paragraphs that sound convincing, yet contain absolute hallucinations and distorted beliefs. In this session, we explore why human minds easily mistake syntactic fluency for objective truth, debate how AI shapes our perception of reality, and master evaluative vocabulary. Join us to challenge your cognitive biases, analyze artificial logic, and engage in a thrilling debate on the boundaries of machine truth!

📖 Session Vocabulary

Delusion
a belief that is maintained despite being contradicted by reality or rational argument.
He was under the delusion that he could win without training.
Reinforce
to strengthen or support (an object, idea, or feeling).
The study's results reinforce the theory of climate change.
Reassurance
the action of removing someone's doubts or fears.
Isolated people often use AI companions to seek comfort and emotional reassurance.
Vulnerable ≠ Resilient
susceptible to physical or emotional attack or harm ≠ able to withstand or recover quickly from difficult conditions.
Lonely people might be more vulnerable to emotional manipulation by AI, so we must help them stay resilient.
Conspiracy theory
a belief that some covert but influential organization is responsible for an unexplained event.
Many conspiracy theories spread rapidly through online forums.
Hallucination
(in AI) a confident response by an AI that does not seem to be justified by its training data.
Large language models sometimes provide "hallucinations" as factual information.
Validation
recognition or affirmation that a person or their feelings or opinions are valid or worthwhile.
Talking to a nonjudgmental chatbot can provide a sense of emotional validation.
Distorted
pulled or twisted out of shape; giving a misleading or false account or impression.
His view of the event was distorted by his anger.
Flourish
to grow or develop in a healthy or vigorous way.
With constant validation, false beliefs can easily flourish and take root.
Elaborate
detailed and complicated in design or planning.
Conspiracy theories can become much more elaborate when an AI helps construct them.

⚡ Grammar Practice : Causative Verbs (make, let, have, get)▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Upper-Intermediate (B2))

Causative verbs describe how a person, AI, or event causes another to act or feel. Use 'make / let' + object + base form of verb (no 'to'). Use 'get' + object + infinitive ('to' + verb). Use 'have' + object + base form of verb.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
It makes me feeling vulnerable→It makes me feel vulnerable(Make + infinitive)
I am using AI for three months→I have been using AI for three months(Present Perfect Continuous)
The AI can hallucinate easy→AI can hallucinate easily(Adverb)
It's a dangerous technology→It's dangerous technology / a dangerous piece of technology(Uncountable technology)
I am agree with you→I agree with you(Verb agreement)
