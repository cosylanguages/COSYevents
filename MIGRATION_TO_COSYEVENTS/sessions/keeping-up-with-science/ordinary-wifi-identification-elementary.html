An Elementary (A2) science session on how standard WiFi routers can identify people by their walking movements and body shapes.

📖 Session Vocabulary

WiFi ≠ cable
a wireless way to connect computers and phones to the internet.
Our home WiFi works in every room.
Signal ≠ silence
waves that carry information through the air.
The WiFi signal gets weak near the balcony.
Router ≠ computer
a device that sends internet signals to your devices.
Restart the router if the internet stops working.
Wall ≠ door
a vertical side of a room.
WiFi signals can pass through wooden walls.
Identify ≠ confuse
to recognize who someone is.
A phone can identify your face to unlock.
Walk ≠ run
to move on foot at a regular pace.
Each person has a unique way they walk.
Secret ≠ public
something that is kept hidden from others.
Keep your personal WiFi password secret.
Smart ≠ basic
having or showing intelligence or computer technology.
Smart homes use WiFi to turn on lights.
Safe ≠ dangerous
not in danger or likely to be hurt.
We want our personal data to be safe.
Test ≠ ignore
to examine something to see if it works.
Scientists tested the new WiFi system in an office.

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Elementary (A2))

Use can + base verb to talk about modern technological capabilities (e.g., Ordinary WiFi can identify people through walls).

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
WiFi can to see people. → WiFi can see people. (Modal 'can' takes base verb without 'to'.)
It recognize my walked. → It recognizes my walk / gait. (3rd person singular present tense 'recognizes', and use noun 'walk'.)
