Duration

Languages

🇬🇧 English

Level

Elementary (A2)

Theme

Technology & Mindset

Discuss the quote: 'We should turn off AI for a week to see if people's brains still work' from an Elementary (A2) level. Practice modal verbs, daily habits, and screen breaks.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Artificial ≠ natural
made by humans rather than occurring naturally.
AI programs can write simple texts very fast.
Brain ≠ computer
the organ in the head that controls thought and memory.
Your brain needs practice to stay smart.
Turn off ≠ turn on
to stop a machine or device from working.
I turn off my computer before going to sleep.
Depend ≠ standalone
to need someone or something for help.
Many students depend on spell checkers.
Memory ≠ forgetting
the ability to remember things.
Writing by hand improves your memory.
Calculate ≠ guess
to discover an amount using numbers.
He can calculate simple math problems mentally.
Screen ≠ paper
the flat display surface on a device.
Staring at a phone screen all day tires your eyes.
Practice ≠ laziness
doing an activity repeatedly to improve.
Daily math practice keeps your mind sharp.
Skill ≠ ignorance
the ability to do something well.
Navigating without GPS is a useful map skill.
Smart ≠ foolish
having or showing quick intelligence.
Solving puzzles keeps seniors smart and active.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What comes to your mind when you hear about this topic?
  • How does this subject relate to your daily habits?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Should for Suggestions & Advice (A2)▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Elementary (A2))

Use should + base verb to give advice or express strong suggestions (e.g., We should turn off AI for a week).

Complete the rule: Modal verb 'should' is followed by a _____ without 'to'.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. We _____ use our brains more often.
  2. You should _____ your phone during dinner.
  3. They should _____ writing on paper.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

Give your group partner one piece of advice about using technology.

🔵 Round 1 : Exploration & Analysis▲
Do you think people depend too much on smartphone apps today?
★ What app do you use most frequently every day?
Can you calculate simple shopping prices without using a phone calculator?
★ How good are you at mental math?
How does turning off screens for a day help your brain rest?
★ Have you ever tried spending a Sunday without internet?
What digital skill would you miss most if technology stopped working?
★ Can you find locations using a printed map?
🟣 Let's Speak Together▲

A comparative reflection on our habits and self-perceptions.

🟢 Round 2 : Debates & Future Outlook▲
Schools should ban AI tools for homework writing. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Did you write your homework by hand when you were young?
People would become smarter if we turned off AI for a week. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What would be hardest about living without AI for one week?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
We should to turn off AI. → We should turn off AI. (Modal verb 'should' is followed by base verb without 'to'.)
My brain is need rest. → My brain needs rest. (Use standard present simple verb 'needs'.)
