🎬 Session Vocabulary

1. Generic Discussion Vocabulary

psychoanalysis
the psychological examination of personality traits, deep motives, and unconscious behavior.
MI5 officer Eve Polastri conducts informal psychoanalysis on female assassins, noticing patterns that male agents ignore.
mutual obsession
an intense, reciprocal fixation where two people become deeply preoccupied with each other.
The dynamic between Eve and Villanelle evolves from an intelligence investigation into a dangerous mutual obsession.
spy espionage
the practice of employing undercover agents and secret intelligence techniques to gather information.
Eve navigates international spy espionage while tracking a clandestine syndicate known as The Twelve.
fluid power dynamics
shifting relations where authority, control, and dominance constantly swing between two opponents.
Their encounters in London, Paris, and Rome feature fluid power dynamics where pursuer and target swap roles.
moral corruption
the gradual erosion of ethical boundaries and personal values through exposure to danger or obsession.
Eve experiences steady moral corruption as her pursuit of Villanelle distances her from her husband and normal life.

2. Authentic Film Quotes & Slang

handler
(spy jargon / noun) an intelligence officer who manages, directs, and supervises covert field agents or assassins.
Konstantin serves as Villanelle's handler, providing her with target dossiers and trying to keep her chaotic behavior under control.
psychopath
(noun / psychological term) a person suffering from chronic mental disorder with abnormal social behavior and lack of empathy.
Eve accurately predicts that the assassin is a psychopath who enjoys luxury, attention, and high-end fashion.
I think about you all the time
(pivotal quote) Eve's famous confession revealing her deep obsession during their face-to-face kitchen confrontation.
When Villanelle corners Eve in her apartment, Eve admits "I think about you all the time," to which Villanelle replies "I know."
I just want to have dinner with you
(iconic dialogue quote) Villanelle's bizarre, casually domestic statement during a high-stakes standoff.
Villanelle breaks the tension of a home break-in by disarmingly stating, "I just want to have dinner with you."
The Twelve
(proper noun / syndicate alias) a secret, shadowy international criminal organization that hires top assassins.
Both MI6 and Villanelle herself are constantly trying to uncover the true motives and leaders behind The Twelve.

🟣 Cinema Spotlight : Scene Study & Adaptation▲

Compare the adaptation differences and visual contrasts in this film.

Novel vs. Television Adaptations
• Adapted from Luke Jennings' novella series Codename Villanelle; showrunner Phoebe Waller-Bridge added rich dark comedy and expanded Eve's character.
• In the books, Eve is a passive investigator, whereas the screen adaptation turns her into an active, morally complex equal to Villanelle.
Visual Directing & Stylistic Aesthetics
• Glamorous, sun-drenched European capitals contrast with dark, narrow London interiors to reflect Eve's claustrophobia and Villanelle's freedom.
• The show utilizes bold, oversized location title cards across the screen accompanied by moody 1960s-style indie pop tracks.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I'm used to watch movies in original→I'm used to watching movies in originalThe phrase 'be used to' is followed by a gerund (-ing form), not a bare infinitive.
The reason is because she was afraid→The reason is that she was afraidAvoid 'the reason is because'; use 'the reason is that' for formal/style clarity.
Seldom I have seen such an ending→Seldom have I seen such an endingNegative adverbs like 'seldom' at the beginning of a clause trigger subject-auxiliary inversion.
