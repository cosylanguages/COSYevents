⚖️ Perspective Mirror

How does sound shape our human identity? Reflecting on acoustic environments, living with hearing loss, and appreciating silence vs. vibrant soundscapes.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Auditory ≠ Visual
relating to the sense of hearing.
Auditory processing helps us interpret music and speech.
Acoustics ≠ Muffledness
the properties or qualities of a room or building that determine how sound is transmitted.
The acoustics in the cathedral made the choir sound magnificent.
Decibel ≠ Silence
a unit used to measure the intensity of a sound.
Concerts often reach loud decibel levels.
Tinnitus ≠ Clarity
ringing or buzzing in the ears.
Constant tinnitus can make sleeping difficult.
Impairment ≠ Full capability
the state or fact of being impaired or damaged.
Hearing impairment affects millions of people worldwide.
Sensory ≠ Numb
relating to sensation or the physical senses.
A walk in the forest offers rich sensory stimulation.
Vibration ≠ Stillness
a continuous rapid oscillation or shaking motion.
Deaf dancers feel the rhythmic vibration of the bass.
Subtitled ≠ Uncaptioned
provided with captions translating or transcribing the dialogue.
Watching subtitled films helps with language learning.
Overstimulation ≠ Calm
excessive stimulation of the senses.
Urban noise pollution causes auditory overstimulation.
Silence ≠ Noise
complete absence of sound.
Finding moments of silence restores mental peace.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up (10 mins)▲

Welcome! Share your initial thoughts on this topic. What personal experiences or observations come to mind right away?

⚡ Grammar Practice : Expressing Preferences & Contrast (B1-B2)▲
Use contrastive markers to compare soundscapes: "Whereas city streets present constant overstimulation, rural retreats offer peaceful silence."
🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)▲
How does city noise pollution impact our long-term auditory health?
★ How noisy is your daily living environment?
What role do subtitled media play in accessibility for people with hearing impairment?
★ Do you prefer watching movies with subtitles turned on?
How do people experiencing tinnitus cope with loud ambient sound?
★ Have you ever experienced temporary ringing in your ears after a concert?
Why is tactile vibration important in sign language and deaf performing arts?
★ How do you respond to ambient rhythm and acoustic music?
Does urban overstimulation make intentional silence more valuable today?
★ Where do you go when you want complete quiet?
🟢 Round 2: Deep Speculation & Future Horizon (Units 6–10)▲
How might advancements in hearing aid technology eliminate severe hearing impairment?
★ How do you think medical technology will improve human senses in 20 years?
Why is designing buildings with proper acoustics essential for public accessibility?
★ What is your favorite quiet architectural spot?
How does losing one sensory channel alter a person's perception of space?
★ If you had to spend 24 hours without sound, what would you miss most?
Should cities enforce maximum decibel limits in residential quarters at night?
★ What quiet hours rules exist in your neighborhood?
How can society build more inclusive environments for the deaf community?
★ Have you ever learned any sign language phrases?
