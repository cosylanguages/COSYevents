🗣️ Speaking Club Session Framework

Use this structured turn-taking framework for live group discussion or solo practice:

  1. Warm-up Round (5 mins): Each participant introduces themselves and gives a 1-sentence quick response to the first card.
  2. Deep Dive Rounds (25 mins): Rotate turn-taking for each prompt card. One speaker shares their main answer (60-90s), followed by follow-up questions from peers.
  3. Synthesis & Debrief (10 mins): Review key vocabulary and insights gathered during the discussion.

💡 Framing Note for Hypothetical Prompts (e.g., "If You Were"): While designed for live group turn-taking and role-play, hypothetical prompts can also serve solo learners as creative journaling or writing prompts. Reflect in writing for 5–10 minutes per prompt to practice target grammar structures (conditionals, speculative phrasing).
