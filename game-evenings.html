Game Evenings

Connect, compete, and converse! Our Game Evenings bring learners together through highly interactive, social, and communicative language games-designed beautifully to play both offline (couch & classroom) and online (Zoom) together.

← All Live Events

🎯 The Power of Language Play

Research in language pedagogy shows that games are not just "fun" fillers-they are powerful cognitive tools. By shifting focus from abstract grammar rules to winning the round, games bypass the affective filter (fear of making mistakes). You begin to formulate arguments, describe complex scenarios, and react with spontaneous expressions naturally.

👥 Hybrid Cohesion Designed to bridge online groups on screen-shares and in-person friends gathering around a table.
⚡ Spontaneous Speech Develop natural reactions, conversational strategies, and slang beyond regular textbooks.

🎲 Curated Game Deck

Launch any of these selected multi-player hits directly in your group session with a teacher or friends.

🎭 Forehead / Screen Play

Action Hero

Hold your smartphone to your forehead with the screen facing the camera or room. Others must describe the secret vocabulary term in the target language while you guess as many as possible before the clock runs out!

Offline Mode Pass phone around the circle
Online Mode Host describes, player closes eyes
Pedagogy Rapid paraphrasing & synonyms
▶ Launch Action Hero
⚖️ Breakout / Team Play

Battle of Wits

Two hot topics, two contrasting sides. Players are split into opposing camps to draft arguments, formulate debate strategies, and speak for 2 minutes to win the jury's final ballot.

Offline Mode Opposite sides of the table
Online Mode Breakout rooms for team huddles
Pedagogy Structuring discourse & rhetoric
▶ Launch Battle of Wits
👤 Interactive Group Play

Identity Mystery

The host is secretly assigned a profession or famous personality. The other players take turns asking yes/no questions in the target language to decode the hidden persona in fewer than 20 questions.

Offline Mode Host displays screen secretly
Online Mode Host receives private Zoom message
Pedagogy Formulating question syntax
▶ Launch Identity Mystery
🃏 Sentence Chain Play

Story Chain

Build an unpredictable, humorous story together, one sentence at a time. Each player must secretly draw a target vocabulary word and weave it naturally into their turn without raising suspicion.

Offline Mode Circle-based speech pacing
Online Mode Sequence in Chat / Turn order
Pedagogy Seamless syntax weaving
▶ Launch Story Chain
🔗 Cooperative Screen Play

Word Linker

Test your vocabulary depth! Examine four target words on the screen. Work together to spot the odd one out, define their hidden relationship, and establish semantic connections.

Offline Mode Projected screen on the wall
Online Mode Screen-share with group chat input
Pedagogy Deep lexical associations
▶ Launch Word Linker
🔢 Multi-Player Listening

Lucky Numbers (Bingo)

Practice number recognition and spelling with absolute focus. The Caller synthesizes and speaks numbers/letters aloud in the target language while players mark their cards to complete a row!

Offline Mode Printable paper grids
Online Mode Digital cards on personal devices
Pedagogy Aural numbers/letters parsing
▶ Launch Lucky Numbers
