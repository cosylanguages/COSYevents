COSY brand colours

Reference for the teal signal ramp. Nothing on the live site uses these steps yet — this page is the review surface before any page adopts them.

The two brand colours

#0ABBB5 --cosy-signal · logo primary · 2.39:1 on white
#0CA2B0 --cosy-signal-secondary · 3.08:1 on white

Both already existed in the canonical design system. Neither can carry body text: at 2.39:1 and 3.08:1 they fail the 4.5:1 threshold. They are fill and decoration.

Rules

do Text starts at 700

Links, body copy, icon labels and any interactive text use --cosy-signal-700 or darker. It clears 4.5:1 on cream, which is the background the site actually uses.

do Tints for surfaces

50, 100 and 200 work as backgrounds under normal ink. They are the natural home for cards, banners, table rows and hover states.

don't Text on the brand fill

White on #0ABBB5 is 2.39:1. If a button is filled with the logo teal, the label is unreadable for many people. Fill with 700 instead.

don't 600 as body text on cream

#0CA2B0 reaches 3.08:1 on pure white but only 2.89:1 on cream. It is safe for large display type on white cards, nothing smaller.
