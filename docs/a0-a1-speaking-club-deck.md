# A0–A1 Speaking Club — Six-Card Deck (themes and worked sessions)

Working document for the club rewrite. The delivery rules, the chunk ladder and the
60-minute timings live in [basic-speaking-club-teacher-manual.md](basic-speaking-club-teacher-manual.md);
this file is the theme bank: two sessions already live on the site (01, 02) and four
are new (03–06).

Each session follows the same six cards: ten words, one opinion question, three
this-or-that choices, one real-life mini-action, one forward-looking frame, and a
takeaway. Card 4 is the only card that gets more time when a group is slow.

## Session 01 — Remote Work & Café Culture

**Card 4 scenario:** order a drink, ask for the Wi-Fi password, find a quiet table
**Prop:** a paper cup and a café menu with three prices

### Card 1 — Intro + 10 vocabulary units (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| laptop | a laptop | LAP-top |
| Wi-Fi | the Wi-Fi symbol | WAI-fai |
| coffee | a cup | KOFF-ee |
| desk | a desk | desk |
| chair | a chair | chair |
| meeting | people at a table | MEE-ting |
| call | a phone at your ear | kaal |
| quiet | finger on lips | KWAI-et |
| noisy | hands over ears | NOI-zee |
| break | a clock and a cup | brayk |

**Warm-up:** "Point to something on your desk right now. What is it?"

Round 1: you say the word, the group repeats, they point at the picture.
Round 2: pairs — A says a word, B points. Then swap.

### Card 2 — General discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I think… / For me… / It is good / It is bad / Maybe

**Question:** "Is a café good for work? Why or why not?"

**Model:** "For me, a café is noisy, so it is bad for calls."

Follow-ups if the room is quiet: "Do you work at home? Do you like it? Where do you
drink your coffee in the morning?"

### Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B] / [A] is better for me

| This | Or that |
|---|---|
| working online at home | working in an office |
| hot coffee | iced tea |
| morning meetings | afternoon meetings |

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line. Stronger students
add one reason: "…because it is quiet."

### Card 4 — Real-life mini-action (15 min)

**Scenario:** A is a barista, B is a remote worker. Order a drink, ask for the
Wi-Fi password, ask where a quiet table is.

**Frames:**

- One coffee, please.
- How much is it?
- What is the Wi-Fi password?
- Is that chair free?
- Yes, of course. / Sorry, it is not free.

**Run it:** demo with a strong student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles
(5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

### Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

**Chunks:** Tomorrow, I want to… / Next week, I will…

**Prompts:** "Where will you work tomorrow? What break will you take today?"

Push for a place and a time, not an opinion: "Tomorrow I want to work at home at
nine o'clock."

### Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

**Wrap-up:** "Say one word from today + one place you want to work next week."

Board the three best sentences, plus the three corrections from your paper:
"we said / we can say".

## If the group is…

- **weaker:** cut Card 3 to two choices, and let Card 4 be read from the card
  word for word. Reading is speaking for a beginner.
- **stronger:** add a Card 4 complication — the café has no Wi-Fi today, and the
  quiet table is taken. They have to solve it, not just order.
## Session 02 — Street Food, Apps & Delivery

**Card 4 scenario:** group lunch order on a €15 budget
**Prop:** a four-item menu with prices, printed big enough to read across the table

### Card 1 — Intro + 10 vocabulary units (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| burger | a burger | BER-ger |
| noodles | a bowl of noodles | NOO-dels |
| spicy | a chilli | SPAI-see |
| sweet | a cake | sweet |
| delivery | a bag on a scooter | de-LIV-er-ee |
| app | a phone screen | ap |
| order | a finger on a screen | OR-der |
| hungry | hand on stomach | HUNG-gree |
| cheap | a small price tag | cheep |
| hot | steam | hot |

**Warm-up:** "Are you hungry right now? What food is in your phone's delivery app?"

Round 2: pairs sort the ten pictures into "I like" and "I don't like" piles, then
say one line each.

### Card 2 — General discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I love… / I don't like… / It is delicious / Too [spicy / expensive]

**Questions:** "Is delivery food cheap or expensive in your city? Do you like
spicy food?"

**Model:** "I love noodles, but delivery is too expensive for me."

### Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I prefer… / I always choose… / Never [A], always [B]

| This | Or that |
|---|---|
| ordering on an app | cooking at home |
| spicy noodles | a sweet dessert |
| street food | a sit-down restaurant |

"Never Monday, always Friday" is a useful extra frame here — it lets A0 students
answer with a day instead of a reason.

### Card 4 — Real-life mini-action (15 min)

**Scenario:** pairs look at the four-item menu. They have €15 in total and must
agree on two items to order together.

**Frames:**

- How much is the burger?
- Do you want noodles?
- I want this one.
- Let's order this.
- That is €14. / Too expensive.

**Run it:** demo one pair (2 min) → pairs decide and write their order (5 min) →
each pair reports to the room: "We want one burger and one noodles. It is €14."
(5 min) → swap partners and do a second order with €10 (3 min).

The €10 round is where the real negotiation happens. Keep it.

### Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

**Chunks:** This weekend, I want to eat… / Tonight, I will…

**Prompts:** "What do you want to eat tonight? Will you cook or order?"

### Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

**Wrap-up:** "Name your favourite food word from today in one short sentence."

Board: "we said / we can say" with three corrections, plus three good sentences
from the room.

## If the group is…

- **weaker:** give the menu only three items and round prices (€5, €7, €9).
- **stronger:** add a missing item — "Do you have a vegetarian option?" — and make
  the waiter say no, so they have to choose again.
## Session 03 — Home, Flat & Neighbours

**Card 4 scenario:** a noise complaint at the door, then asking about a flat
**Prop:** a one-line flat advert ("2 rooms, €600, quiet street, no lift")

### Card 1 — Intro + 10 vocabulary units (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| flat | a block of flats | flat |
| kitchen | a cooker | KICH-en |
| bedroom | a bed | BED-room |
| neighbour | two doors side by side | NAY-ber |
| lift | lift doors | lift |
| rent | a price on a door | rent |
| clean | a sponge | kleen |
| dirty | a dirty plate | DER-tee |
| move | boxes and a van | moov |
| keys | a key ring | keez |

**Warm-up:** "Look around this room. Point to something that is also in your home.
What is it?"

### Card 2 — General discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I think… / For me… / It is good / It is bad / I don't know

**Question:** "Is your home quiet or noisy? Do you like your neighbours?"

**Model:** "For me, my flat is quiet. I think my neighbours are good."

### Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B] / [A] is better for me

| This | Or that |
|---|---|
| a small flat in the city centre | a big flat outside the city |
| a lift | stairs |
| living alone | living with family |

Add the reason rung for anyone ready: "I prefer the city centre because I don't
have a car."

### Card 4 — Real-life mini-action (15 min)

**Scenario A — the noise (8 min).** A lives next door to B. It is 11 pm, the music
is loud. A knocks on the door.

**Frames:**

- Excuse me.
- It is very noisy.
- Can you stop the music, please?
- Sorry! OK, no problem.
- Thank you. Good night.

**Scenario B — the flat advert (7 min).** A is the owner, B wants to rent.

**Frames:**

- How much is the rent?
- Is it quiet?
- Is there a lift?
- Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
- Can I see it on Saturday?

**Run it:** demo the noise scene yourself with a strong student (2 min) → pairs do
A (4 min) → swap roles (4 min) → pairs switch to the flat advert (5 min).

### Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

**Chunks:** Next week, I want to… / Tomorrow, I will…

**Prompts:** "What do you want to change in your home? What will you do at home
this weekend?"

**Model:** "Next week I want to clean my kitchen. On Sunday I will buy a new chair."

### Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

**Wrap-up:** "Say one word from today + one thing you want to change at home."

## If the group is…

- **weaker:** do only the noise scene, and let A read the four lines from the card.
- **stronger:** the neighbour is not sorry — "I can play music in my flat" — and A
  has to stay polite and find a solution.
## Session 04 — Shopping, Clothes & Returns

**Card 4 scenario:** return a shirt in the wrong size
**Prop:** a real receipt and a paper shirt with the size written on it (M / L)

### Card 1 — Intro + 10 vocabulary units (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| shirt | a shirt | shert |
| size | S / M / L labels | saiz |
| price | a price tag | prais |
| cheap | a small tag | cheep |
| expensive | a big tag | ik-SPEN-siv |
| try on | a changing-room curtain | TRAI on |
| cash | a banknote | kash |
| card | a bank card | kard |
| receipt | a paper receipt | ri-SEET |
| change | coins | chaynj |

**Warm-up:** "Look at your shoes. Are they cheap or expensive?"

### Card 2 — General discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I like… / I don't like… / It is good / It is bad / Too [big / expensive]

**Questions:** "Do you like shopping? Is shopping online good or bad?"

**Model:** "I don't like shopping in big shops. It is too noisy for me."

### Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I prefer… / I always choose… / [A] is better for me

| This | Or that |
|---|---|
| paying by card | paying cash |
| shopping online | shopping in a shop |
| a cheap shirt | an expensive shirt |

### Card 4 — Real-life mini-action (15 min)

**Scenario:** B wants to return a shirt. It is too small. A is the shop assistant.

**Frames:**

- Hello. Can I help you?
- I want to return this shirt.
- It is too small. Do you have a bigger size?
- Do you have the receipt?
- Here you are.
- Yes, we have L. Do you want to try it on?
- Thank you. How much is it?

**Run it:** demo (2 min) → pairs, customer first (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) →
one pair performs (3 min).

**Second round, if time:** the shirt is the last one and there is no bigger size.
The customer asks for the money back: "Can I have my money back, please?"

### Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

**Chunks:** This weekend, I want to buy… / Next month, I will buy…

**Prompts:** "What do you want to buy this weekend? What will you buy for your home
next month?"

### Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

**Wrap-up:** "Say one word from today + one thing you want to buy."

## If the group is…

- **weaker:** write the price on the shirt (€20) so numbers are read, not invented.
- **stronger:** add a discount — "It is €20, but today it is €14" — and ask them to
  check the change.
## Session 05 — Health, Pharmacy & Sleep

**Card 4 scenario:** at the pharmacy with a headache
**Prop:** two empty medicine boxes with "2 times a day" written on one

### Card 1 — Intro + 10 vocabulary units (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| head | hand on head | hed |
| stomach | hand on stomach | STUM-ak |
| throat | hand on throat | throat |
| cold | a tissue | kold |
| medicine | a pill box | MED-i-sin |
| pharmacy | a green cross | FAR-ma-see |
| appointment | a calendar with a time | a-POINT-ment |
| hurt | a red spot on an arm | hert |
| tired | a yawn | TAI-erd |
| sleep | a pillow | sleep |

**Warm-up:** "How do you feel today — good or tired? Show me."

Keep this card light. Body words are easy and physical, so use pointing and mime
and get through all ten.

### Card 2 — General discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I feel… / It hurts / I need… / It is good / It is bad

**Questions:** "When you are sick, do you go to the doctor or stay home? Do you
sleep eight hours?"

**Model:** "I feel tired this week. I need more sleep."

### Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I prefer… / I always choose… / [A] is better for me

| This | Or that |
|---|---|
| tea | coffee |
| sleep early | walk in the evening |
| pharmacy first | doctor first |

### Card 4 — Real-life mini-action (15 min)

**Scenario:** B is the customer, A is the pharmacist.

**Frames:**

- Good morning. Can I help you?
- I have a headache. / I have a cold.
- What do you recommend?
- Take this. Two times a day.
- Before food or after food?
- After food. / Drink a lot of water.
- How much is it?
- Thank you. Goodbye.

**Run it:** demo (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles, new symptom: stomach
(5 min) → one pair performs (3 min).

### Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

**Chunks:** Tomorrow, I will… / Next week, I want to…

**Prompts:** "What time will you sleep tonight? What will you do for your health
next week?"

**Model:** "Tonight I will sleep at eleven. Next week I want to walk 30 minutes
every day."

### Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

**Wrap-up:** "Say one word from today + one small health goal."

## If the group is…

- **weaker:** give the customer a card with the symptom written on it, so nobody
  has to remember the word under pressure.
- **stronger:** the pharmacy is out of the medicine, and the pharmacist suggests
  an appointment with a doctor instead. Book it: day, time, name.
## Session 06 — Getting Around: Transport & Tickets

**Card 4 scenario:** buy a ticket and find the platform
**Prop:** a small paper timetable with three lines and one delayed train

### Card 1 — Intro + 10 vocabulary units (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| bus | a bus | bus |
| train | a train | trayn |
| ticket | a ticket | TIK-et |
| stop | a bus stop sign | stop |
| platform | a platform number | PLAT-form |
| late | a clock, red | layt |
| early | a clock, green | ER-lee |
| map | a city map | map |
| walk | legs | waak |
| taxi | a taxi | TAK-see |

**Warm-up:** "How do you come to this club? Bus, train, walk, bike?"

### Card 2 — General discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I think… / For me… / It is good / It is bad / Too [late / expensive]

**Questions:** "Is public transport in your city good or bad? Do you like the bus?"

**Model:** "For me, the bus is good, but it is too late in the evening."

### Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

**Chunks:** I prefer… / I always choose… / [A] is better for me

| This | Or that |
|---|---|
| the bus | the train |
| a ticket on your phone | a paper ticket |
| walking 20 minutes | taking a taxi |

### Card 4 — Real-life mini-action (15 min)

**Scenario:** B is at the ticket office. A is the clerk.

**Frames:**

- Hello. One ticket to the city centre, please.
- Single or return?
- Single, please. How much is it?
- €3.50. Here you are.
- Which platform?
- Platform 3. It is late — ten minutes.
- Thank you very much.

**Run it:** demo (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles, new destination and a
return ticket (5 min) → one pair performs with the delayed train (3 min).

### Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

**Chunks:** Next weekend, I want to go to… / I will take the train to…

**Prompts:** "Where do you want to go next weekend? How will you go there?"

**Model:** "Next weekend I want to go to the lake. I will take the train at nine."

### Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

**Wrap-up:** "Say one word from today + one place you want to go next week."

## If the group is…

- **weaker:** use three destinations only, with prices written on the board.
- **stronger:** the train is cancelled and the clerk offers a bus instead. They
  have to ask about time and platform again.
