🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
Short introduction to the research topic and initial engagement questions.
  • Have you read or listened to the summary podcast for today's article?
  • What is your initial reaction to the research topic?
🔵 Round 1: Theoretical Research Analysis & Article Focus▲
Article & Podcast Analysis
Round 1 Structure Rules:
Focuses on the theoretical core of the source article, our written summary script, and the podcast.
Each item MUST include a theoretical analysis question paired with a personal application question (.round-item-main + .round-item-personal).
According to the research article and podcast, what main mechanism explains the reported finding?
★ Personal application: How does this mechanism relate to your own daily experiences or observations?
What key evidence or experiment did the researchers present in the summary to support their hypothesis?
★ Personal application: Were you surprised by this evidence? Why or why not?
How does the author of the article distinguish between causation and correlation in this study?
★ Personal application: Can you think of a situation where you previously mistook correlation for causation?
What potential limitations or critiques of the study design were highlighted in our podcast analysis?
★ Personal application: Do these limitations make you doubt the practical value of the findings?
In what ways does the summary script reframe the scientific jargon into everyday concepts?
★ Personal application: How easily can you explain this scientific concept to a non-expert friend?
What role do environmental factors play in the findings described in the source paper?
★ Personal application: How influential are environmental factors in your own habits or lifestyle choices?
How does this new scientific discovery challenge traditional theories in the field?
★ Personal application: Have you ever had to change your mind after discovering new scientific evidence?
What ethical considerations or societal impacts did the authors address?
★ Personal application: What ethical boundaries would you set if you were regulating this technology/method?
What future research questions did the scientists propose at the conclusion of the paper?
★ Personal application: If you were funded to run the next experiment, what aspect would you investigate?
What is the central take-away message conveyed in the podcast's conclusion?
★ Personal application: What single action or mindset shift will you adopt based on this article?
🟣 Let's Speak Together: Visual & Collaborative Synthesis▲

Collaborative group task: Map out the causes, effects, and real-world implications of the research using shared visual diagrams or scenario cards.

🟢 Round 2: Agree / Disagree Statements & Future Projections▲
Debate & Future Projections
Round 2 Structure Rules:
Consists of agree/disagree statements and speculative future scenarios.
Each statement MUST be paired with a personal stance question (.round-item-main + .round-item-personal).
Statement 1: "Scientific findings should immediately influence government public policy, regardless of economic costs."
★ Personal stance: Do you agree or disagree? How would this policy impact your daily life?
Statement 2: "Within the next decade, consumer technology based on this research will be part of every household."
★ Personal stance: Is this projection realistic or overhyped? Would you purchase such technology?
Statement 3: "Individual behavioral changes are ineffective compared to institutional regulations when addressing this issue."
★ Personal stance: Do you agree or disagree? What personal effort are you willing to make?
Statement 4: "Media reporting on scientific studies creates more confusion than clarity for the general public."
★ Personal stance: Do you agree or disagree? Where do you personally turn for trustworthy science news?
Statement 5: "The benefits of commercializing this research far outweigh the potential ethical risks."
★ Personal stance: Where do you draw the line between innovation and caution?
Statement 6: "By 2050, this scientific breakthrough will fundamentally alter human health and longevity."
★ Personal stance: Would you personally welcome a significantly longer lifespan if offered by science?
Statement 7: "Funding for theoretical research should be redirected toward urgent practical global problems."
★ Personal stance: Do you agree or disagree? Which scientific field deserves the highest priority?
Statement 8: "AI models will soon generate scientific hypotheses faster and more accurately than human researchers."
★ Personal stance: Do you agree or disagree? How does this change the role of human scientists?
Statement 9: "Public skepticism toward scientific consensus is growing and poses a danger to society."
★ Personal stance: Have you observed scientific skepticism in your community or social circle?
Statement 10: "In the future, access to scientific enhancements will widen inequality between social classes."
★ Personal stance: Do you agree or disagree? How can society ensure fair access to breakthroughs?
