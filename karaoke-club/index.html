🎤 Live Karaoke Session Format

Structure your group Karaoke Club event using this 4-step framework:

  1. Step 1 — Pronunciation & Rhythm Warm-Up (10 mins): Use the pronunciation deck above to practice vocal projection, connected speech, and consonant articulation.
  2. Step 2 — Song Selection & Lyric Review (10 mins): Participants select a song title and access lyrics via external licensed streaming platforms.
  3. Step 3 — Turn-Taking Performances (30–45 mins): Perform songs individually or in pairs, focusing on clear enunciation and rhythmic delivery.
  4. Step 4 — Group Debrief & Vocabulary Spotlight (10 mins): Discuss interesting phrases or rhythm challenges experienced during singing.
