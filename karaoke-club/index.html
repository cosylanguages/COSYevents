🎵 Karaoke Club Live Session Framework

Structure your live group event or guided studio session using this step-by-step facilitation framework:

  1. Warm-Up Pronunciation Drills (10 mins): Before putting on music, go through the interactive Pronunciation Prompt Deck above as a group. Each participant takes a turn reading/chanting the original practice phrases out loud, focusing on vowel connections, consonant clarity, and rhythmic cadence.
  2. Song Selection & Level Matching (5 mins): Participants select a song suited to their CEFR level from the curated theme list. Learners review the official lyrics externally via the provided licensed external links (e.g. Genius / Spotify).
  3. Vocal & Rhythm Practice Rounds (20 mins): Participants perform individually or in pairs. Encourage focus on target phonetic elements highlighted during the warm-up (e.g., linked vowels, plosive consonants, pitch contours).
  4. Group Chorus & Peer Feedback (15 mins): Conclude with a collective group chorus song where everyone sings along. Provide constructive, encouraging feedback on pronunciation and rhythm.

💡 Host Tip: Encourage participants to pay attention to syllable stress and connected speech (liaison) while performing. The practice prompts are designed to build confidence in these exact phonetic patterns prior to singing full tracks.
