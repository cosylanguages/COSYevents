🎞️ Cinema Club Session Format

Follow this 3-stage format for hosting or participating in a Cinema Club event:

  1. Stage 1 — Pre-Watch Vocabulary Primer (10 mins): Review the common narrative/cinema vocabulary terms above. Clarify target meanings and pronunciation before viewing.
  2. Stage 2 — Independent / Group Viewing (30–60 mins): Participants watch a selected movie, short film, or scene clip independently beforehand or together in a group screening.
  3. Stage 3 — Post-Watch Prompt Discussion (30 mins): Cycle through the discussion prompt deck. Participants share scene breakdowns, character analyses, and personal perspectives.

📌 Facilitator Note: Prompt cards are genre-agnostic and plot-spoiler-free. Facilitators may choose any film or short video fitting the suggested genre theme.
