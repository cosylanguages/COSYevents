2. What This Website Does & Local Storage

COSYevents is a static website. We set no cookies and use no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

To deliver a seamless user experience, your preference choices are stored strictly inside your browser's local storage on your own device:

  • ce-theme: Stores your light or dark theme selection.
  • ce-lang & ce-langs: Store your chosen practice languages and language switcher preferences.
  • ce-i18n-manifest, ce-i18n-aliases, & UI banners: Cache localized route maps and UI dismissal states.

This data is saved in your own browser, is never transmitted to our servers, and can be cleared by you at any time through your browser settings.
