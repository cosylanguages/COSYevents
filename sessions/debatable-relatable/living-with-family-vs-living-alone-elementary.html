Home & Daily Habits

An Elementary (A2) conversation comparing living with family versus living alone. Practice comparative adjectives, simple household vocabulary, and daily routines.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Alone ≠ together
without other people around.
She lives alone in a small apartment.
Family ≠ stranger
a group of people related to each other.
My family eats dinner together every evening.
Rent ≠ mortgage
money paid regularly for the use of a room or house.
Paying monthly rent is expensive in big cities.
Quiet ≠ noisy
making very little noise.
Living alone gives you a quiet room to study.
Share ≠ hide
to use or enjoy something with others.
Brothers and sisters often share a bedroom.
Clean ≠ dirty
free from dirt or mess.
We clean the kitchen every Saturday morning.
Cook ≠ order
to prepare food by heating it.
Cooking at home is cheaper than buying restaurant meals.
Help ≠ hinder
to make it easier for someone to do something.
Family members help each other with house work.
Free ≠ busy
having time to relax or do what you want.
I have free time on Sunday afternoons.
Space ≠ crowd
an empty area available for use.
Having your own space feels comfortable.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What comes to your mind when you hear about this topic?
  • How does this subject relate to your daily habits?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Comparative Adjectives (Cleaner / More Expensive) (A2)▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Elementary (A2))

Use short adjective + er or more + long adjective to compare two living options (e.g., Living alone is more expensive than living with family).

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: Add '-er' to short adjectives and use 'more' before long adjectives, followed by _____.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. Living with family is _____ than renting alone.
  2. Living alone is _____ than sharing a flat.
  3. My room is _____ when I am alone.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

Compare your current room or apartment with your childhood home.

🟣 Let's Speak Together▲

A comparative reflection on our habits and self-perceptions.

🟢 Round 2 : Debates & Future Outlook▲
Living alone helps a young person become independent faster. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you like to live alone for a year?
Sharing a home with family is much better than renting an apartment with strangers. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Have you ever lived with flatmates?
Monthly rent prices in big cities are becoming far too high for young workers. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Is finding affordable housing difficult near you?
Living in a quiet home environment improves daily mental health. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you need silence when working or studying?
Family members should always share household chores equally. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How were household tasks divided in your childhood home?
Learning to clean and manage a home is a necessary life skill for everyone. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Did your parents teach you household management skills?
Learning how to cook healthy meals saves money and improves health. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Should cooking classes be mandatory in schools?
Asking family for financial help is better than taking a bank loan. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Are you comfortable borrowing money from relatives?
Having your own free time without interruption is essential for happiness. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How many hours of alone time do you need each week?
Every adult needs personal space that nobody else can enter without asking. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you have a private corner or study room at home?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
Living alone is more cheap. → Living alone is cheaper. (Short adjective comparative uses '-er': cheaper.)
I live with my family since two years. → I have lived with my family for two years. (Use present perfect + 'for' to show duration up to now.)
