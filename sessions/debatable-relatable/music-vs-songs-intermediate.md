We contrast purely instrumental compositions-which rely on instruments alone to convey meaning-with songs that incorporate explicit lyrics and human vocals. We will debate whether words restrict our creative imagination and explore how different soundscapes alter our mental focus.</p>

    definition: music enacted without human vocals or lyrics.

round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : Personal Exploration'
  badge: ''
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: Why do some people prefer <strong>instrumental</strong> compositions when they feel emotionally overwhelmed?
      personal: ★ What instrument do you think has the most soothing, therapeutic sound?
    - main: Do explicit <strong>lyrics</strong> restrict our personal interpretation of a piece of music?
      personal: ★ Can you share a song whose lyrics have had a major impact on your perspective?
    - main: Does a skilled <strong>vocalist</strong> have more power to move an audience than a brilliant pianist?
      personal: ★ Who is your absolute favorite vocalist of all time, and why?
    - main: What neural processes make a beautiful <strong>melody</strong> stay stuck in our memories for days?
      personal: ★ Have you ever had a song melody trigger a sudden, vivid childhood memory?
    - main: Why are certain natural or industrial sounds highly <strong>evocative</strong> of quiet loneliness?
      personal: ★ What environmental sound (e.g. rain, train tracks) do you find most comforting?
    - main: How does the role of a professional <strong>lyricist</strong> differ from that of a traditional poet?
      personal: ★ Do you ever write down your thoughts or poems when you are stressed?
    - main: Should modern workplaces play <strong>ambient</strong> music to reduce employee burnout?
      personal: ★ Do you listen to ambient sounds or white noise when sleeping or working?
    - main: What makes a classical <strong>composition</strong> survive stably across hundreds of years?
      personal: ★ Do you ever attend live classical concerts or opera performances?
    - main: How does the acoustic <strong>resonance</strong> of a physical hall alter our enjoyment of a show?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer listening to music on high-quality speakers or small headphones?
    - main: Why is our <strong>interpretation</strong> of abstract jazz compositions so subjective and diverse?
      personal: ★ Has a friend ever completely misunderstood your emotional mood or intentions?

      personal: ★ Do you care about what a songwriter meant, or do you prefer your own interpretation?
mistakes:
  - wrong: We discussed <u>about the lyrics</u>.
    right: We discussed <strong>the lyrics / had a discussion about</strong> the lyrics.
    note: '(Underlined error: ''discuss'' is a transitive verb and takes a direct object without ''about'')'
  - wrong: I am <u>agree with</u> your interpretation.
    right: I <strong>agree with</strong> your interpretation.
    note: '(Underlined error: ''agree'' is a verb, do not use it with auxiliary ''am'')'
