mistakes:
  - wrong: It depends of the student

    note: '(Collocation: depend + on)'
  - wrong: The AI can makes mistakes
    right: AI can make mistakes
    note: (Modal verbs + bare infinitive)
  - wrong: I suggest the teacher <u>to use</u> more AI
    right: I suggest that the teacher <strong>should use / use</strong> more AI
    note: (The verb 'suggest' cannot be followed by an object pronoun + to-infinitive; it requires a that-clause or gerund)
  - wrong: Our school curriculum is very <u>ancient</u>
    right: Our school curriculum is very <strong>outdated / archaic</strong>
    note: (Use 'outdated' or 'archaic' to describe old methods/curricula, as 'ancient' refers to historical periods like Ancient Rome)
