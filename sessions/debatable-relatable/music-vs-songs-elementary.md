vocabulary:
  - word: Instrument
    definition: a tool used to make music sounds (like a piano or guitar).
    example: Playing a physical instrument is a highly rewarding hobby.
warm_up:
  instruction: ''
  questions:
    - What instrument do you like the sound of most (piano, violin, or guitar)?
    - Do you sing when you are alone in the shower or car?
round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : Personal Opinions'
  badge: ''
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: Do you think a physical <strong>instrument</strong> can express sadness better than a human voice?
      personal: ★ What instrument did you try to play when you were a child?
    - main: Why are the <strong>lyrics</strong> of old songs often more romantic and poetic than modern ones?
      personal: ★ Do you try to memorize the lyrics of your favorite songs?
    - main: Does a singer need a perfect <strong>voice</strong> to make a great emotional recording?
      personal: ★ Who is a singer with a unique voice that you enjoy listening to?
    - main: Why is a simple <strong>melody</strong> easier to remember than a complex classical concert?
      personal: ★ What melody is stuck in your head today?
    - main: Does classical piano music help you <strong>relax</strong> better than modern pop songs?
      personal: ★ How do you usually relax after a very noisy or busy day?
    - main: What makes a pop <strong>song</strong> popular on the radio for many years?
      personal: ★ What pop song makes you smile or want to dance immediately?
    - main: Should cafes play loud music, or is quiet <strong>background</strong> music better?
      personal: ★ Do you study better with background music or in absolute quiet?
    - main: Why do we often remember the <strong>singer</strong> of a band but forget the guitar players?
      personal: ★ Have you ever met a professional singer or musician in real life?
    - main: Why does a <strong>beautiful</strong> violin solo make some people feel like crying?
      personal: ★ What is the most beautiful place you have ever visited during a holiday?
    - main: Why do people's <strong>favorite</strong> music styles change as they grow older?
      personal: ★ What was your favorite music genre when you were fifteen?
lets_speak_together:

Work with your partner to choose the perfect tracks. Will you include songs with English lyrics or purely instrumental piano music? Present your perfect study playlist!</p>
round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : Agree or Disagree: The Future'
  badge: ''
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: 'In the future, physical <strong>instruments</strong> will disappear because computers will make all sounds. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Do you prefer the sound of a real acoustic piano to a digital keyboard?
    - main: 'Computers will write better song <strong>lyrics</strong> than human poets in the coming years. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Would you buy a book of poetry written entirely by an artificial intelligence?
    - main: 'An AI <strong>voice</strong> will soon sound more emotional than any human singer. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Do you think an AI singer can have real soul or feeling?
    - main: 'A song with no repeating <strong>melody</strong> is not real music. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Do you enjoy listening to chaotic or unusual jazz melodies?
    - main: 'Future cities will be so noisy that humans will be unable to <strong>relax</strong> without headphones. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Do you wear headphones when you walk on busy city streets?
    - main: 'A <strong>song</strong> with words is always more interesting than a piece of instrumental music. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Do you listen to classical music when you are doing chores at home?
    - main: 'In the future, smart offices will automatically play personalized <strong>background</strong> music to make us work faster. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Would you like your boss to choose your study music?
    - main: 'A virtual, holographic <strong>singer</strong> will host the most popular concerts of the future. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Would you pay for a ticket to see a holographic concert of a dead singer?
    - main: 'A <strong>beautiful</strong> song is the fastest way to learn the culture of another country. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Has listening to songs helped you learn foreign language pronunciation?
    - main: 'We should only share our <strong>favorite</strong> songs with our closest friends. : Do you agree or disagree?'
      personal: ★ Do you feel embarrassed when someone looks at your music playlist?
mistakes:
  - wrong: I listen <u>the music</u> every day.
    right: I listen <strong>to music</strong> every day.
    note: '(Underlined error: ''listen'' is followed by the preposition ''to'', and do not use ''the'' for general concepts)'
  - wrong: The song is <u>very beautifully</u>.
    right: The song is <strong>very beautiful</strong>.
    note: '(Underlined error: use the adjective ''beautiful'' after the linking verb ''is'' rather than the adverb ''beautifully'')'
