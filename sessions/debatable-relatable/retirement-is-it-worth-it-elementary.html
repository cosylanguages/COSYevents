Duration

60 minutes

Languages

🇬🇧 English

Level

Elementary (A2)

Theme

Retirement & Future Planning

Explore the question of retirement from an Elementary (A2) perspective. Discuss saving money, pensions, staying active, and life after work.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Retirement ≠ career
the period of life after stopping official work.
Many people plan their retirement carefully.
Pension ≠ salary
a regular payment made by the state or company after stopping work.
He receives his pension every month.
Save ≠ spend
to keep money for future use.
It is wise to save money when you are young.
Workplace ≠ home
the building or place where people do their jobs.
She misses her friends at the workplace.
Freedom ≠ obligation
the state of being able to act without control by others.
Retirement offers freedom to travel.
Routine ≠ chaos
regular daily habits.
A healthy daily routine keeps seniors active.
Boredom ≠ excitement
the feeling of having nothing interesting to do.
He joined a gardening club to avoid boredom.
Budget ≠ waste
a plan of how to spend money.
A monthly budget helps manage expenses.
Schedule ≠ surprise
a plan that gives expected times for activities.
I keep a light schedule on weekends.
Active ≠ passive
doing things and moving regularly.
Staying active is good for your health.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What comes to your mind when you hear about this topic?
  • How does this subject relate to your daily habits?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Going to for Future Plans (A2)▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Elementary (A2))

Use be going to + verb to talk about future intentions and retirement plans (e.g., I am going to travel more).

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: Form future intention with subject + be + _____ + base verb.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. He _____ going to retire next summer.
  2. They _____ going to learn new languages after work.
  3. I _____ going to save more money every month.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

Tell your partner one thing you are going to do when you have free time.

★ Do you look forward to retirement?

★ Is the pension system in your country reliable?

★ Will you miss working when you retire?

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I am go to retire. → I am going to retire. (The future intention structure requires 'going to' + base verb.)
He don't work now. → He doesn't work now. (3rd person singular negative present simple uses 'doesn't'.)
