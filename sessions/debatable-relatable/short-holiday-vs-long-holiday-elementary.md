mistakes:
  - wrong: I am <u>prefer</u> long holidays.
    right: I prefer long holidays.
    note: '(Verb conjugation check: do not use ''am'' before simple active verbs.)'
  - wrong: It was a very <u>good</u> trip.
    right: It was a wonderful / fantastic trip.
    note: '(Style: use more expressive adjectives like ''wonderful'' or ''fantastic''.)'
  - wrong: I have been <u>in</u> France last year.
    right: I went to France last year.
    note: (Past simple is required with specific past time markers like 'last year'.)
