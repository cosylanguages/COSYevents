Money vs Free Time

🔥 Debate Duel

Core Dilemma High Salary vs. Ample Free Time
Side A (Hot Take) Money pays for your life and comfort.
Side B (Hot Take) Free time brings genuine happiness.
Thematic Symbol 💵 Wallet & Gold Coins vs. ⏳ Hourglass & Sleep

This session explores the eternal choice between a high salary and abundant free time. We will debate whether wealth or relaxation brings more happiness to our daily life. We will also learn simple comparative structures in English to express our personal choices and preferences.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Salary
a fixed regular payment made by an employer to an employee.
She receives a very good monthly salary from her bank job.
Free time ≠ busy time
time when you are not working and can relax ≠ time when you have many duties.
During my free time I love reading books, but my busy time at work is very exhausting.
To earn
to receive money in return for work or services.
He works on Saturdays to earn some extra money.
To spend
to give money to pay for goods or services.
I try not to spend too much money on expensive coffee.
Rich ≠ poor
having a lot of money ≠ having very little money.
The rich businessman bought a yacht, while the poor student walked to school.
Relaxed ≠ stressed
feeling calm and comfortable ≠ feeling worried, anxious, and tired.
A long walk in the park makes me feel relaxed, but my desk job makes me stressed.
Hobby
an activity done regularly in one's leisure time for pleasure.
My favorite weekend hobby is playing tennis with my friends.
Important
of great significance or value; necessary.
Good health is much more important than a big house.
Happiness
the state of feeling happy, joyful, and satisfied.
Spending time with my family brings me genuine happiness.
To afford
to have enough money to pay for something.
I cannot afford to buy a new smartphone this month.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Are you currently very busy at work or do you have a lot of free time?
  • What is more expensive in your city: renting an apartment or dining at restaurants?
🎯 Linguistic Focus (Interactive Grammar)▲

Use comparatives (richer, happier, more important) to compare wealth and time.

📦 Word Bank (Tap to select)
more more relaxed -er happier richer more important easier
Task 1A: Rule Formulation

Complete the rule: For short adjectives ending in 'y', we change the ending to make it _____. For long adjectives like 'important', we use _____ + adjective.

Task 1B: Structured Practice
  1. People with a lot of free time are often _____ than busy workers.
  2. For me, having a relaxing hobby is _____ than a high salary.
  3. Earning a big salary makes you feel much _____ but also busier.
  4. A quiet weekend makes me feel _____ than a shopping trip.
  5. It is _____ to spend money than to save it.
Task 2: Task-Specific Speaking Prompt

Compare having a high salary to having abundant free time. Which is better?

🔵 Round 1 : Advantages (A/B Ideas)▲
Debate
A: A high salary pays for a comfortable apartment and delicious food.
B: Having abundant free time allows you to sleep well and stay healthy.
★ Do you think a high salary makes life much easier?
A: Having a lot of free time keeps you away from toxic office stress.
B: Being busy at work makes you feel active and productive.
★ How do you usually spend your precious free time?
A: Working hard to earn money builds a strong, disciplined character.
B: Having free hours lets you learn new skills at your own pace.
★ Do you have to work on weekends to earn extra money?
A: Having money to spend on luxury travel is very exciting.
B: Free time lets you enjoy simple pleasures like walking in nature.
★ Do you prefer to spend your money or save it for the future?
A: A rich lifestyle offers absolute safety and high-quality healthcare.
B: A simple, quiet life prevents complicated social anxieties.
★ Do you wish to be very rich, or do you prefer a simple life?
A: Feeling relaxed during a long morning walk is beautiful.
B: A busy schedule keeps your mind sharp and highly focused.
★ Do you feel completely relaxed when you are at home alone?
A: Having a creative hobby like painting brings great joy.
B: Earning a bonus at work is extremely satisfying.
★ What is your favorite creative hobby today?
A: A high salary is important to support your children.
B: Free hours are important to play and talk with your kids.
★ Is a big bank account the most important thing for you?
A: Real happiness comes from feeling calm and relaxed.
B: Buying nice gifts for friends brings wonderful shared joy.
★ What brings you more happiness: buying a product or sleeping?
A: Having money to afford good books and classes is great.
B: Free hours are necessary to actually read the books you buy.
★ What is one expensive thing you want to afford soon?

🟢 Round 2 : Disadvantages (A/B Ideas)▲
Debate
A: A low salary can create intense survival anxiety.
B: Having too much free time can make you feel lazy.
★ Have you ever had a job with a very low salary?
A: A lack of free time leads to rapid burnout and stress.
B: Infinite free hours can make your life feel empty.
★ Does a completely empty weekend ever make you feel bored?
A: Having no way to earn money makes you dependent on others.
B: Earning money often requires working in toxic offices.
★ Have you ever had a very stressful boss?
A: Having no money to spend stops you from enjoying the city.
B: Spending too much money makes you regret your actions.
★ Do you regret some of your past shopping purchases?
A: A poor person struggles to afford basic necessities.
B: A rich lifestyle can make you highly materialistic.
★ Do you worry about becoming too materialistic?
A: A highly stressed manager is very difficult to work with.
B: A completely relaxed schedule can make you lose focus.
★ What is the most stressed moment of your week?
A: An expensive hobby can ruin your monthly savings.
B: Having no hobby makes your weekends feel very dry.
★ Is tennis or skiing considered an expensive hobby in your town?
A: Sacrificing family time for an important meeting is painful.
B: Having no important goals can make you feel lost.
★ What is the most important goal of your life right now?
A: True happiness is impossible when you are constantly tired.
B: Possessions bring only a temporary wave of happiness.
★ What is the simplest thing that brings you genuine happiness?
A: If you cannot afford rent, you will feel extreme anxiety.
B: Affording luxury items can alienate you from simple friends.
★ Do you think luxury clothes make people look arrogant?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
Free time is more important than money→Free time is more important than money(For long adjectives like 'important', use 'more important than', not 'importanter')
He work very hard to earn money→He works very hard to earn money(Third-person singular 's' is required for verbs in the present simple)
I cannot afford to buy a car→I cannot afford to buy a car(The verb 'afford' is followed by a to-infinitive)
