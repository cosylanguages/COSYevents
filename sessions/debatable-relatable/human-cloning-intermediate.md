      personal: ★ How important is genetic relation compared to emotional bonding in a family, in your view?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> We can use therapeutic cloning to <strong>duplicate</strong> healthy cells and replace damaged organs. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Organ donor registries provide a morally clear and altruistic way to save lives.
      personal: ★ If science offered you a cloned organ to extend your lifespan, would you accept it?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> In <strong>bioethics</strong>, some argue that cloned tissue could save millions of lives from degenerative diseases. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Traditional research focuses on non-destructive cell methods, respecting biological boundaries.
      personal: ★ Do you think scientific research should have absolute freedom if its goal is to save lives?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Cloning endangered animal species is a highly <strong>controversial</strong> but necessary path to protect our planetary biodiversity. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Protecting natural habitats is a more sustainable way to conserve ecosystems than cloning animals in labs.
      personal: ★ Do you believe cloning animals is a <strong>controversial</strong> issue in your country?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> <strong>Genetic</strong> engineering and cloning allow us to study complex human diseases in controlled lab environments. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Studying diverse human populations provides real-world epidemiological insights.
      personal: ★ Are you generally optimistic or cautious about genetic discoveries reported in the news?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Cloning technology is a natural, exciting step in the <strong>progress</strong> of biological sciences. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Establishing strict legal <strong>limits</strong> on cloning protects society from unpredictable dystopian outcomes.
      personal: ★ Do you think we should put a strict legal <strong>limit</strong> on human cloning?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Modern <strong>biotechnology</strong> can eliminate inherited genetic conditions before birth through cloning lines. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Accepting genetic diversity, including natural differences and vulnerabilities, builds a more compassionate society.
      personal: ★ Would you want to know your own genetic predispositions to diseases if you could?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Developing cloning skills elevates a nation's biotechnology sector and attracts global scientific talent. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Restricting biotechnology spending ensures that healthcare budgets are focused on basic, accessible medical needs.
      personal: ★ Do you think there are some areas of nature that humans should simply never try to control?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Analyzing a cloned <strong>embryo</strong> helps us understand early developmental biology and cell growth. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Protecting human embryos from commercial experimentation maintains a clear ethical standard.

      personal: ★ How has modern medicine already improved or changed the daily life of someone you know?

      personal: ★ Have you ever felt pressure to live up to the expectations of your parents or siblings?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> From the perspective of <strong>bioethics</strong>, treating human lives as experimental objects is morally unacceptable. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Refusing to experiment on embryos holds back medical progress that could cure living, breathing patients.
      personal: ★ How do you define the moral value of a human life in your personal philosophy?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Unexpected <strong>genetic</strong> mutations in cloned embryos can lead to severe health defects and suffering. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Natural reproduction also carries a high risk of spontaneous genetic mutations and defects.
      personal: ★ How do you handle situations in life where a decision leads to unexpected, negative consequences?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Human cloning is highly <strong>controversial</strong> and could cause a deep division in society. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Societies have always split over major technological steps, but they eventually adapt.
      personal: ★ How do you usually handle deep disagreements with friends or family members on sensitive topics?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Cloning research could divert funds from urgent, standard medical <strong>progress</strong>. <br/><strong>B:</strong> It could speed up clinical trials and unlock new discoveries.
      personal: ★ Do you think cloning represents real medical <strong>progress</strong>?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Large corporations in <strong>biotechnology</strong> could patent human genetic lines for profit. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Patent protections are necessary to incentivize private companies to invest billions in medical research.
      personal: ★ Do you think it is fair for private companies to own patents on natural resources or life forms?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> If we do not set a strict moral <strong>limit</strong>, humanity might face dystopian scenario. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Overly defensive legal boundaries block positive innovations out of irrational fear of sci-fi scenarios.
      personal: ★ What is your favorite dystopian movie or book, and why does it feel scary or relatable?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Using a human <strong>embryo</strong> solely as a resource for organs is ethically wrong. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Restricting the use of early cells devalues the lives of actual patients suffering from terminal organ failure.
      personal: ★ In what ways can we make sure that human dignity is always respected in medical progress?
