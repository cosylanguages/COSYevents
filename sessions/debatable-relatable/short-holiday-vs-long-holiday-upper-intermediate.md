mistakes:
  - wrong: Taking short trips is more <u>beneficial for</u> me.
    right: Taking short trips is more <strong>beneficial to</strong> me / of greater benefit to me.
    note: '(Preposition collocation: ''beneficial'' is typically paired with ''to'' rather than ''for'' when describing a personal impact.)'
  - wrong: We discussed <u>about the itinerary</u>.
    right: We discussed the itinerary.
    note: '(Transitive verb constraint: ''discuss'' takes a direct object with no preposition.)'
  - wrong: I suggested <u>to travel</u> by train.
    right: I suggested traveling by train.
    note: '(Verb pattern: ''suggest'' is followed by a gerund, not an infinitive.)'
