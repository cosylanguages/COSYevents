🔥 Debate Duel: Mind vs. Machine

Is chatting with an AI chatbot better than talking to a human? Explore arguments for instant availability and helpful tips versus real human warmth and true friendship.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Chatbot ≠ Human speaker
a computer program designed to simulate conversation with human users.
The chatbot answered my questions instantly.
Artificial ≠ Natural
made by human skill; not natural.
AI uses artificial intelligence to generate text.
Human ≠ Machine
a person rather than an animal or machine.
Only a real human can share true feelings.
Connection ≠ Isolation
a feeling of understanding and friendship between people.
We formed a strong emotional connection.
Advice ≠ Misdirection
guidance or recommendations offered with regard to prudent future action.
My friend gave me great advice for my project.
Privacy ≠ Publicity
the state of being free from public attention or intrusion.
I value my privacy when talking about personal topics.
Response ≠ Silence
an answer or reply in words or action.
The AI gave a fast response to my question.
Memory ≠ Forgetfulness
something remembered from the past; data storage capability.
The computer memory holds our chat history.
Available ≠ Unavailable
ready for use or at hand.
AI chatbots are available 24 hours a day.
Real ≠ Fake
actually existing as a thing or occurring in fact; genuine.
Real friends listen with true care.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up (10 mins)▲

Welcome! Share your initial thoughts on this topic. What personal experiences or observations come to mind right away?

⚡ Grammar Practice : Comparative Structures (better than, faster than) (A2)▲
Use comparative forms to debate AI vs Human interactions: "AI is faster than humans." / "Human friends are more empathetic than chatbots."
🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)▲
Chatting with an AI chatbot gives instant responses 24/7 without waiting.
★ How often do you use AI chatbots for quick information?
An artificial tutor is ideal for practicing language basics without feeling nervous.
★ Do you prefer practicing speaking with an AI or a real partner?
Talking to AI as a digital penpal is convenient and safe.
★ Have you ever had a penpal or online friend?
AI offers quick, logical advice without making judgments.
★ Who do you turn to first when you need advice?
AI memory keeps track of your conversation details perfectly.
★ What is a special memory you shared with a close friend?
🟢 Round 2: Deep Speculation & Future Horizon (Units 6–10)▲
AI is always available whenever you feel lonely at night.
★ What do you do when you feel lonely late at night?
Data privacy is easier to manage with anonymous software.
★ Are you careful about what personal details you type online?
AI can serve as an affordable digital companion for elderly people.
★ How can communities better support lonely individuals?
AI language apps make learning interactive and fun.
★ What helps you feel confident when speaking English?
Synthetic connections will soon become normal in everyday life.
★ What qualities make a great friend in your opinion?
