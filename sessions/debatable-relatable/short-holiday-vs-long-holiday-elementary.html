Short Holiday vs Long Holiday

18 July 2026

← Back to Club

Duration

60 minutes

Languages

🇬🇧 English

Level

Elementary (A2)

Theme

Travel, Holidays, and Rest

🔥 Debate Duel

Core Dilemma Many short trips vs. one long vacation
Side A (Hot Take) Short holidays are cheap and easy to plan.
Side B (Hot Take) Long holidays let you completely relax.
Thematic Symbol 📅 Many short weekends vs. 🗓️ One long trip

In this session, we discuss the best way to spend our vacation time. Some people prefer taking many short holidays throughout the year, while others love having one long, relaxing trip. We will talk about travel plans, packing bags, and our favorite destinations.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Holiday
a day of festivity or recreation when no work is done.
I love to go to the beach during my summer holiday.
Travel
make a journey, typically of some length.
Many people like to travel to different countries by plane.
Short ≠ Long
measuring a small distance or duration ≠ measuring a great distance or duration.
A short weekend trip is very fun, but a long summer trip is more relaxing.
Vacation
an extended period of recreation, especially one spent away from home or in traveling.
We planned a winter vacation to go skiing in the mountains.
Relax
make or become less tense or anxious.
A warm bath helps me relax after a long day at school.
Destination
the place to which someone or something is going or being sent.
Paris is a very popular tourist destination for people from all over the world.
Plan
a detailed proposal for doing or achieving something.
Do you have a clear plan for your next weekend trip?
Pack
fill a suitcase or bag with clothes and other items needed for travel.
I need to pack my swimsuit and sunglasses for the beach trip.
Active ≠ Passive
engaging or ready to engage in physically energetic pursuits ≠ accepting or allowing what happens or what others do, without active response or resistance.
I prefer an active holiday with hiking and swimming rather than a passive one spent sitting in a hotel.
Choose
pick out or select someone or something as being the best in a situation.
It is always difficult to choose between a mountain holiday and a seaside holiday.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Do you prefer the sea or the mountains?
  • What do you pack first in your bag?
🎯 Linguistic Focus (Interactive Grammar)▲

Use would rather, prefer ... to, or had better to discuss travel.

Task 1A: Rule Formulation

Complete the rule: We use 'would rather' followed by the _____ form of the verb. We use 'prefer' followed by a gerund or noun, and connect choices with _____.

Task 1B: Structured Practice
  1. I would rather _____ close to home than go on long-distance flights.
  2. He prefers packing a suitcase _____ carrying a backpack.
  3. You had better _____ your summer trip early to save money.
  4. She would rather _____ on the beach than do sports.
  5. They prefer short weekend trips _____ long vacations.
Task 2: Task-Specific Speaking Prompt

Would you rather travel once a year or take short weekend trips?

🔵 Round 1 : Advantages (A/B Ideas)▲
Debate
A: A short holiday is great because you do not leave work.
B: A long holiday lets you forget about work completely.
★ How often do you take a short holiday to rest?
A: It is exciting to travel to a nearby town for a weekend.
B: It is better to travel to a far country for a month.
★ Do you like to travel by train or by plane?
A: A short trip is easy to organize and very cheap.
B: A long trip gives you more time to see everything.
★ Do you prefer a short weekend trip or a long summer trip?
A: A short vacation every month keeps you very happy.
B: A long summer vacation is the best way to rest.
★ What is your favorite season to take a vacation?
A: You can relax very well during a quick three-day weekend.
B: You need two weeks to truly relax and feel happy.
★ How do you usually relax after a busy day?
A: Visiting a quiet destination nearby is very peaceful.
B: An exotic, far-away destination offers a much bigger adventure.
★ What is the most beautiful destination you have ever visited?
A: It is simple to plan a quick weekend trip.
B: You must plan a long trip very carefully.
★ Do you like to plan every detail of your trip?
A: You only need to pack a small bag for a weekend.
B: You have to pack a big suitcase with clothes.
★ Is it hard for you to pack your bags?
A: A short trip keeps you active because you move fast.
B: A long trip lets you stay lazy on the beach.
★ Do you prefer an active holiday or a lazy one?
A: It is fun to choose a different town every month.
B: It is better to choose one place for a month.
★ How do you choose your travel destinations?

🟢 Round 2 : Disadvantages (A/B Ideas)▲
Debate
A: A short holiday is too fast, and you cannot rest.
B: A long holiday is very expensive and hard to pay.
★ Have you ever felt tired after a short holiday?
A: Spontaneous travel can be very stressful for new tourists.
B: Long-distance travel causes bad tiredness and jet lag.
★ Does long-distance travel make you feel tired?
A: A short trip is too fast to learn any culture.
B: A long trip can make you feel very homesick.
★ Do you think a short trip is too fast?
A: A short vacation feels like a waste of packing time.
B: An excessively long vacation can make you feel bored.
★ Have you ever felt bored during a long vacation?
A: It is impossible to relax when checking your watch.
B: You cannot relax if you worry about your home.
★ Is it easy for you to relax?
A: A nearby destination can feel too similar to home.
B: A far destination is hard if you do not speak.
★ Would you visit a destination if it had no internet?
A: Traveling without a plan can lead to bad hotels.
B: A strict plan ruins the fun of surprising ideas.
★ Have you ever traveled without any plan?
A: If you do not pack well, you lose your clothes.
B: Lugging heavy bags that you had to pack is bad.
★ Do you usually pack too many things?
A: Being too active leaves you more tired than before.
B: A long, non-active trip makes you lose physical health.
★ Do you feel tired when your holiday is active?
A: It is hard to choose a place with limited time.
B: It is risky to choose one place if you hate it.
★ Is it hard for you to choose travel options?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I am prefer long holidays. → I prefer long holidays. (Verb conjugation check: do not use 'am' before simple active verbs.)
It was a very good trip. → It was a wonderful / fantastic trip. (Style: use more expressive adjectives like 'wonderful' or 'fantastic'.)
I have been in France last year. → I went to France last year. (Past simple is required with specific past time markers like 'last year'.)
I am looking forward to go on holiday. → I am looking forward to going on holiday. ('Look forward to' is followed by a gerund/noun, not an infinitive.)
