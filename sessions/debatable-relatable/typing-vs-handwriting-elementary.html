Topic

Typing vs Handwriting

This session is about writing with a pen and typing on a keyboard. We will talk about which one is better and faster for our daily life. We will also learn simple words to describe our school and office habits.

🔥 Debate Duel

Core Dilemma Paper Writing vs. Digital Typing
Side A (Hot Take) Handwriting helps you learn.
Side B (Hot Take) Typing is very fast and easy.
Thematic Symbol ✍️ Pen & Notebook vs. 💻 Laptop Keyboard

📖 Session Vocabulary

Handwriting
writing with a pen or pencil in your hand.
His handwriting is very neat and clean.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲

  • What color of ink do you like?

Use comparatives (faster, slower) to compare writing.

🔵 Round 1 : Advantages (A/B Ideas)▲
Debate
A: Good handwriting is a beautiful personal skill.
B: Typing is a fast way to write.
★ Do you have neat handwriting?
A: Learning typing helps you in school.
B: Writing by hand helps your memory.
★ Is your typing speed fast?
A: A computer keyboard is very easy to use.
B: Writing with a pen feels creative.
★ What computer keyboard do you use?
A: A simple pen never runs out of battery.
B: A computer lets you edit easily.
★ Do you write with a blue pen?
A: Writing on paper keeps you away from screens.
B: Digital files are easier to save.
★ Do you buy paper notebooks?
A: Typing is extremely fast and saves time.
B: Writing slowly helps your brain think.
★ Is it more important to type fast?
A: A slow writing process helps you learn English.
B: Slow work can make you tired.
★ Do you like a slow life?
A: Keeping a notebook is great for your ideas.
B: Digital files are safer.
★ Do you have a paper notebook?
A: A big office chair is comfortable.
B: Writing in bed with a book is cozy.
★ Is your study chair comfortable?
A: Making a mistake on paper is easy to erase.
B: You can delete any mistake on computers.
★ Are you afraid of making a mistake?

🟢 Round 2 : Disadvantages (A/B Ideas)▲
Debate
A: Bad handwriting is very hard to read.
B: Typing can feel cold and boring.
★ Have you ever struggled with bad handwriting?
A: Long typing can make your fingers tired.
B: Writing by hand is too slow.
★ Does typing on phones make you tired?
A: A noisy keyboard distracts other people.
B: Paper notes have no keys to help.
★ Does a noisy keyboard annoy you?
A: A leaking pen leaves stains on clothes.
B: Without a computer, you cannot type.
★ Has a pen ever leaked?
A: Physical paper is easy to lose.
B: Screens make your eyes very tired.
★ Have you ever lost important paper?
A: Writing too fast makes many typos.
B: Slow work is bad for deadlines.
★ Do you make errors when typing fast?
A: A slow writer cannot take fast notes.
B: Fast typing stops you from thinking.
★ Is a slow texter annoying?
A: Carrying a paper notebook is heavy.
B: Digital files can be hacked.
★ Do you worry about your notebook?
A: Working at a table can be uncomfortable.
B: Computers are uncomfortable for your eyes.
★ What is the most uncomfortable place to study?
A: Erasing a mistake looks messy on paper.
B: Autocorrect can make a mistake.
★ Share a funny mistake you made.
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I write more fast with laptop → I write faster with a laptop (Comparative form of 'fast' is 'faster')
I have many homeworks → I have a lot of homework (Homework is uncountable)
