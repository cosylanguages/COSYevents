Instrumental Music vs Songs with Lyrics

10 March 2027

Home / Events / Debatable & Relatable / Music vs Songs (Elementary)
← Back to Club

Duration

60 minutes

Languages

🇬🇧 English

Level

Elementary (A2)

Topic

Music Preferences

This session is an exciting debate about how we listen to music. We compare instrumental music (without words) to popular songs with singing and lyrics. We will talk about which one helps us study better, which one makes us feel happier, and share our favorite musicians.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Instrument
a tool used to make music sounds (like a piano or guitar).
Playing a physical instrument is a highly rewarding hobby.
Lyrics
the words of a song.
I love reading the lyrics of a song to understand the meaning.
Voice
the sound made by a person singing.
The opera singer had a very strong and beautiful voice.
Melody
the main tune or musical line of a song.
The piano melody was simple but exceptionally peaceful and relaxing.
Relax
to rest, calm down, and feel quiet.
Listening to soft ambient music helps me relax after a busy workday.
Song
a piece of music with words that a person sings.
My favorite song always makes me want to dance.
Background
quiet music played while you do other activities.
Soft background music is perfect when you study for an exam.
Singer
a person who sings songs.
The lead singer of the band talked to the fans after the concert.
Beautiful
very pretty or exceptionally nice to hear.
The classical violin concert was incredibly beautiful.
Favorite
liked more than all other similar things.
Jazz is my absolute favorite music genre to listen to at home.

🎙| Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What instrument do you like the sound of most (piano, violin, or guitar)?
  • Do you sing when you are alone in the shower or car?
🔵 Round 1 : Personal Opinions▲
Do you think a physical instrument can express sadness better than a human voice?
★ What instrument did you try to play when you were a child?
Why are the lyrics of old songs often more romantic and poetic than modern ones?
★ Do you try to memorize the lyrics of your favorite songs?
Does a singer need a perfect voice to make a great emotional recording?
★ Who is a singer with a unique voice that you enjoy listening to?
Why is a simple melody easier to remember than a complex classical concert?
★ What melody is stuck in your head today?
Does classical piano music help you relax better than modern pop songs?
★ How do you usually relax after a very noisy or busy day?
What makes a pop song popular on the radio for many years?
★ What pop song makes you smile or want to dance immediately?
Should cafes play loud music, or is quiet background music better?
★ Do you study better with background music or in absolute quiet?
Why do we often remember the singer of a band but forget the guitar players?
★ Have you ever met a professional singer or musician in real life?
Why does a beautiful violin solo make some people feel like crying?
★ What is the most beautiful place you have ever visited during a holiday?
Why do people's favorite music styles change as they grow older?
★ What was your favorite music genre when you were fifteen?
🟣 Let's Speak Together : The Study Playlist▲

The Playlist Task: Imagine you are designing a 5-song study playlist for an intensive language school. Work with your partner to choose the perfect tracks. Will you include songs with English lyrics or purely instrumental piano music? Present your perfect study playlist!

🟢 Round 2 : Agree or Disagree: The Future▲
In the future, physical instruments will disappear because computers will make all sounds. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you prefer the sound of a real acoustic piano to a digital keyboard?
Computers will write better song lyrics than human poets in the coming years. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you buy a book of poetry written entirely by an artificial intelligence?
An AI voice will soon sound more emotional than any human singer. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think an AI singer can have real soul or feeling?
A song with no repeating melody is not real music. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you enjoy listening to chaotic or unusual jazz melodies?
Future cities will be so noisy that humans will be unable to relax without headphones. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you wear headphones when you walk on busy city streets?
A song with words is always more interesting than a piece of instrumental music. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you listen to classical music when you are doing chores at home?
In the future, smart offices will automatically play personalized background music to make us work faster. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you like your boss to choose your study music?
A virtual, holographic singer will host the most popular concerts of the future. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you pay for a ticket to see a holographic concert of a dead singer?
A beautiful song is the fastest way to learn the culture of another country. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Has listening to songs helped you learn foreign language pronunciation?
We should only share our favorite songs with our closest friends. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you feel embarrassed when someone looks at your music playlist?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I listen the music every day. → I listen to music every day. (Underlined error: 'listen' is followed by the preposition 'to', and do not use 'the' for general concepts)
The song is very beautifully. → The song is very beautiful. (Underlined error: use the adjective 'beautiful' after the linking verb 'is' rather than the adverb 'beautifully')
He plays very good on the piano. → He plays very well on the piano. (Underlined error: use the adverb 'well' to modify the action verb 'plays' rather than the adjective 'good')
