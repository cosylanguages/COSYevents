🔥 Debate Duel

Core Dilemma High Income vs. Ample Leisure Time
Side A (Hot Take) Financial security is the foundation of peace.
Side B (Hot Take) Personal fulfillment requires open schedules.
Thematic Symbol 💵 Credit Card & Investments vs. 🧘‍♂️ Yoga Mat & Books

This session explores the delicate balance between financial accumulation and personal leisure. We will debate whether a high monthly income or abundant leisure hours brings more authentic fulfillment to our adult lives. We will also master comparative adverbs and focus adverbs in English to express our priorities and set healthy boundaries.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Income
money received, especially on a regular basis, for work or through investments.
Getting a secondary source of income dramatically reduced her monthly stress.
Leisure time ≠ working hours
time free from work or duties ≠ time spent performing professional obligations.
He spent his leisure time playing acoustic guitar, far away from his grueling working hours.
To accumulate
to gather or acquire an increasing number or quantity of something over time.
He worked eighty hours a week simply to accumulate wealth he had no time to enjoy.
Financial security
the peace of mind you feel when you have enough money to cover your expenses and emergencies.
For many young adults, financial security is far more important than buying luxury brands.
Affluence ≠ poverty
the state of having a great deal of money or wealth ≠ the state of being extremely poor.
Living in extreme affluence can occasionally blind people to the painful realities of modern poverty.
Burnout
physical or mental collapse caused by overwork or stress.
She had to take a two-month leave of absence to recover from severe professional burnout.
Fulfillment
the feeling of absolute satisfaction and happiness with your life achievements and goals.
He found genuine artistic fulfillment in writing short stories rather than chasing corporate bonuses.
To prioritize
to treat a specific goal or duty as being more important than other things.
To save his mental health, he decided to prioritize slow leisure time over career advancement.
Workaholism
an obsessive or compulsive need to work, often damaging one's personal life.
In many competitive corporate cultures, workaholism is praised as a positive trait.
Gratification
pleasure, especially when gained from the satisfaction of a desire.
Shopping online offers instant gratification, but the happiness is extremely short-lived.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:

Debate
A: A high income allows you to travel in absolute luxury and buy comfort.
B: Having abundant leisure hours keeps you physically fit and calm.
★ Do you think a higher monthly income would solve most of your daily worries?
A: Spending your leisure time with loved ones builds deep, beautiful memories.
B: Dedicating long hours to your career builds a powerful, respected network.
★ How do you protect your precious leisure time from office emails?
A: Working hard to accumulate savings prepares you for early retirement.
B: Taking regular breaks during the year allows you to enjoy youth.
★ Are you actively trying to accumulate assets, or do you prefer spending?
A: Absolute financial security frees you from survival anxiety and fear.
B: Living with minimal expenses teaches you true resourcefulness.
★ At what age do you hope to achieve complete financial security?
A: Living in affluence allows you to support charities and help family.
B: Living simply prevents the stress of maintaining expensive possessions.
★ Does living in a highly wealthy neighborhood make people happier or more competitive?
A: Prioritizing mental health keeps your body completely free from burnout.
B: Pushing your limits at work occasionally leads to extraordinary promotions.
★ Have you ever experienced professional burnout? How did you recover?
A: Finding deep fulfillment in creative hobbies is extremely satisfying.
B: Reaching a high sales target at work brings great pride and energy.
★ Where do you find more genuine fulfillment: in your career or hobbies?
A: Deciding to prioritize family shows a healthy, balanced character.
B: Prioritizing your company builds long-term wealth and power.
★ How do you prioritize your tasks when everything seems urgent?
A: Conquering workaholism allows you to rediscover slow, beautiful pleasures.
B: An intense dedication to work keeps you focused and away from lazy habits.
★ Do you consider yourself a victim of mild workaholism?
A: Delaying gratification helps you build massive long-term wealth.
B: Enjoying instant gratification keeps your daily morale high and joyful.
★ Are you good at delaying gratification for long-term goals?

🟢 Round 2 : Disadvantages (A/B Ideas)▲
Debate
A: A low monthly income makes it impossible to afford high-quality education.
B: High earnings often require sacrificing your physical and mental health.
★ Have you ever turned down a high-paying job because of the long hours?
A: Endless leisure time can lead to deep boredom and lack of purpose.
B: Infinite working hours completely destroy your social life and marriage.
★ Do you find long vacations relaxing, or do you start to feel unproductive?
A: Refusing to accumulate savings leaves you vulnerable to sudden crises.
B: Obsessing over accumulation turns you into a cold, greedy individual.
★ What is the absolute most useless thing you have accumulated?
A: Chasing financial security can trap you in a boring, uninspiring career.
B: Having zero security makes every daily expense highly stressful.
★ Have you ever stayed in a boring job solely for the steady paycheck?
A: Living in extreme affluence alienates you from simple, real-world friends.
B: Living in poverty stops you from pursuing creative artistic dreams.
★ Do you think wealth makes it harder to find authentic, loyal friends?
A: Pushing yourself to burnout can permanently damage your nervous system.
B: Avoiding all pressure makes you lazy and unprepared for life's challenges.
★ What are the early warning signs of burnout in your body?
A: Seeking fulfillment solely in hobbies can delay your career success.
B: Seeking fulfillment solely in your corporate title is highly dangerous.
★ Has your concept of personal fulfillment changed since you left school?
A: If you fail to prioritize your health, no amount of wealth can save you.
B: Failing to prioritize your duties can lead to sudden professional failure.
★ What is the hardest thing you had to deprioritize this year?
A: Chronic workaholism is a socially accepted addiction that ruins children.
B: Having no professional ambition makes you look lazy in modern dating.
★ Do you think modern society rewards workaholism too much?
A: Pursuing instant gratification ruins your long-term focus and discipline.
B: Delaying all gratification makes you a dry, unhappy person.
★ What is your favorite form of instant gratification after a hard day?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
We discussed about our working hours→We discussed our working hours('Discuss' is transitive and does not take the preposition 'about')
I prefer leisure time than working hours→I prefer leisure time to working hours(Use 'prefer A to B', not 'than')
The burnout made me to leave my job→The burnout made me leave my job(The causative verb 'make' is followed by a bare infinitive without 'to')
