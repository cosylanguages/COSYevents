      personal: ★ Do you think a high <strong>salary</strong> makes life much easier?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Having a lot of <strong>free time</strong> keeps you away from toxic office stress. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Being busy at work makes you feel active and productive.
      personal: ★ How do you usually spend your precious <strong>free time</strong>?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Working hard to <strong>earn</strong> money builds a strong, disciplined character. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Having free hours lets you learn new skills at your own pace.
      personal: ★ Do you have to work on weekends to <strong>earn</strong> extra money?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Having money to <strong>spend</strong> on luxury travel is very exciting. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Free time lets you enjoy simple pleasures like walking in nature.
      personal: ★ Do you prefer to <strong>spend</strong> your money or save it for the future?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> A <strong>rich</strong> lifestyle offers absolute safety and high-quality healthcare. <br/><strong>B:</strong> A simple, quiet life prevents complicated social anxieties.
      personal: ★ Do you wish to be very <strong>rich</strong>, or do you prefer a simple life?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Feeling <strong>relaxed</strong> during a long morning walk is beautiful. <br/><strong>B:</strong> A busy schedule keeps your mind sharp and highly focused.
      personal: ★ Do you feel completely <strong>relaxed</strong> when you are at home alone?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Having a creative <strong>hobby</strong> like painting brings great joy. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Earning a bonus at work is extremely satisfying.
      personal: ★ What is your favorite creative <strong>hobby</strong> today?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> A high salary is <strong>important</strong> to support your children. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Free hours are important to play and talk with your kids.

    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Real <strong>happiness</strong> comes from feeling calm and relaxed. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Buying nice gifts for friends brings wonderful shared joy.
      personal: '★ What brings you more <strong>happiness</strong>: buying a product or sleeping?'
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Having money to <strong>afford</strong> good books and classes is great. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Free hours are necessary to actually read the books you buy.
      personal: ★ What is one expensive thing you want to <strong>afford</strong> soon?

    - main: <strong>A:</strong> True <strong>happiness</strong> is impossible when you are constantly tired. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Possessions bring only a temporary wave of happiness.

mistakes:
  - wrong: Free time is more <u>important than money</u>
    right: Free time is <strong>more important than</strong> money
    note: (For long adjectives like 'important', use 'more important than', not 'importanter')
  - wrong: He <u>work</u> very hard to earn money
    right: He <strong>works</strong> very hard to earn money
    note: (Third-person singular 's' is required for verbs in the present simple)
