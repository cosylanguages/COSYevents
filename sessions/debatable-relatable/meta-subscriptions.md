    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Direct subscription fee <strong>revenue</strong> keeps companies focused on improving product quality. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Advertising <strong>revenue</strong> allows platforms to stay free and scale up their services.
      personal: ★ Do you think tech companies earn too much <strong>revenue</strong> from selling user data?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Platforms can share subscription revenue directly with each verified content <strong>creator</strong>. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Creator programs on free platforms allow anyone to gain viral fame without initial fees.
      personal: ★ Have you ever financially supported an independent content <strong>creator</strong>?
