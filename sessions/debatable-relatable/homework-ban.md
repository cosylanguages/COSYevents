mistakes:
  - wrong: The teacher gave me many homeworks
    right: The teacher gave me a lot of homework
    note: (Homework is an uncountable noun and cannot be pluralized)
  - wrong: I stopped to do assignments
    right: I stopped doing assignments
    note: (Stop + gerund means ending the action, whereas stop + infinitive means stopping another activity in order to do assignments)
  - wrong: If I was a student, I would...
    right: If I were a student, I would...
    note: (Using the subjunctive 'were' in the second conditional to express hypothetical scenarios)
  - wrong: We discussed about the homework
    right: We discussed the homework / had a discussion about the homework
    note: (The verb 'discuss' is transitive and does not take the preposition 'about')
