A balanced B1 debate on retirement. We analyze financial security, identity loss, pension systems, inflation risks, and finding purpose after a career.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Retirement ≠ career
the act of leaving one's job and ceasing to work permanently.
Early retirement requires disciplined financial planning.
Financial security ≠ debt
the condition of having steady income to support standard living.
Achieving financial security takes decades of effort.
Pension plan ≠ loan
an arrangement to provide an income when a person stops working.
Company pension plans vary widely across industries.
Inflation ≠ stability
a general increase in prices and fall in purchasing power.
High inflation erodes the real value of fixed pensions.
Identity ≠ obscurity
the characteristics defining who a person is.
Many professionals struggle when their job identity ends.
Purpose ≠ apathy
the reason for which something is done or created.
Finding a daily purpose is crucial for healthy aging.
Anxiety ≠ calmness
a feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease.
Financial anxiety can shadow early retirement dreams.
Longevity ≠ brevity
long life or long existence.
Increased longevity means retirement savings must last longer.
Contribution ≠ consumption
giving time, money, or effort to a common cause.
Senior citizens make valuable community contributions.
Lifestyle ≠ isolation
the way in which a person or group lives.
Maintaining an active lifestyle supports cognitive health.

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Use If + present simple, will + base verb to discuss realistic future retirement scenarios (e.g., If prices rise, pensions will lose value).

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
If I will save money, I will retire early. → If I save money, I will retire early. (Use present simple in the 'if' condition clause, not 'will'.)
Retirement depends from economic growth. → Retirement depends on economic growth. (Preposition rule: 'depend' collocates with 'on', not 'from'.)
