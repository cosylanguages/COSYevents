      personal: ★ Have you ever suffered from work-related <strong>burnout</strong>, and how did you recover?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Shorter working weeks increase overall <strong>productivity</strong> because employees waste less time. <br/><strong>B:</strong> A 5-day week ensures constant availability for clients.
      personal: ★ Do you feel your personal <strong>productivity</strong> is higher when you have a structured schedule?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> An extra day off directly improves mental <strong>well-being</strong> and reduces stress. <br/><strong>B:</strong> A standard 5-day routine provides stable habits that support physical health.
      personal: ★ What activities contribute most to your personal <strong>well-being</strong> during the weekend?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Offering 4-day schedules dramatically boosts staff <strong>retention</strong>. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Traditional schedules ensure predictable staffing and corporate stability.
      personal: ★ Would the promise of a 4-day week influence your choice to stay with a company, affecting employee <strong>retention</strong>?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Closing the office for an extra day reduces corporate <strong>overhead</strong> costs like electricity. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Spreading operations over 5 days maximizes the value of office lease investments.
      personal: ★ Do you think cutting corporate <strong>overhead</strong> is a good enough reason to work from home?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Employees love the extreme <strong>flexibility</strong> of a three-day weekend. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Fixed schedules make planning meetings and collaborative teamwork much simpler.
      personal: ★ How much <strong>flexibility</strong> do you currently have in your working hours?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Starting a 4-day <strong>trial</strong> allows the company to test results safely. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Consistent long-term schedules are safer for business than constant trials.
      personal: ★ Would you like to participate in a temporary <strong>trial</strong> of a shorter work week?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> It forces managers to focus on results and <strong>output</strong> rather than hours spent at a desk. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Physical presence for 5 days maximizes opportunities for creative collaboration.
      personal: ★ Is your job performance measured by your physical hours or by your actual <strong>output</strong>?
    - main: <strong>A:</strong> Eliminating one working day reduces weekly <strong>commute</strong> stress and carbon emissions. <br/><strong>B:</strong> Spreading travel over 5 days makes public transport schedules more predictable.
      personal: ★ How long is your daily <strong>commute</strong>, and does it drain your energy?

      personal: ★ What challenges would you expect during the <strong>implementation</strong> of a 4-day schedule in your workplace?
