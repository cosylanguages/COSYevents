🔬 Field Journal Research Log

Specimen / Topic

Anthropogenic Thermal Dynamics

Primary Hypothesis

Self-censorship occurs among clima-researchers to avoid alarmism.

Experimental Findings

Over 70% of polled meteorologists admit to softening report titles.

Linguistic Focus

Reported speech & softening devices (e.g. He claimed that, it is believed that)

🎙️ Audio Briefing Transcription / Science Digest ▼

How do scientists turn overwhelming climate data into compelling narratives that inspire real political change? When raw thermodynamic data is communicated in sterile statistics, society often fails to grasp the urgency of global warming. In this session, we analyze the power of linguistic framing, explore how public policy reports are crafted, and practice persuasive language techniques. Step up to voice your perspective on environmental policy, sharpen your debate skills, and contribute to an important global dialogue!

📖 Session Vocabulary

Controversy
a lot of disagreement or public discussion about a topic.
The construction of a new factory caused a major controversy.
Warming
the process of becoming warmer or hotter in temperature.
Global warming is causing glaciers to melt rapidly.
Censor
to block or remove parts of a report that are considered bad.
The government decided to censor the sensitive report.
Softness
the quality of being gentle, mild, or less severe.
The scientist adjusted the title to add softness to the report.
Pressure
strong persuasion or influence to force a decision.
He felt constant pressure from his boss to change the results.
Impact
a powerful effect or consequence of an action or event.
Climate change has a severe impact on farming.
Predict
to say or estimate that a specific thing will happen in the future.
Meteorologists predict heavy rain for the weekend.
Evidence
facts or information indicating whether a belief is true.
Scientists found clear evidence of rising sea levels.
Reduce
to make something smaller or less in size, amount, or value.
We must reduce our carbon emissions to protect the planet.
Support
to agree with or give assistance to a person or cause.
Many countries support the new climate agreement.

⚡ Grammar Practice : Reported Speech & Softening Devices▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Reported speech shifts tenses back in time. Softening devices (e.g., 'It is believed that...') make claims sound less direct or aggressive.

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: When reporting opinions, present simple usually shifts to _____. Softening devices use _____ constructions like 'it is said' to reduce directness.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. The climate scientist claimed that researchers _____ immense public pressure.
  2. It is widely _____ that self-censorship is becoming common in laboratories.
  3. The meteorologist reported that the glacier _____ entirely by 2025.
  4. Many policy-makers suggested that carbon taxes _____ increased immediately.
  5. It has been _____ that the report title was softened to prevent panic.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
The scientifics did a controversy→The scientists had a controversy(Use noun 'scientists', verb 'have a controversy')
I am agree with the report→I agree with the report(Agree is a verb)
It depend on computer models→It depends on computer models(Depends + on, singular verb s)
