🔬 Field Journal Research Log

Specimen / Topic

Liminal Spaces and Virtual Geography

Primary Hypothesis

Empty, repeating yellow hallways on the internet (the Backrooms) trigger strong feelings of comfort and fear together.

Experimental Findings

A psychology study shows that people find these virtual spaces highly interesting, and real museums are building yellow rooms to attract tourists.

Linguistic Focus

Expressing feelings and opinions (e.g. I feel scared when, makes me feel)

🎙️ Audio Briefing Transcription / Science Digest ▼

Why do endless, repetitive corridors and empty rooms trigger an intense psychological feeling of unease? The viral sensation of 'liminal spaces' captures how our brains struggle when environments lack familiar semantic landmarks and social cues. In this session, we explore environmental psychology, practice spatial prepositions, and describe mysterious atmospheres in simple English. Step into the mystery, describe strange landscapes, and build your confidence in a fun, immersive speaking club!

📖 Session Vocabulary

Creepy
scary or strange.
Empty buildings at night look very creepy.
Space
a physical area or room.
We need a quiet space to read books.
Explore
to travel around a place to learn about it.
I love to explore old castles and parks.
Mystery
something strange that is hard to explain.
The old house had a dark and interesting mystery.
Exit
a way out of a room or building.
Please find the red exit sign in case of an emergency.
Internet
a global computer network.
The internet helps us study and learn languages at home.
Popular
liked by many people.
Scary movies are very popular on Halloween.
Real
not fake or imaginary.
The yellow hallways are fake, but the museum is real.
Lonely
sad because you are alone.
A massive empty office can feel very lonely.
Wall
a physical side of a room.
They painted the bedroom wall a soft blue color.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
Endless halls make me to feel lonely. → Endless halls make me feel lonely. (Underlined error: causative 'make' requires a bare infinitive without 'to')
This photo is very popular on the internet. → This photo is very popular on the internet. (Underlined structure is correct! Good usage of preposition 'on')
I am scared about empty offices. → I am scared of empty offices / frightened by empty offices. (Underlined error: 'scared' is followed by preposition 'of' rather than 'about')
