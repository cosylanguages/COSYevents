🔬 Field Journal Research Log

A comprehensive clinical trial investigated bioactive compounds in coffee, showing marked effects on basal metabolic rate, cortisol regulation, and lean muscle mass preservation.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Metabolism ≠ Stagnation
the chemical processes that occur within a living organism to maintain life.
Caffeine temporarily accelerates basal metabolism.
Cortisol ≠ Serotonin
a steroid hormone regulating stress responses and metabolism.
Drinking coffee on an empty stomach can elevate cortisol levels.
Polyphenol ≠ Pro-oxidant
an antioxidant compound found abundantly in plant foods.
Coffee is a major dietary source of protective polyphenols.
Insulin Sensitivity ≠ Insulin resistance
how responsive cells are to insulin.
Improved insulin sensitivity aids glucose uptake in muscle tissue.
Lipid ≠ Carbohydrate
a class of organic compounds that are fatty acids or insoluble in water.
Coffee compounds stimulate lipid oxidation during exercise.
Bioactive ≠ Inert
having a biological effect on living tissue.
Bioactive polyphenols exhibit strong anti-inflammatory properties.
Endocrine ≠ Exocrine
relating to glands that secrete hormones directly into the bloodstream.
Caffeine interacts with the human endocrine system.
Preservation ≠ Degradation
the maintenance of something in its original state or in good condition.
Lean muscle preservation is crucial during weight loss.
Modulation ≠ Disruption
the exertion of a modifying or controlling influence on something.
Hormonal modulation influences appetite and energy levels.
Homeostasis ≠ Imbalance
the tendency toward a relatively stable equilibrium between interdependent elements.
The body continuously strives for metabolic homeostasis.
