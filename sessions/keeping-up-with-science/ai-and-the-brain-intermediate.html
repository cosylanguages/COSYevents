🎙️ Audio Briefing Transcription / Science Digest ▼

What if artificial intelligence and your biological brain process reality in completely opposite ways? While AI supercomputers consume vast amounts of electricity to simulate neural nodes, your biological brain achieves incredible feats of reasoning on just twenty watts of power. In this session, we will examine how synaptic plasticity differs from machine backpropagation, unpack the true efficiency of biological memory, and practice contrast connectors to express complex differences. Step into our laboratory of ideas, test your assumptions about digital mind power, and share your perspective on the future of AI!

📖 Session Vocabulary

Synapse
a junction between two nerve cells in the brain.
Signals travel across the synapse to connect brain cells.
Artificial ≠ Natural
made or produced by human beings rather than occurring naturally ≠ existing in or caused by nature; not made or caused by humankind.
There is a massive contrast between artificial networks and natural biological brains.
Contrast
the state of being strikingly different from something else.
There is a massive contrast between biological and digital brains.
Network
a complex system of interconnected things, cells, or computers.
Our brain is a vast biological network of billions of cells.
Energy
power derived from physical or chemical resources.
The brain uses very little energy to perform complex calculations.
Plasticity
the brain's ability to adapt and change its structure.
Synaptic plasticity is the biological basis of learning.
Node
a connection point in a system or artificial network.
An artificial neural network consists of thousands of nodes.
Efficiency ≠ Inefficiency
the state or quality of being efficient and saving resources ≠ lack of efficiency; waste of time or energy.
Biological brains possess superior energy efficiency, whereas standard digital chips show thermodynamic inefficiency.
Structure
the arrangement of and relations between parts of something.
The brain's structure is fundamentally different from a chip.
Compare
estimate, measure, or note the similarity or dissimilarity.
Scientists compare artificial nodes to biological synapses.

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Contrast connectors show a difference. Connectors like 'whereas' link two clauses, while 'despite' takes a noun or gerund.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
The AI is more better than human. → The AI is much better than a human / superior to a human (Double comparison: Do not combine 'more' with comparative adjectives like 'better')
We must discuss about this new chip. → We must discuss this new chip. (Do not use 'about' after the transitive verb 'discuss')
The computer make a lot of research. → The computer does a lot of research. (Collocation: We 'do research', we do not 'make research'. Also match singular subject 'computer')
