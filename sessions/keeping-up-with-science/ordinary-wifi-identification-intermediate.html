Based on ScienceDaily research (August 2026). Explore how ordinary WiFi routers can identify individuals through body wave distortion, passive biometric tracking, and privacy implications.

📖 Session Vocabulary

WiFi signal ≠ cable
a facility allowing computers or smartphones to connect to the internet wirelessly.
Standard WiFi signals can bounce off human bodies to identify individuals.
Accuracy ≠ error
the quality or state of being correct or precise.
The new system identifies people with near-perfect accuracy.
Detection ≠ ignorance
the action or process of identifying the presence of something.
WiFi-based detection works through solid walls without cameras.
Biometric ≠ password
relating to the measurement and analysis of unique physical characteristics.
Gait and body shape serve as invisible biometric markers.
Surveillance ≠ privacy
close observation, especially by a government or organization.
WiFi tracking raises fresh concerns about invisible surveillance.
Distortion ≠ clarity
the action of giving a misleading account or impression.
Body movements create distinct distortion patterns in radio waves.
Receiver ≠ transmitter
an apparatus for receiving incoming radio signals.
The router receiver analyzes signal fluctuations instantly.
Intrusion ≠ security
an unwelcome infiltration into a personal environment.
Unseen tracking is seen by many as a privacy intrusion.
Authentication ≠ denial
the process or action of proving something to be genuine.
WiFi biometrics could replace password authentication for smart homes.
Passive ≠ active
accepting or allowing what happens without active response.
Passive tracking requires no wearable devices or user action.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
The signals is analyzed by AI. → The signals are analyzed by AI. (Plural subject 'signals' takes plural passive 'are analyzed'.)
It identify people with accuracy. → It identifies people with high accuracy / accurately. (3rd person singular present simple verb requires '-s': 'identifies'.)
