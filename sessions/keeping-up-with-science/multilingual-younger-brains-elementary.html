🔬 Field Journal Research Log

Neuroscientists discovered that switching between languages builds cognitive reserve and keeps the brain flexible across all age groups.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Multilingual ≠ Monolingual
using or knowing several languages.
Growing up in a multilingual family is a great advantage.
Brain ≠ Body
the organ inside the head that controls thought and feeling.
Learning new skills builds new brain pathways.
Young ≠ Old
having lived or existed for only a short time; youthful.
Active habits keep your mind young.
Memory ≠ Forgetfulness
the ability to remember information.
Language practice improves daily memory.
Active ≠ Passive
engaged in action or activity.
An active mind stays sharp longer.
Focus ≠ Distraction
the center of interest or attention.
Switching tasks requires mental focus.
Flexible ≠ Rigid
able to adapt easily to different situations.
Multilingual speakers have flexible minds.
Health ≠ Illness
the state of being free from illness or injury.
Exercise supports overall brain health.
Skill ≠ Inability
the ability to do something well.
Speaking two languages is a useful skill.
Daily ≠ Occasional
done or occurring every day.
Daily practice builds lasting habits.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up (10 mins)▲

Welcome! Share your initial thoughts on this topic. What personal experiences or observations come to mind right away?

⚡ Grammar Practice : Present Simple for Scientific Facts (A2)▲
Use the Present Simple to express general truths and facts: "Language practice keeps the brain active." / "Multilingual people switch between tasks faster."
🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)▲
Being multilingual keeps your brain active and young.
★ How many languages do you practice every week?
Switching languages improves daily focus and mental flexibility.
★ Do you find it easy or hard to switch between languages?
Regular language learning builds stronger memory skills.
★ What is your favorite memory-training trick?
Keeping an active mind supports long-term mental health.
★ What non-language activities keep your mind active?
Developing a new skill gives confidence in daily conversations.
★ What new skill would you like to learn next year?
🟢 Round 2: Deep Speculation & Future Horizon (Units 6–10)▲
Will future schools teach two or more languages to all young students?
★ At what age did you start learning your second language?
How does having a flexible brain help in modern digital careers?
★ How do you handle unexpected changes at work or school?
Can daily mental exercises prevent memory loss in older age?
★ What daily habit brings you the most joy?
Why is language learning one of the best activities for brain health?
★ Why did you choose to learn English?
How can multilingual communities encourage better cultural understanding?
★ What culture would you love to explore deeply?
