vocabulary:
  - word: "Interpersonal chemistry"
    definition: "the psychological and emotional connection that develops between individuals."
    example: "They mistook their shared project struggles for a rare, organic interpersonal chemistry."
  - word: "Cognitive dissonance"
    definition: "the mental discomfort that occurs when a person's behavior contradicts their beliefs."
    example: "Asking an adversary for a favor triggers cognitive dissonance, forcing them to re-evaluate their dislike."
  - word: "Self-perception theory"
    definition: "the psychological model suggesting we determine our attitudes by observing our own behavior."
    example: "According to self-perception theory, we conclude we like someone because we chose to help them."
  - word: "Reciprocity"
    definition: "the social norm of exchanging actions, kindness, or benefits for mutual advantage."
    example: "True connection transcends simple reciprocity; it reshapes our underlying emotional alignments."
  - word: "Adversary"
    definition: "an opponent, competitor, or person who opposes or is hostile toward another."
    example: "Franklin successfully transformed a bitter political adversary into a lifelong, cooperative ally."
  - word: "Attitude"
    definition: "a settled way of thinking or feeling about someone, typically reflected in behavior."
    example: "His hostile attitude softened immediately after he agreed to lend his rare book."
  - word: "Favor"
    definition: "an act of kindness or helpfulness beyond what is strictly required or expected."
    example: "Lending the rare book was a strategic favor designed to resolve political tension."
  - word: "Connection"
    definition: "an emotional or intellectual relationship characterized by deep mutual understanding."
    example: "A small, cooperative gesture can establish a profound, lasting connection between complete strangers."
  - word: "Indebtedness"
    definition: "the state of feeling obliged or duty-bound to return a benefit or kindness."
    example: "He disliked the feeling of social indebtedness, preferring to keep his relationships strictly transactional."
  - word: "Cooperate"
    definition: "to work or act together with others to achieve shared goals or mutual benefits."
    example: "When forced to cooperate on a crisis, rivals often discover their shared humanity and values."
