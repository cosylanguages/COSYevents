Bounded Rationality : The Limits of Decision Making

This session explores Bounded Rationality-the psychological reality that human decision-making is strictly limited by cognitive constraints, time, and available information. We will examine how our minds default to 'satisficing' (choosing a good-enough option) rather than optimizing, and discuss how this shapes modern consumer choices and financial anxiety.

🧠 Subconscious Mind Profile

Core Human Tendency Seeking Perfection in Complex Situations
Subconscious Trigger Information Overload & Time Constraints
Psychological Phenomenon Satisficing vs. Optimizing
Self-Reflection Anchor Accepting Cognitive and Information Limits

📖 Session Vocabulary

Satisficing
the decision-making strategy of choosing an option that is 'good enough' rather than optimal.
Faced with fifty different options, he settled on a satisficing choice to save time and energy.
Optimizing
the cognitively demanding process of searching for the absolute best possible outcome.
The algorithm spent hours optimizing the route, while the driver preferred a simple, satisficing path.
Heuristic
a cognitive shortcut or rule of thumb used to make decisions quickly.
She relied on a simple heuristic: if a product has thousands of positive reviews, it is safe to buy.
Cognitive limit
the boundary of human mental capacity to process complex data and variables.
The sudden influx of market data pushed his cognitive limit to the absolute breaking point.
Anxiety
the feeling of worry, nervousness, or unease about an uncertain outcome.
Information overload in the modern digital landscape directly fuels our chronic decision anxiety.
Sovereignty
the state of having independent authority or complete freedom of choice.
Consumer sovereignty is often an illusion manufactured by sophisticated marketing algorithms.
Pragmatic
dealing with things sensibly and realistically in a way that is based on practical considerations.
He took a pragmatic approach to his career, choosing stability over a risky search for a perfect dream job.
Optimization
the action of making the best or most effective use of a situation or resource.
The constant optimization of our personal lives through tracking apps can lead to severe burnout.
Satiation
the state of being completely satisfied or filled to maximum capacity.
The constant stream of entertainment leads to mental satiation, making simple pleasures feel boring.
Information-processing
the cognitive system of gathering, analyzing, and storing external data.
Our brain's information-processing speed has not kept pace with the rapid growth of the digital world.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up ▲
Short introduction to making choices.
  • Are we truly rational decision-makers, or are we just tired biological systems looking for 'good enough' shortcuts?
  • Why does having unlimited options often make us feel less satisfied with our ultimate choice?
🔵 Round 1 : Deep Discussion ▲
Questions
10 items required. Use .round-item-main and .round-item-personal.
How does Satisficing protect our psychological well-being from the exhaustion of modern consumer culture?
★ When was the last time you made a quick, Satisficing decision that turned out to be perfect?
Why is the pursuit of absolute Optimizing in relationships a recipe for chronic dissatisfaction?
★ Have you ever felt paralyzed by the pressure of Optimizing every single aspect of your career?
Which mental Heuristic do you rely on most often when navigating high-stakes professional crises?
★ Can you identify a personal Heuristic that has occasionally led you to make a major error?
How does modern software exploit our Cognitive limit to nudge us toward predictable behavior?
★ When did you last feel that you had reached your absolute Cognitive limit during a discussion?
Why does the illusion of unlimited choice directly trigger deep, underlying decision Anxiety ?
★ How do you personally manage your Anxiety when forced to make a life-changing choice under pressure?
To what extent does algorithmic targeting undermine our individual decision Sovereignty ?
★ Do you believe that you retain complete Sovereignty over your daily political and social opinions?
Why is a Pragmatic choice often far more valuable than a theoretically perfect solution in business?
★ How Pragmatic are you when managing your household budget versus your creative passions?
Does the continuous Optimization of our lives leave any room for spontaneous joy and chance?
★ What is one area of your life where you refuse to practice any form of self- Optimization ?
Why does digital media aim for complete sensory Satiation rather than intellectual growth?
★ Have you ever reached a point of complete Satiation where even your favorite hobby felt boring?
How can we improve our Information-processing habits to protect ourselves from digital burnout?
★ What strategy do you use to filter out irrelevant data and protect your Information-processing focus?
🟣 Let's Speak Together ▲

The Choice Paradox: Imagine you are advising a friend who is paralyzed by choice while looking for a new apartment. Guide them to a decision using at least one second conditional, one third conditional, and one mixed conditional.

🟢 Round 2 : Conditional Scenarios ▲
Dilemmas & Projections
10 items required. Speculate, brainstorm, and share your personal opinions and ideas.
If you adopted a purely Satisficing strategy tomorrow, would your daily stress levels drop significantly?
If search engines stopped ranking results, would you find Optimizing your purchases completely impossible?
If you relied solely on a logical Heuristic , would you lose your capacity for creative intuition?
If you had respected your Cognitive limit last year, would you have avoided that severe burnout?
If they had designed a more Pragmatic interface, would their software have dominated the market today?
If your brain had not been overwhelmed by choice, would you now feel less decision Anxiety about your path?
If you had maintained complete Sovereignty over your time back then, would you be living in a different city today?
If your self- Optimization habits had been less intense, would you feel more content with your life today?
If we had reached a state of digital Satiation years ago, would we now spend more time in nature?
If our Information-processing speed had been doubled by evolution, would we still struggle with modern complexity today?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections) ▲
We are Bounded with time → We are Bounded by time (Use preposition 'by' after passive Bounded )
I took a decision fastly → I made a decision quickly (Collocation 'make a decision', adverb 'quickly')
He explained me the theory → He explained the theory to me (Structure of explain + object + to + person)
