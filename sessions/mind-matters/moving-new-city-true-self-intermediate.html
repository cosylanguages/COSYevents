Why Do We Feel Like We Find Our "True Self" When We Move to a New City?

20 January 2027

Home / Events / Mind Matters / Moving and the True Self (B1)
← Back to Club

Duration

60 minutes

Languages

🇬🇧 English

Level

Intermediate (B1)

Theme

Geographical Relocation & Renewal

This session explores the psychological fantasy of starting fresh in a new environment. We will discuss whether moving actually transforms our character or if we simply carry our old anxieties into new streets. Join us for a friendly, deeply reflective conversation about the geography of happiness and inner peace.

🧠 Subconscious Mind Profile

Core Human Tendency Identity Reconstruction
Subconscious Trigger Geographical Dislocation
Psychological Phenomenon Tabula Rasa Effect
Self-Reflection Anchor Defining Self outside Comfort Zones

📖 Session Vocabulary

Identity
the qualities, beliefs, and personality traits that make a person who they are.
Moving to another country is a great opportunity to redefine your personal identity.
Belonging
the comfortable feeling of being accepted and supported by a group or place.
After living in London for six months, she finally developed a sweet sense of belonging.
Attachment
a strong feeling of affection, connection, or loyalty to a specific person or place.
His deep attachment to his childhood neighborhood made it very difficult for him to relocate.
Novelty
the quality of being new, exciting, fresh, and interesting.
The initial novelty of exploring a foreign city can make you forget all your worries.
Adaptation
the process of changing your behavior or habits to suit a new situation or place.
Linguistic adaptation is a gradual process that requires patience and regular practice.
Self-concept
the mental picture and ideas you have about your own character, abilities, and value.
A positive self-concept helps people face difficult life transitions with more confidence.
Habits
regular routines and behaviors that you do automatically without thinking.
It is much easier to establish healthy daily habits when you change your surroundings.
Environment
the physical, social, and cultural conditions in which people live and work.
A quiet and peaceful physical environment is essential for productive studying.
Reinvention
the process of changing your style, attitude, or career to present yourself in a fresh way.
Starting a new job offers the perfect opportunity for professional reinvention.
Comfort zone
a situation or place where you feel safe, relaxed, and not stressed.
You must step out of your comfort zone if you want to discover your true capabilities.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Have you ever traveled or moved to a city where you did not know anyone?
  • Do you think that changing your physical surroundings can make you a happier person?
🔵 Round 1 : Analysis▲
Why do we feel a sudden shift in our personal Identity when we walk down unfamiliar streets in a brand new city?
※ How did your sense of Identity change when you moved or traveled to a new place for the first time?
Is the search for a new city often driven by a deep subconscious desire for a sense of Belonging?
※ Have you ever felt a strong feeling of Belonging in a place where you were a complete stranger?
Why does the human mind form a strong, immediate Attachment to certain geographic places but not others?
※ Do you feel a powerful emotional Attachment to the town where you grew up?
Does the chemical excitement of pure Novelty temporarily hide our deeper anxieties when we arrive in a new town?
※ Have you ever chased a sense of Novelty simply to distract yourself from daily routine stress?
Why does the gradual process of Adaptation to a new culture feel so emotionally exhausting after a few months?
※ What was the most difficult part of Adaptation you experienced during a major change in your life?
How does changing our physical surroundings destabilize our Self-concept and force us to look at ourselves differently?
※ How would you describe your Self-concept to someone who has absolutely no knowledge of your background?
Is starting fresh in a new environment the easiest way to break old, automated Habits?
※ Have you ever successfully built positive daily Habits simply by changing your physical space?
How does our daily physical Environment quietly reinforce the roles we play and keep us from growing?
※ What is one thing in your current social Environment that you would like to change today?
Why is it psychologically easier to achieve radical self-Reinvention when we are surrounded by complete strangers?
※ If you could have a blank slate, what part of your character would you choose for Reinvention?
Does stepping out of our geographical Comfort zone accelerate our cognitive growth and personal maturity?
※ Tell us about a time when you pushed yourself far outside your Comfort zone. What did you discover?
🟣 Let's Speak Together : The Fresh Start Experiment▲

The Blank Canvas Scenario: Imagine you wake up tomorrow in a city where nobody knows your name, your job, your past, or your mistakes. Who would you become during the next year? Explain your plans and hopes using at least one second conditional, one third conditional, and one mixed conditional.

🟢 Round 2 : Conditional Practices▲
If you moved to a remote, quiet village tomorrow, your core Identity would stay exactly the same.
If you had built a deeper sense of Belonging at home, you would not have felt the need to relocate.
If we never developed any Attachment to physical places, our lives would feel much more liberating and light.
If a new city no longer offered you any Novelty after a few weeks, you would immediately begin packing again.
If your initial Adaptation to your current town had been completely effortless, you would not have grown stronger.
If you had been raised in five different countries, your core Self-concept would be completely different today.
If you relocated to a different country every month, your daily Habits would become totally chaotic.
If we lived in a highly restrictive social Environment, we would have to suppress our true desires.
If you had never experienced the freedom of geographic anonymity, your self-Reinvention would be much slower today.
If your family never pushed you to leave your comfortable Comfort zone, you would have missed some great opportunities.
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
If I would move to London, I would be happy. → If I moved to London, I would be happy. In a second conditional 'if' clause, use the past simple ('moved') instead of 'would move'.
Living in a new city made me to change my habits. → Living in a new city made me change my habits. The causative verb 'make' takes a bare infinitive ('change') without 'to'.
I am living here since five years. → I have been living here for five years. Use 'for' to specify a period or duration of time, and 'since' for a specific starting point.
