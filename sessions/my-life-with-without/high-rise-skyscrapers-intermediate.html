Duration

Languages

🇬🇧 English

Level

Intermediate (B1)

Theme

Skyscrapers, Urban Living, and Community Structure

This session explores the practical and visual impact of skyscrapers on modern urban environments and our personal lives. We will discuss the experience of living or working in a towering high-rise building versus a traditional low-rise neighborhood. Participants will share stories of how vertical cities affect our daily stress levels and our sense of neighborhood community.

⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: High-Rise Skyscrapers (B1)

Presence (With)

• Spectacular panoramic city views and housing density.
• Convenient integrated amenities (elevators, waste pipes, parking floors).
• High risk of community isolation and lack of garden/ground green space.

Absence (Without)

• Cozy horizontal neighborhoods with superior local community connections.
• Absolute ease of walking directly onto the grass or streets without lifts.
• Uncontrolled suburban sprawl and long, exhausting commutes to city centers.

🗣️ Syntactic Anchor (CEFR B1): Express comparative preferences using would rather (e.g., "I would rather live in a low-rise house because of the garden, than live in a skyscraper penthouse").

📖 Session Vocabulary

Skyscraper
a very tall building of many stories.
The city skyline is dominated by a newly built glass skyscraper.
High-rise
a multi-story building.
Living in a high-rise apartment offers a wonderful panoramic view of the harbor.
Panoramic
with a wide view surrounding an observer.
The rooftop restaurant offers a panoramic view of the mountain range.
Density
the degree of compactness of a population.
High population density in cities requires vertical housing models.
Isolation
the state of being separate from others.
Living on the 40th floor of a high-rise can cause a feeling of isolation.
Connection
a feeling of being linked, integrated, or united with others.
Participating in local neighborhood events creates a strong sense of connection.
Urbanization
the process of making an area more urban and developed.
Rapid urbanization has transformed sleepy villages into bustling high-rise cities.
Infrastructure
the basic physical and organizational structures needed for society.
High-rise neighborhoods require advanced water and waste infrastructure.
Community
a group of people living in the same place.
Low-rise suburban neighborhoods often have a stronger sense of community.
Tranquility
the quality or state of being calm and quiet.
He escaped the noisy city center to find some tranquility in the countryside.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Have you ever visited or lived in a high-rise apartment on a very high floor?
  • Do you feel nervous or excited when looking down from great heights?
🔵 Round 1 : My Life With Skyscrapers▲
Narrative & Necessity
Why do giant corporations prefer vertical architecture? Would you rather work in an iconic glass skyscraper or a cozy brick building?
★ Have you ever visited the top floor of a famous skyscraper?
Would you rather buy a small high-rise studio apartment with a view or a spacious ground-floor townhouse?
★ What floor do you currently live on, and do you like it?
Would you rather have a panoramic city view with high wind noise or a ground-floor patio with street noise?
★ What is the most beautiful urban view you have ever seen?
Does extreme vertical population density force you to behave more politely to neighbors?
★ Do you prefer bustling urban environments or quiet, empty spaces?
Why do skyscraper residents struggle with social isolation? Would you rather have 1,000 neighbors or live in a quiet forest?
★ Have you ever felt lonely in a large city?
Would you rather build a strong connection with your floor neighbors or with the local shopkeepers downstairs?
★ How do you build good relationships with people in your neighborhood?
Does rapid urbanization require that cities build vertical towers to prevent massive agricultural loss?
★ Has your home city changed much since you were a child?
Would you rather manage municipal water infrastructure for a vertical tower or for 100 separate houses?
★ Have you ever experienced a water or power outage in a high-rise building?
Why is it nearly impossible to develop a cooperative neighborhood community inside a modern luxury skyscraper?
★ Do you know your neighbors' names in your current building?
Would you rather find emotional tranquility on a high-rise rooftop or by a quiet forest lake?
★ How do you find a quiet, tranquil moment during a busy work week?

🟢 Round 2 : My Life Without Skyscrapers▲
Speculative Adaptation
If towering skyscrapers were banned, how would urban planners reorganize city centers to prevent overcrowding?
★ Would you be willing to pay higher rent to live in a low-rise house?
Without any vertical high-rise blocks, how would metropolises protect nearby farmlands from horizontal sprawl?
★ How would a longer commute change your lifestyle?
If there were no top floors to visit, what would replace the panoramic observation deck as the primary tourist attraction of a city?
★ Do you feel happier when you can step directly onto green grass?
Without high population density, what new local community networks would people invent to maintain social connections?
★ Do you prefer protected forests or spacious suburbs?
If vertical living disappeared, would we completely cure the social isolation typical of massive metropolises?
★ What is your favorite time of day to look at the city sky?
If physical buildings were kept low, would we naturally restore a deep, empathetic connection among neighbors?
★ What local community group or activity would you enjoy joining?
If urbanization shifted back to flat, rural-style layouts, what speculative industries would collapse or grow?
★ Would you ride a bicycle to work if there were no high-speed highways?
Without vertical pipes and lifts, would cities find it easier to upgrade their general utility infrastructure?
★ Do you feel more productive working in a home office?
Without vertical blocks, what local squares or physical hubs would the community design to gather and socialize?
★ Does crowded public transport make you feel highly stressed?
If we lived closer to the natural soil, how would our personal mental tranquility be affected?
★ Do you think small town residents are friendlier than big city residents?
🏁 Closing "Mic Drop" Rituals▲

1. The Adjective Sentence Completion

Complete the sentence in exactly one turn:
"My life without skyscrapers would be ___________ because..."
(Examples: peaceful, sprawling, grounded, quiet, simple, flat, low)

2. The Year 2125 Technology Pivot

Imagine the year is 2125. Overpopulation forces humanity to live in subterranean underground cities, while the surface is returned to wild forests and oceans. There are no high buildings.
Would you choose to live in this highly vertical underground hive, or would you reject it and try to survive in the wild surface? Why?

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I live in a high floor→I live on a high floor(Use preposition 'on' for floors)
The skyscrapers are beautifuls→Skyscrapers are beautiful(Adjectives do not have plural forms, omit 'the' for general plurals)
It depend of your city→It depends on your city(Depends + on preposition)
