round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : Speculative Analysis (Life WITHOUT Planes)'
  badge: Projections
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: Imagine a world with absolutely no <strong>aviation</strong>-how would our global economy and remote supply chains survive?
    - main: Without planes, every international <strong>passenger</strong> would have to spend weeks traveling on ocean liners and trans-continental trains.
    - main: Reaching a distant <strong>destination</strong> would become a rare, once-in-a-lifetime achievement rather than a quick weekend plan.
    - main: The complete absence of planes would mean the end of physical <strong>jet lag</strong>, aligning our bodies with local geographic time.
    - main: Instead of booking quick weekend splurges, <strong>long-haul</strong> journeys would require deep, careful planning and months of free time.
    - main: A shift to trains and ships would make global tourism highly <strong>eco-friendly</strong>, drastically reducing atmospheric pollution.
    - main: The philosophy of <strong>slow travel</strong> would become a normal reality for everyone rather than a trendy lifestyle choice.
    - main: Without strict airport security and <strong>baggage allowance</strong> fees, packing would become much more relaxed and comfortable.
    - main: The prestigious concept of the <strong>frequent flyer</strong> would be replaced by slow, seafaring passengers sharing months-long voyages.
    - main: Instead of a quick airport <strong>layover</strong>, travelers would spend days resting in beautiful, historic train station hotels.
mistakes:
  - wrong: We traveled <u>by the plane</u> last summer.
    right: We traveled <strong>by plane</strong> last summer.
    note: '(Underlined error: when specifying a means of transport with ''by'', do not use an article like ''the'' or ''a'')'
  - wrong: The jet lag made me <u>to feel</u> very tired.
    right: The jet lag made me <strong>feel</strong> very tired.
    note: '(Underlined error: the causative verb ''make'' takes a bare infinitive without ''to'')'
