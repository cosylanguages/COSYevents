life_ledger:
  title: '⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: Car Life'
  with_title: Presence (With)
  with_content: • Unrestricted point-to-point mobility and spontaneous travel autonomy.<br>• Comfort of a private cabin shelter from cold/inclement weather.<br>• Regular financial outlays for fuel, maintenance, insurance, and parking.
  without_title: Absence (Without)
  without_content: • Drastic reduction in household overheads and zero depreciation stress.<br>• Healthier lifestyle driven by active transport (cycling, walking).<br>• Vulnerability to transit schedules, delays, and last-mile connectivity gaps.

round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : My Life With a Car'
  badge: Narrative &amp; Necessity
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: How does owning a car provide a level of personal <strong>mobility</strong> that you simply cannot find on public transit?
      personal: ★ Think about how you <strong>used to</strong> travel when you were a teenager-was it more or less comfortable?
    - main: Is a city's road <strong>infrastructure</strong> an absolute necessity, or does it exist because societies <strong>used to</strong> prioritize cars over pedestrian life?
      personal: ★ Have you ever had a car breakdown at a bad time?
    - main: Why do car owners refuse to use <strong>public transport</strong> even if the network is faster than driving?
      personal: ★ Are you satisfied with the transport in your city?
    - main: Can someone maintain good mental health if they have to sit in traffic <strong>congestion</strong> on their daily commute?
      personal: ★ If your personality was a car, what model would it be?
    - main: Does the financial cost of regular car <strong>maintenance</strong> feel like a reasonable price for comfort, or does it feel like a burden?
      personal: ★ Do you track your car expenses carefully?
    - main: Does owning a private car give you genuine <strong>autonomy</strong>, or are you actually dependent on its constant expenses?
      personal: ★ Think about how your family <strong>used to</strong> plan weekend trips when you were young-did a car make it easier?
    - main: Is selling your car the only realistic way for a citizen to drastically lower their personal <strong>carbon footprint</strong>?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer to walk or drive when running local errands?
    - main: Why do people subject themselves to a stressful daily <strong>commute</strong> in a private car instead of walking or cycling?
      personal: ★ Was learning to drive a stressful experience for you?
    - main: How does high <strong>walkability</strong> in a neighborhood reduce the psychological need to own a private vehicle?
      personal: ★ Do you care about the brand or look of the car you drive?
    - main: Why do millions of buyers accept the rapid financial <strong>depreciation</strong> of a new vehicle just to own a private cabin?
      personal: ★ Would you feel comfortable sharing a ride with complete strangers?

round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : My Life Without a Car'
  badge: Speculative Adaptation
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: If personal cars were completely outlawed in city centers, how would communities redesign local urban <strong>mobility</strong> and transit systems?
      personal: ★ What would be your biggest daily challenge without a car?
    - main: Without any cars to plan for, how could urban planners adapt city <strong>infrastructure</strong> to support vibrant pedestrian life?
      personal: ★ Do you think you would know your neighbors better if you walked everywhere?
    - main: If private vehicles did not exist, how would local authorities adapt <strong>public transport</strong> to handle the massive passenger load?
      personal: ★ Would you trust a car that has no steering wheel?
    - main: In a car-free city, would road <strong>congestion</strong> completely disappear, or would it simply shift to bike lanes and sidewalks?
      personal: ★ What would you do with the extra space in your home?
    - main: If families didn't have to pay for car <strong>maintenance</strong>, what alternative local services or experiences would they spend their savings on?
      personal: ★ Have you ever experienced "road rage"?
    - main: Without a private vehicle, would you lose your sense of personal <strong>autonomy</strong>, or would you find new ways to stay independent?
      personal: ★ What is the one thing public transport can never replace for you?
    - main: In a fully car-free society, how would the commercial logistics network adapt to lower its collective <strong>carbon footprint</strong>?
      personal: ★ What is the best place you have ever reached only by car?
    - main: Without roads dominating our landscape, would the daily <strong>commute</strong> to work become a highly active and social journey?
      personal: ★ Was childhood neighborhood safe for playing in the street?
    - main: If streets were turned into parks, how would high <strong>walkability</strong> change the way neighbors communicate and support each other?
      personal: ★ How would a massive increase in fuel prices change your lifestyle?
    - main: Since citizens wouldn't suffer from the financial <strong>depreciation</strong> of vehicles, how would they invest their capital instead?
      personal: ★ Which city do you think is the best model for a car-free future?
