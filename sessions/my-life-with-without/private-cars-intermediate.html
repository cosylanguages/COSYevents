Theme

Private Cars, Transportation, and Mobility Choices

This session explores the transition from relying on private cars to adopting alternative methods of transportation. We will discuss the freedom and financial costs associated with owning a personal vehicle. Participants will share stories of navigating cities without a car and debate the future of private mobility.

⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: Private Cars (B1)

Presence (With)

• Unrestricted point-to-point mobility and spontaneous travel autonomy.
• Shelter from inclement weather and private comfortable cabin.
• Financial outlays for fuel, insurance, and rapid depreciation.

Absence (Without)

• High cost savings and zero car maintenance stress.
• Active, healthy transit habits (walking, cycling, clean trains).
• Vulnerability to rigid timetables and delayed connections.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Mobility
the ability to move or be moved freely and easily.
Owning a private car offers unparalleled personal mobility.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Do you own a private car or use other transport?
  • What is the most popular car brand in your country?
🔵 Round 1 : My Life With Private Cars▲
Narrative & Necessity
If you owned a luxury electric vehicle, would your overall daily mobility feel more comfortable?
★ What was your first car, and how did you feel driving it?
If city authorities built more bike lanes, would you continue supporting massive road infrastructure for private cars?
★ Are roads well-maintained in your local area?
If your local public transport system were completely free, would you sell your personal car immediately?
★ What is your favorite method of public transport?
If your city banned private vehicles, would the total lack of traffic congestion reduce your daily stress?
★ Have you ever been stuck in a massive traffic jam?
If you sold your car, would you save enough money to pay for a luxury vacation every year?
★ Have you ever paid too much for a car repair or regular maintenance?
If you didn't have a driver's license, would you lose your sense of personal autonomy?
★ What is the most scenic road trip you have ever taken?
If everyone used carpooling services, would our collective carbon footprint decrease dramatically?
★ Do you try to walk more to reduce your emissions?
If your office were 15 miles away, would you still try to walk or cycle your morning commute?
★ How far is your daily commute, and how do you travel?
If the walkability of suburban areas were higher, would people buy fewer vehicles?
★ Can you easily walk to local shops from your house?
If you bought a brand-new car today, would you feel frustrated by its rapid financial depreciation?
★ Do you think buying a brand-new car is a smart decision?

🟢 Round 2 : My Life Without Private Cars▲
Speculative Adaptation
If personal cars were completely banned in city centers, how would communities redesign local urban mobility and transit systems?
★ What would be your primary method of transport in a car-free city?
Without any cars to plan for, how could urban planners adapt city infrastructure to support vibrant pedestrian life?
★ Do you think you would walk 10,000 steps a day without a car?
If private vehicles did not exist, how would local authorities adapt public transport to handle the massive passenger load?
★ What is the best public transport system you have experienced?
In a car-free city, would road congestion completely disappear, or would it simply shift to bike lanes and sidewalks?
★ Did you play in the street when you were a child?
If families didn't have to pay for car maintenance, what alternative local services or experiences would they spend their savings on?
★ What would you buy with the money saved from fuel and maintenance?
Without a private vehicle, would you lose your sense of personal autonomy, or would you find new ways to stay independent?
★ What would you do with a garage if you had no car?
In a fully car-free society, how would the commercial logistics network adapt to lower its collective carbon footprint?
★ Do you feel safe riding a bicycle in your city?
Without roads dominating our landscape, would the daily commute to work become a highly active and social journey?
★ What is the hardest place to reach that you have visited?
If streets were turned into parks, how would high walkability change the way neighbors communicate and support each other?
★ What was your favorite family trip when you were young?
Since citizens wouldn't suffer from the financial depreciation of vehicles, how would they invest their capital instead?
★ Would you ride in a driverless flying taxi?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲

The public transport is cheap → Public transport is cheap (General noun doesn't need 'the')
