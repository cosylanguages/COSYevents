Theme

Work-Life Balance, Holidays, and Well-being

Holidays and vacations are traditionally viewed as essential for rest and mental recovery, yet many modern professionals struggle to completely disconnect from work. This session compares a life enriched by regular vacations with a life driven by constant, uninterrupted productivity. We will discuss the psychological costs of never taking a break, and how changing our resting habits can change our entire life perspective.

⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: Holidays & Vacations

Presence (With)

• Essential psychological decompression, burnout prevention, and rest.
• Opportunity for travel, cultural enrichment, and family bonding.
• Potential pile-up of work duties and high travel financial costs.

Absence (Without)

• High uninterrupted momentum and continuous task progression.
• Absence of post-vacation workplace stress and planning logistics.
• Severe risk of chronic fatigue, physical exhaustion, and low creativity.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Disconnect
break the connection of; detach oneself from work or digital devices.
It is incredibly hard to completely disconnect from office emails during a summer holiday.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • When you have a week of holidays, do you travel to a busy tourist destination, or do you stay at home to sleep?
  • Do you feel like you are wasting your time when you are doing absolutely nothing?
🔵 Round 1 : My Life With Holidays▲
Narrative & Necessity
Why is it nearly impossible to completely disconnect from office stress unless you physically leave your city?
★ Have you ever spent a full week without checking any work emails or messages?
Why can't most people stay healthy unless they take regular, quiet, and restorative weekend trips?
★ What is the most restorative holiday you have ever taken?
Can someone truly enjoy leisure activities unless they have designated, uninterrupted holiday time?
★ What is your absolute favorite way to spend a completely free Sunday?
Is it inevitable that a modern professional will fall into severe burnout unless they take annual leave?
★ What are the first warning signs of burnout that you notice in yourself?
Do you agree that you cannot maintain high long-term productivity at work unless you take regular vacations?
★ Have you ever felt more productive at work immediately after returning from a vacation?
Can a tired mind completely rejuvenate unless it is given several consecutive days of absolute rest?
★ Do you prefer active sports or lying by the pool when you want to relax?
Why do employees feel a constant obligation to check work emails unless their employer enforces a strict 'offline' policy?
★ Have you ever had to answer an urgent work call while resting on a beach?
Why are professionals unable to rest without a deep sense of guilt unless they know their entire team is also on holiday?
★ Have you ever felt guilty about leaving your coworkers with your work while on vacation?
Is working non-stop without holidays extremely detrimental to your health, even if you enjoy your job?
★ Do you believe that travel is the best way to invest your personal savings?
Can a person have a good state of overall well-being unless they regularly take vacations from their daily duties?
★ What is the most beautiful sunrise you have ever watched while on holiday?

🌴
The Rested Life
🟢 Round 2 : My Life Without Holidays▲
Speculative Adaptation
If holidays were never invented, what daily rituals would people create instead to completely disconnect from work?
★ Have you ever gone a very long time without a holiday, and how did you adapt?
Without vacations, how would families celebrate important moments and find restorative quiet time together?
★ Where do you usually get your most creative ideas-during work or during rest?
Without any weekends or annual leave, what new forms of leisure and play would emerge in local neighborhoods?
★ Do you practice mindfulness, meditation, or any form of daily mental rest?
Without holidays, what would employers have to do to prevent employees from falling into severe burnout?
★ Do you post a lot of photos during your holidays, or do you stay offline?
If holidays disappeared, how would society reorganize work hours to maintain a high level of national productivity?
★ Have you ever used work or a busy schedule to avoid dealing with a personal issue?
Without the possibility of travel, where would people find adventure to completely rejuvenate their minds?
★ What is one small change you could make to your daily routine to feel less tired?
Without any legal obligation to provide time off, what incentives would businesses use to attract talented workers?
★ Have you ever worked for an employer who made it difficult for you to take time off?
In a holiday-free world, would people invent a brand-new social custom to help them rest without feeling any guilt?
★ Do you feel guilty when you spend a whole day doing absolutely nothing productive?
If travel were completely banned, would a life restricted to our local cities be extremely detrimental to our personal development?
★ If you won the lottery, would you stop working completely and travel forever?
Without any holidays to look forward to, what daily hobbies or habits would we rely on to protect our well-being?
★ What is the genuine purpose of a holiday for you?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I am not comfortable to disconnect from work. → I am not comfortable disconnecting from work. (Gerund construction: the adjective phrase 'comfortable' is followed by a gerund 'disconnecting'.)
We discussed about holidays. → We discussed holidays. (Transitive verb: do not use the preposition 'about' after the verb 'discuss'.)
