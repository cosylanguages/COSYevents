life_ledger:
  title: '⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: Diversity Life'
  with_title: Presence (With)
  with_content: • Immense exposure to varied worldviews, gastronomies, and languages.<br>• Enhanced flexibility, problem-solving, and tolerance in community spaces.<br>• Potential cultural friction, misunderstandings, and integration challenges.
  without_title: Absence (Without)
  without_content: • Predictable, stable social norms and high cultural homogeneity.<br>• Simplified community organization and ease of local communication.<br>• Risk of creative stagnation, narrow worldviews, and echo-chamber thinking.
vocabulary:
  - word: Diversity ≠ Uniformity
    definition: including people from a range of backgrounds ≠ the state of being entirely the same.
    example: The school celebrated its cultural diversity, rejecting any form of forced uniformity.

  instruction: ''

round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : Life With'
  badge: Questions
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: How would diversity influence your daily life if you regularly interacted with people from many cultures, languages, and backgrounds?
      personal: ★ What is the most interesting cultural difference you have ever experienced?
    - main: Would greater exposure to different traditions make people more curious, adventurous, or open-minded?
      personal: ★ What new tradition or lifestyle would you like to experience?
    - main: How could having access to many different perspectives help society solve future problems that seem impossible today?
      personal: ★ Has someone ever changed your perspective on an important issue?
    - main: Would growing up in a highly diverse society improve people's adaptability when technology, jobs, and lifestyles change rapidly?
      personal: ★ How adaptable do you think you are?
    - main: Could occasional misunderstandings actually become valuable opportunities to learn about other people?
      personal: ★ Have you ever learned something useful from a misunderstanding?
    - main: How might an extremely inclusive society look in 100 years if people from every background felt equally welcome?
      personal: ★ What makes you feel included in a group?
    - main: How could exposure to many cultures strengthen rather than weaken a person's identity?
      personal: ★ Which part of your identity is most important to you?
    - main: Would greater diversity eventually reduce harmful stereotypes, or would people simply create new ones?
      personal: ★ What stereotype about your country annoys you most?
    - main: In an increasingly globalized world, could diversity become one of the most valuable skills a person can have?
      personal: ★ What international skill would you like to develop?
    - main: Could future cultural exchange lead to completely new forms of music, food, art, or even lifestyles that do not exist today?
      personal: ★ Which cultures would you love to see mixed together?

round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : Life Without'
  badge: Dilemmas
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: If there were no linguistic diversity and everyone spoke exactly the same language, what creative alternatives might people invent to express individuality?
      personal: ★ Would you feel limited or relieved if you could only ever speak one universal language?
    - main: Without exposure to different cultures, would people become more curious about space, virtual worlds, or fictional civilizations instead?
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt deeply fascinated by a fictional universe or video game culture?
    - main: If everyone shared the same perspective, would society become more peaceful-or would people invent new reasons to disagree?
      personal: ★ Do you enjoy healthy debates with people who hold completely opposite views from yours?
    - main: Without cultural differences, would people lose some adaptability, forcing schools to create artificial challenges and simulations to train it?
      personal: ★ What is the hardest unfamiliar situation you've ever had to adapt to in your life?
    - main: If language and culture were identical everywhere, how would the absence of any <strong>misunderstanding</strong> affect our motivation to truly listen and adapt to others?
      personal: ★ Have you ever had a frustrating or funny misunderstanding with an AI assistant like Siri or ChatGPT?
    - main: If society became completely inclusive because everyone was essentially the same, would people start dividing themselves according to personality types, hobbies, or even genetic traits?
      personal: ★ Which of your hobbies or interests makes you feel most connected to a specific subculture?
    - main: Without cultural or linguistic differences shaping our identity, what would people use to define themselves? Their memories? Their online avatars? Their achievements?
      personal: ★ If you had to describe your core identity in three words, without using your nationality or language, what would they be?
    - main: If cultural <strong>stereotypes</strong> disappeared completely, would humanity finally achieve harmony, or would we simply invent new prejudices based on professions or lifestyle choices?
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt misjudged or stereotyped based on your profession or a hobby?
    - main: In a fully globalized world with one culture, could local traditions survive as hobbies, historical reenactments, or premium experiences similar to museums?
      personal: ★ Is there a local tradition or family custom that you feel personally responsible for preserving?
    - main: If there were no international cultural exchange because everyone already shared the same culture, where would innovation come from? Artificial cultures designed by AI? Colonies on Mars? Entirely virtual civilizations?
      personal: ★ Would you be willing to move to a virtual or off-world colony if it offered a completely unique culture?
