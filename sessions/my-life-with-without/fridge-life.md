life_ledger:
  title: '⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: Fridge Life'
  with_title: Presence (With)
  with_content: • Unprecedented food safety, preservation, and reduction of bacterial spoilage.<br>• Ability to bulk-buy and store fresh produce for multiple weeks.<br>• Tendency to over-purchase, leading to major domestic food waste.
  without_title: Absence (Without)
  without_content: • Forced daily shopping driving high reliance on local fresh markets.<br>• Mastery of traditional preservation methods (salting, pickling, fermenting).<br>• High vulnerability of fresh ingredients to immediate seasonal spoiling.

round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : My Life With a Fridge'
  badge: Narrative &amp; Necessity
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: While a fridge allows us to <strong>preserve</strong> food for weeks, does it also make us disconnected from the natural seasons?
      personal: ★ What foods do you always try to preserve in your fridge?
    - main: Perishable ingredients start to <strong>spoil</strong> immediately, whereas cold storage slows bacterial decay; is artificial cold therefore an absolute requirement for urban survival?
      personal: ★ Which perishable food would you miss the most if you couldn't keep it cold?
    - main: While proper food <strong>storage</strong> saves domestic planning time, does it also encourage people to over-purchase and over-consume?
      personal: ★ Do you think your fridge helps you save money?
    - main: A refrigerator maintains the <strong>freshness</strong> of ingredients throughout the week, on the other hand, does it lower the nutritional value of what we eat compared to fresh-picked food?
      personal: ★ Which foods taste much better when they stay fresh?
    - main: While keeping food cold reduces daily kitchen <strong>waste</strong>, on the other hand, do stocked shelves make us blind to our global footprint?
      personal: ★ Have you ever managed to avoid waste thanks to your fridge?
    - main: Our parents used to throw away excess food, whereas modern busy professionals rely heavily on cold <strong>leftovers</strong> to survive the workweek; is this a healthy shift?
      personal: ★ How often do you eat leftovers instead of cooking again?
    - main: While controlling the exact <strong>temperature</strong> of our larders is a modern standard, on the other hand, does it make us vulnerable to sudden power grid failures?
      personal: ★ What everyday convenience would disappear without your fridge?
    - main: Is the refrigerator a genuine daily <strong>convenience</strong>, or does it lock us into a cycle of massive weekly supermarket expenditures?
      personal: ★ How often do you stock up on food?
    - main: While some prefer to stock up, others are forced to <strong>shop daily</strong> at local markets; which habit fosters a better connection to local farmers?
      personal: ★ Have you ever tried preserving food yourself?
    - main: While the refrigerator is a pillar of domestic food <strong>hygiene</strong>, does it also make our immune systems weaker by protecting us too much from natural bacteria?
      personal: ★ Could you imagine your current lifestyle without owning one?

round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : My Life Without a Fridge'
  badge: Speculative Adaptation
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: If domestic refrigerators had never existed, what traditional methods would people rely on to <strong>preserve</strong> seasonal produce in city apartments?
      personal: ★ Have you ever eaten fermented, salted, or dried food that you absolutely loved?
    - main: Without refrigeration, many fresh ingredients would quickly <strong>spoil</strong> on shelves. Which new dietary habits or local dishes would emerge to adapt to this?
      personal: ★ What is one perishable food item you buy every week that you would miss the most?
    - main: If home food <strong>storage</strong> were limited to a simple dry pantry, how would your daily cooking routine and work hours adapt?
      personal: ★ Do you enjoy the process of preparing food every day, or do you prefer quick meal-prep?
    - main: Without coolers, how would travelers maintain the <strong>freshness</strong> of their meals during long summer road trips?
      personal: ★ What is your go-to non-perishable snack when you travel long distances?
    - main: Without cold storage, how would commercial restaurants manage their inventory to avoid massive daily food <strong>waste</strong>?
      personal: ★ Do you hate throwing away food, or are you comfortable discarding leftovers after a few days?
    - main: In a world without cold storage, would we see a massive revival of local neighborhood vendors instead of eating cooked <strong>leftovers</strong>?
      personal: ★ How often do you visit your local grocery store or market in a typical week?
    - main: If we couldn't control the <strong>temperature</strong> of our larders, would people become more mindful menu planners or would they eat less variety?
      personal: ★ Are you a meticulous planner when it comes to your weekly meals, or do you cook on a whim?
    - main: If we had to forfeit the modern <strong>convenience</strong> of a fridge, what alternative, low-tech cooling methods would we develop?
      personal: ★ What household appliance, other than the fridge, could you absolutely not live without?
    - main: If home appliances were a rare luxury, would societies adapt by forcing everyone to <strong>shop daily</strong> for fresh ingredients in local town squares?
      personal: ★ Would you enjoy a lifestyle where you had to visit local food vendors every single morning?
    - main: If a sudden power crisis eliminated all refrigeration tomorrow, how would we manage public health and food <strong>hygiene</strong> in densely populated cities?
      personal: ★ How do you think you would handle the first 48 hours of a sudden global refrigeration failure?
mistakes:
  - wrong: I put the leftovers in the fridge since 3 days
    right: I've had the leftovers in the fridge for 3 days
    note: (Present Perfect)
  - wrong: I suggest to buy a bigger fridge
    right: I suggest buying a bigger fridge
    note: (Suggest + -ing)
  - wrong: The food have a good freshness
    right: The food is fresh / stays fresh
    note: (Freshness as a state)
  - wrong: There is many foods in the fridge
    right: There is a lot of food in the fridge
    note: (Uncountable food)
