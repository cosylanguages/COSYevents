life_ledger:
  title: '⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: Private Cars (B2)'
  with_title: Presence (With)
  with_content: • High personal sovereignty, convenience, and status symbol advantages.<br>• Unmatched point-to-point mobility across poorly connected suburban hubs.<br>• Extreme financial depreciation, high insurance, and municipal gridlock stress.
  without_title: Absence (Without)
  without_content: • Unprecedented municipal pedestrianization, clean air, and green spaces.<br>• Freedom from vehicle devaluation, fuel pricing volatility, and parking limits.<br>• Critical dependence on municipal transit system efficiency and route limits.
vocabulary:
  - word: Automotive dominance
    definition: the absolute dominance of cars in determining city layouts and transportation options.
    example: Cities are trying to break free from decades of automotive dominance by investing in bike lanes.

  title: 'Round 2 : My Life Without Private Cars'

  items:
    - main: If we completely eliminated <strong>automotive dominance</strong>, how would communities redesign physical metropolitan maps and local logistics?

      personal: ★ How would a car-free neighborhood change how children spend their weekends?
    - main: If everyone used the public <strong>transit system</strong>, what new civic funding models would emerge to pay for infrastructure upkeep?
      personal: ★ Do you think public transport should be completely free, funded by tax?

    right: Automotive dominance is very <strong>prevalent / pervasive</strong>.
    note: (Omit 'the' for general abstract concepts; also select more precise adjectives like 'prevalent'.)
