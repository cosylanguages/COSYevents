life_ledger:
  title: '⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: Pets Life'
  with_title: Presence (With)
  with_content: • Constant emotional support, unconditional love, and companionship.<br>• Forced daily physical routine (dog walking) and home vibrancy.<br>• High recurring expenses (veterinarian, food) and restricted travel freedom.
  without_title: Absence (Without)
  without_content: • Complete travel flexibility and zero domestic cleaning overheads.<br>• Clean, silent home environment with absolute personal freedom.<br>• Potential lack of physical touch and higher risk of domestic isolation.
