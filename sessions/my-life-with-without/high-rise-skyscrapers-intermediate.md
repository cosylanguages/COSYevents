life_ledger:
  title: '⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: High-Rise Skyscrapers (B1)'
  with_title: Presence (With)
  with_content: • Spectacular panoramic city views and housing density.<br>• Convenient integrated amenities (elevators, waste pipes, parking floors).<br>• High risk of community isolation and lack of garden/ground green space.
  without_title: Absence (Without)
  without_content: • Cozy horizontal neighborhoods with superior local community connections.<br>• Absolute ease of walking directly onto the grass or streets without lifts.<br>• Uncontrolled suburban sprawl and long, exhausting commutes to city centers.

  instruction: ''

round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : My Life With Skyscrapers'
  badge: Narrative &amp; Necessity
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: Why do giant corporations prefer vertical architecture? Would you rather work in an iconic glass <strong>skyscraper</strong> or a cozy brick building?
      personal: ★ Have you ever visited the top floor of a famous skyscraper?
    - main: Would you rather buy a small <strong>high-rise</strong> studio apartment with a view or a spacious ground-floor townhouse?
      personal: ★ What floor do you currently live on, and do you like it?
    - main: Would you rather have a <strong>panoramic</strong> city view with high wind noise or a ground-floor patio with street noise?
      personal: ★ What is the most beautiful urban view you have ever seen?
    - main: Does extreme vertical population <strong>density</strong> force you to behave more politely to neighbors?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer bustling urban environments or quiet, empty spaces?
    - main: Why do skyscraper residents struggle with social <strong>isolation</strong>? Would you rather have 1,000 neighbors or live in a quiet forest?
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt lonely in a large city?
    - main: Would you rather build a strong <strong>connection</strong> with your floor neighbors or with the local shopkeepers downstairs?
      personal: ★ How do you build good relationships with people in your neighborhood?
    - main: Does rapid <strong>urbanization</strong> require that cities build vertical towers to prevent massive agricultural loss?
      personal: ★ Has your home city changed much since you were a child?
    - main: Would you rather manage municipal water <strong>infrastructure</strong> for a vertical tower or for 100 separate houses?
      personal: ★ Have you ever experienced a water or power outage in a high-rise building?
    - main: Why is it nearly impossible to develop a cooperative neighborhood <strong>community</strong> inside a modern luxury skyscraper?
      personal: ★ Do you know your neighbors' names in your current building?
    - main: Would you rather find emotional <strong>tranquility</strong> on a high-rise rooftop or by a quiet forest lake?
      personal: ★ How do you find a quiet, tranquil moment during a busy work week?

round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : My Life Without Skyscrapers'
  badge: Speculative Adaptation
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: If towering <strong>skyscrapers</strong> were banned, how would urban planners reorganize city centers to prevent overcrowding?
      personal: ★ Would you be willing to pay higher rent to live in a low-rise house?
    - main: Without any vertical <strong>high-rise</strong> blocks, how would metropolises protect nearby farmlands from horizontal sprawl?
      personal: ★ How would a longer commute change your lifestyle?
    - main: If there were no top floors to visit, what would replace the <strong>panoramic</strong> observation deck as the primary tourist attraction of a city?
      personal: ★ Do you feel happier when you can step directly onto green grass?
    - main: Without high population <strong>density</strong>, what new local community networks would people invent to maintain social connections?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer protected forests or spacious suburbs?
    - main: If vertical living disappeared, would we completely cure the social <strong>isolation</strong> typical of massive metropolises?
      personal: ★ What is your favorite time of day to look at the city sky?
    - main: If physical buildings were kept low, would we naturally restore a deep, empathetic <strong>connection</strong> among neighbors?
      personal: ★ What local community group or activity would you enjoy joining?
    - main: If <strong>urbanization</strong> shifted back to flat, rural-style layouts, what speculative industries would collapse or grow?
      personal: ★ Would you ride a bicycle to work if there were no high-speed highways?
    - main: Without vertical pipes and lifts, would cities find it easier to upgrade their general utility <strong>infrastructure</strong>?
      personal: ★ Do you feel more productive working in a home office?
    - main: Without vertical blocks, what local squares or physical hubs would the <strong>community</strong> design to gather and socialize?
      personal: ★ Does crowded public transport make you feel highly stressed?
    - main: If we lived closer to the natural soil, how would our personal mental <strong>tranquility</strong> be affected?
      personal: ★ Do you think small town residents are friendlier than big city residents?
