Pets have evolved from working animals to integral members of the modern family, providing unique emotional support and companionship. This session explores the deep psychological bond between humans and their animal friends and the responsibilities that come with pet ownership. We will imagine how our lives and mental well-being might differ without the presence of pets and what alternative forms of companionship could exist.

⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: Pets Life

Presence (With)

• Constant emotional support, unconditional love, and companionship.
• Forced daily physical routine (dog walking) and home vibrancy.
• High recurring expenses (veterinarian, food) and restricted travel freedom.

Absence (Without)

• Complete travel flexibility and zero domestic cleaning overheads.
• Clean, silent home environment with absolute personal freedom.
• Potential lack of physical touch and higher risk of domestic isolation.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Emotional support
help that makes someone feel calmer or happier emotionally.
Pets often give emotional support when people feel stressed.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Are you a dog person, a cat person, or neither?
  • What is the first animal you ever touched?
🔵 Round 1 : My Life With Pets▲
Narrative & Necessity
Why do many people feel they must have animals around them to receive authentic, non-judgmental emotional support?
★ Do you prefer comfort or solutions when you are upset?
Do isolated people have to rely on pet companionship because human friendships are too temporary?
★ What makes a relationship feel “stable” for you?
Why must parents teach their children the daily responsibility of feeding an animal at a young age?
★ What habit has changed your personality the most?
Does an active dog-owner have to exercise regularly, thus curing their own loneliness and physical stagnation?
★ Do you feel your routines control you or you control them?
Do you agree that a pet owner must adhere strictly to their animal's morning feeding routine?
★ Is emotional expression easier or harder for you?
Why do people feel they don't have to hide their pure affection when interacting with dogs or cats?
★ Do you value stories or outcomes more in life decisions?
Why must a rescue animal's adoption process be strictly supervised to ensure its safety?
★ When do you notice your own patience breaking?
Does a handler have to show extreme empathy during the training of an energetic, stubborn puppy?
★ Have you ever experienced a random social connection?
If a child has a pet allergy, does the family have to completely ban animals from the household?
★ When do you prefer silence over talking?
Why must modern municipalities prioritize street animal welfare as a matter of public ethical health?
★ What turns a place into a real home for you?

🟢 Round 2 : My Life Without Pets▲
Speculative Adaptation
If emotional support from companion animals never existed, what alternative coping mechanisms would humans develop to manage stress?
★ What or who is your primary source of comfort when you are going through a difficult time?
Without any animal-based companionship, how would homebound individuals adapt to fulfill their natural need for connection?
★ Do you feel that a person can be completely happy and fulfilled without any close human or animal friends?
Without pets to nurture, how would families teach young children empathy and daily responsibility?
★ What daily task or chore in your life gives you the strongest sense of responsibility and purpose?
Without our history of animal allies, would humanity have succumbed to deeper, historic levels of systemic loneliness?
★ How do you structure your mornings to ensure you start the day with positive energy?
If animal-related obligations disappeared, how differently would active owners structure their morning routine?
★ Are you comfortable expressing affection through physical gestures like hugs or high-fives?
If showing physical affection to animals was culturally absent, would humans become physically closer to each other?
★ Have you ever volunteered for or donated to a charity that you care deeply about?
If the adoption of rescue animals was banned, what new local community habits would emerge to manage shelter surpluses?
★ How do you train yourself to develop a new good habit or break an old bad one?
Without our history of training beasts of burden, would agricultural and industrial history have moved much slower?
★ Do you ever start conversations with strangers on the street or in public spaces?
If we magically cured every pet allergy worldwide overnight, how would urban housing infrastructure adapt?
★ What is your favorite healthy way to relieve stress after a long, exhausting week?
If cities prioritized animal welfare as highly as human health, how would the physical design of neighborhoods transform?
★ Do you believe that human well-being is closely connected to the preservation of natural wildlife?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I've been looking for a pet since a long time → I've been looking for a pet for a long time (For vs Since)
It makes me feeling happy → It makes me feel happy (Make + infinitive)
If I would have a dog, I would be happy → If I had a dog, I would be happy (Second conditional)
He was barking me → He was barking at me (Preposition "at")
