life_ledger:
  title: '⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: High-Rise Skyscrapers (B2)'
  with_title: Presence (With)
  with_content: • Spectacular metropolitan verticality, scenic views, and high land-use efficiency.<br>• Every desired physical amenity (gyms, pools, security desks) under one roof.<br>• Psychological alienation and lower general community cohesion.
  without_title: Absence (Without)
  without_content: • Exceptional social cohesion and horizontal neighborhood community warmth.<br>• Direct access to outdoor grounds and gardens without high elevator wait times.<br>• Unchecked horizontal urban sprawl and massive, inefficient infrastructure costs.

    definition: the state of being vertical or towering in height.
    example: The verticality of Manhattan forces residents to live high above the noisy streets.

round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : My Life With Skyscrapers'
  badge: Narrative &amp; Necessity
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: If city planners had not been embracing extreme <strong>verticality</strong>, would horizontal suburban sprawl have destroyed all surrounding woodlands?
      personal: ★ What is the most impressive observation deck you have ever visited?
    - main: If engineers had not achieved this landmark <strong>architectural feat</strong>, would metropolises have struggled to establish their economic presence?
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt proud of an architectural landmark in your city?
    - main: If developers had been constructing lower buildings, would urban <strong>congestion</strong> around office blocks have been far less intense?
      personal: ★ How do you handle stressful delays during your morning commute?
    - main: If massive tower complexes had not been isolating residents, would the feeling of social <strong>alienation</strong> be less common in modern metropolises?
      personal: ★ Have you ever lived in a place where you felt completely disconnected from those around you?
    - main: If physical communities had been focusing on ground-level interactions, would we have built superior social <strong>cohesion</strong>?
      personal: ★ How do you connect with neighbors when there is no shared physical yard?
    - main: If your <strong>metropolitan</strong> area had restricted building heights decades ago, would you still have moved there?
      personal: ★ Would you choose to live in a vertical city center if budget were not an issue?
    - main: If local authorities had been permitting horizontal housing, would uncontrolled <strong>sprawl</strong> have consumed the entire agricultural preserve?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer dense city cores or quiet suburban sprawl?
    - main: If luxury high-rises had not been integrating every modern <strong>amenity</strong>, would vertical living still be attractive?
      personal: ★ What is the one building amenity you could not live without?
    - main: If you had been living on the noisy ground floor, would you have lost your sense of absolute <strong>serenity</strong>?
      personal: ★ What is your favorite ritual for winding down after a busy day?
    - main: If high-speed lifts had not been facilitating rapid <strong>ascent</strong>, would vertical towers have become completely obsolete?
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt dizzy or disoriented during a fast elevator ride?

round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : My Life Without Skyscrapers'
  badge: Speculative Adaptation
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: If towering <strong>verticality</strong> was banned worldwide, how would metropolitan city planners adapt to house growing urban populations?
      personal: ★ Would you support banning buildings above five stories to preserve old city layouts?
    - main: Without any monumental <strong>architectural feat</strong> of height, how would cities project their global aesthetic prestige?
      personal: ★ What historical flat city do you find most visually charming?
    - main: If flat horizontal sprawl replaced vertical towers, how would transit authorities adapt to manage the massive <strong>congestion</strong>?
      personal: ★ How far would you be willing to travel to reach your office every day?
    - main: Without physical high-rises, would we completely eliminate the psychological social <strong>alienation</strong> typical of dense cities?
      personal: ★ Do you find small towns more welcoming than towering metropolises?
    - main: Does flat, ground-level living naturally restore authentic, empathetic neighborhood <strong>cohesion</strong>?
      personal: ★ Do you think low-rise buildings encourage children to play outside more?
    - main: If <strong>metropolitan</strong> areas became completely flat, would they lose their centralized economic productivity?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer working in a cozy, flat office or a vertical corporate tower?
    - main: If horizontal <strong>sprawl</strong> was the only layout choice, what new environmental protections would society invent to protect farmlands?
      personal: ★ How important is the preservation of nearby natural spaces to you?
    - main: Without private building <strong>amenities</strong>, would citizens invest more effort in developing outstanding public parks?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer private pools or swimming in local lakes and oceans?
    - main: If we all lived on the ground floor with zero sky views, would we miss the absolute <strong>serenity</strong> of physical height?
      personal: ★ Does having a view of the street make you feel more connected to the city?
    - main: Can a modern civilization survive and travel without relying on the rapid elevator <strong>ascent</strong>?
      personal: ★ Do you always take the elevator, or do you try to climb the stairs for exercise?
mistakes:
  - wrong: This verticality is <u>more superior</u> than sprawl.
    right: This verticality is <strong>superior to</strong> sprawl.
    note: '(Double comparative error: ''superior'' is already comparative. Also, ''superior'' is followed by ''to'', not ''than''.)'
  - wrong: We discussed <u>about</u> this architectural feat.
    right: We discussed this architectural feat / had a discussion <strong>about</strong> this feat.
    note: ('Discuss' is a transitive verb that directly takes an object without a preposition.)
