This session compares a life enriched by regular vacations with a life driven by constant, uninterrupted productivity. We will discuss the psychological costs of never taking a break, and how changing our resting habits can change our entire life perspective.</p>

  title: '⚖️ Dual-Perspective Balance Sheet: Holidays & Vacations'
  with_title: Presence (With)
  with_content: • Essential psychological decompression, burnout prevention, and rest.<br>• Opportunity for travel, cultural enrichment, and family bonding.<br>• Potential pile-up of work duties and high travel financial costs.
  without_title: Absence (Without)
  without_content: • High uninterrupted momentum and continuous task progression.<br>• Absence of post-vacation workplace stress and planning logistics.<br>• Severe risk of chronic fatigue, physical exhaustion, and low creativity.

round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : My Life With Holidays'
  badge: Narrative &amp; Necessity
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: Why is it nearly impossible to completely <strong>disconnect</strong> from office stress <strong>unless</strong> you physically leave your city?
      personal: ★ Have you ever spent a full week without checking any work emails or messages?
    - main: Why can't most people stay healthy <strong>unless</strong> they take regular, quiet, and <strong>restorative</strong> weekend trips?
      personal: ★ What is the most restorative holiday you have ever taken?
    - main: Can someone truly enjoy <strong>leisure</strong> activities <strong>unless</strong> they have designated, uninterrupted holiday time?
      personal: ★ What is your absolute favorite way to spend a completely free Sunday?
    - main: Is it inevitable that a modern professional will fall into severe <strong>burnout</strong> <strong>unless</strong> they take annual leave?
      personal: ★ What are the first warning signs of burnout that you notice in yourself?
    - main: Do you agree that you cannot maintain high long-term <strong>productivity</strong> at work <strong>unless</strong> you take regular vacations?
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt more productive at work immediately after returning from a vacation?
    - main: Can a tired mind completely <strong>rejuvenate</strong> <strong>unless</strong> it is given several consecutive days of absolute rest?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer active sports or lying by the pool when you want to relax?
    - main: Why do employees feel a constant <strong>obligation</strong> to check work emails <strong>unless</strong> their employer enforces a strict 'offline' policy?
      personal: ★ Have you ever had to answer an urgent work call while resting on a beach?
    - main: Why are professionals unable to rest without a deep sense of <strong>guilt</strong> <strong>unless</strong> they know their entire team is also on holiday?
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt guilty about leaving your coworkers with your work while on vacation?
    - main: Is working non-stop without holidays extremely <strong>detrimental</strong> to your health, even if you enjoy your job?
      personal: ★ Do you believe that travel is the best way to invest your personal savings?
    - main: Can a person have a good state of overall <strong>well-being</strong> <strong>unless</strong> they regularly take vacations from their daily duties?
      personal: ★ What is the most beautiful sunrise you have ever watched while on holiday?

  title: 'Round 2 : My Life Without Holidays'

    - main: If holidays were never invented, what daily rituals would people create instead to completely <strong>disconnect</strong> from work?

    - main: Without holidays, what would employers have to do to prevent employees from falling into severe <strong>burnout</strong>?

    - main: If holidays disappeared, how would society reorganize work hours to maintain a high level of national <strong>productivity</strong>?
      personal: ★ Have you ever used work or a busy schedule to avoid dealing with a personal issue?
    - main: Without the possibility of travel, where would people find adventure to completely <strong>rejuvenate</strong> their minds?
