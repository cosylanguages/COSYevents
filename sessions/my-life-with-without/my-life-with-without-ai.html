A comparative B1 session on living with vs without artificial intelligence. Explore efficiency, cognitive skills, digital dependency, and human originality.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Automation ≠ manual labor
the use of automatic equipment in a process.
AI automation speeds up administrative paperwork.
Efficiency ≠ delay
the state or quality of being efficient.
Using AI tools increases daily work efficiency.
Creativity ≠ routine
the use of imagination or original ideas.
Relying on AI summaries can limit human creativity.
Dependency ≠ self-reliance
the state of relying on someone or something for support.
Overusing search assistants creates digital dependency.
Outsource ≠ perform
to obtain goods or services from an outside supplier.
We should not outsource critical thinking to machines.
Algorithm ≠ human thought
a process or set of rules followed by a computer.
Recommender algorithms shape our online habits.
Originality ≠ imitation
the ability to think independently and creatively.
Originality remains a uniquely human characteristic.
Productivity ≠ stagnation
the effectiveness of productive effort.
AI assistants boost student writing productivity.
Cognitive ≠ physical
relating to mental processes of knowledge and memory.
Mental math exercises protect our cognitive agility.
Unplug ≠ connect
to disconnect an electrical device or take a screen break.
Taking time to unplug restores mental focus.

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Use so... that and because of to explain cause and result (e.g., AI tools are so fast that people rely on them daily).

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
AI makes me to work faster. → AI makes me work faster. (Causative verb 'make' takes a bare infinitive: 'make me work'.)
I rely in AI tools. → I rely on AI tools. (Preposition rule: 'rely' collocates with 'on'.)
