Why Do I Spend More When I Earn More?

🌍 LANGUAGE : 🇬🇧 📊 LEVEL : Upper Intermediate (B2) 🎯 THEME : Relationship Column, Narrative Metaphors, Quirky Speculation

📰 PERSPECTIVE MIRROR

Urban Setting

A sleek espresso bar overlooking the skyscrapers of Wall Street

The Romantic Metaphor

Chasing a higher salary vs. Chasing a phantom standard of happiness

The Introspective Query

Why do we promise our future selves frugality, only to splurge the moment our wallets expand?

Linguistic Focus

Focus Adverbs & Economic Metaphors

📜 Read Column Transcript▼

I was sitting in a lively bistro in Rennes, watching a young student purchase a ten-euro organic double-shot flat white right after receiving her scholarship, while complaining about her rent. I took a sip of my own espresso, lit a Marlboro Red, and couldn't help but wonder about our endless dance with money. We swear that the next raise will be the one we save, the magic number that finally brings us peace. But the moment our income grows, our lifestyle expands to meet it. Do we genuinely want financial freedom, or is our spending simply a comfort blanket we buy to survive the daily grind of earning?

📖 Session Vocabulary

Lifestyle inflation
the tendency to increase one's spending as one's income increases.
Despite getting a major promotion, her savings stayed at zero due to lifestyle inflation.
Frugal ≠ extravagant
careful when using money or food ≠ spending much more than is necessary.
He tried to live a frugal lifestyle, but his extravagant taste in designer shoes ruined his budget.
Splurge
to spend a lot of money on buying luxury or unnecessary goods.
Every time she feels stressed, she tends to splurge on high-end beauty treatments.
Disposable income
income remaining after deduction of taxes and other mandatory charges.
A higher salary gave him more disposable income, which he promptly spent on fine dining.
Impulse buy
an unplanned decision to buy a product, made just before a purchase.
The supermarket registers are designed to make customers impulse buy candy and magazines.
Financial safety net
an emergency fund or resources that protect against unexpected financial loss.
Building a robust financial safety net requires disciplined monthly saving.
Earning capacity
the potential of an individual to earn salary or wages in the labor market.
Obtaining an advanced degree dramatically increased her long-term earning capacity.
Living beyond one's means
spending more money than one actually earns.
Using credit cards for luxury vacations is a classic example of living beyond one's means.
Budgeting
the process of creating a plan to spend your money.
Without strict budgeting, even millionaires can end up bankrupt.
Materialistic
excessively concerned with physical comforts and acquiring material possessions.
In a materialistic society, our worth is too often measured by what we own.

🔵 Round 1 : Column Analysis▲
Questions
Does getting a higher salary inevitably lead to lifestyle inflation, or is it merely a choice we make?
★ Have you ever noticed yourself spending more on daily luxuries right after your income went up?
Is it possible to remain truly frugal in a highly materialistic urban environment?
★ When was the last time you made a deliberate choice to be frugal instead of choosing comfort?
Why do we feel an intense psychological urge to splurge on luxury goods after a stressful work week?
★ What is your favorite guilty pleasure to splurge on when you want to treat yourself?
Does having more disposable income make us feel more secure, or does it simply make us more anxious about spending it?
★ How do you distribute your disposable income between savings, necessities, and fun?
Are we more vulnerable to making an impulse buy online late at night, or is it solely a retail trick?
★ Share the most ridiculous or useless impulse buy you have ever made.
Why do so many high-earning individuals fail to build a proper financial safety net?
★ Do you have a strict plan for building your emergency fund, or do you save whatever is left?
Does our happiness depend on our actual wealth, or is it precisely about our future earning capacity?
★ Do you feel optimistic about your professional earning capacity over the next five years?
Why is living beyond one's means so socially accepted and even encouraged on social media?
★ Have you ever felt pressured to spend more money just to fit in with a certain social circle?
Is strict monthly budgeting the death of fun, or is it particularly liberating?
★ Do you use budgeting apps, spreadsheets, or do you prefer to track everything in your head?
Can a deeply materialistic mindset ever be cured by financial stability, or will it merely escalate?
★ Do you consider yourself a materialistic person, or do you prioritize experiences over possessions?

🟢 Round 2 : Deep Speculation: Agree or Disagree?▲
Debate
In modern society, lifestyle inflation is unavoidable because our social status is directly tied to our level of consumption.
★ Do you think traditional societies had less pressure to show off wealth than we do today?
A frugal lifestyle was a survival necessity in the past, whereas today it is simply an aesthetic choice for the rich.
★ Would you find it easier to live a frugal life if everyone around you did the same?
The urge to splurge is a symptom of urban exhaustion rather than a genuine desire for physical objects.
★ Do you think people who live in rural areas feel less need to splurge than city dwellers?
An abundance of disposable income makes people less creative because they can solve any problem merely by paying for it.
★ Do you agree that some of your best memories were made when you had very little money?
The convenience of online 1-click shopping has turned every modern purchase into an automated impulse buy.
★ Do you think online shopping has made us more materialistic than physical department stores used to?
In the future, the concept of a financial safety net will disappear as AI and automation make job security obsolete.
★ Would you feel safe without a personal safety net if the government guaranteed a basic income?
Our earning capacity is the single most important factor in determining our choice of partner in modern dating.
★ Do you reckon that traditional relationships were built on more stable foundations than modern ones?
Most people are living beyond their means solely because credit card companies and banks prey on human vulnerability.
★ Have you ever had to borrow money from a friend or a bank to cover an extravagant purchase?
If children were taught the art of budgeting in elementary school, our consumer-driven economy would completely collapse.
★ Why do you think schools avoid teaching practical budgeting and financial management?
We live in the most materialistic era in human history, where even our relationships have been optimized like financial portfolios.
★ Do you worry that we are losing the ability to appreciate non-materialistic beauty and slow-paced connections?
