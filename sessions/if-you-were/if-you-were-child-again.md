round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : Personal Depth'
  badge: Narrative
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: What is the very first game or playground activity you would play if you became a <strong>carefree</strong> seven-year-old tomorrow?
      personal: ★ Who was your favorite childhood friend, and what did you play together?
    - main: If you had a whole afternoon with no chores or responsibilities, where would you choose to <strong>wander</strong>?
      personal: ★ Did you have a secret hiding spot as a child that no adult knew about?
    - main: How would your <strong>imaginative</strong> mind turn a simple cardboard box into an epic adventure?
      personal: ★ What was your favorite childhood toy or stuffed animal?
    - main: Do you remember what it felt like to be completely <strong>fascinated</strong> by something as simple as an ant hill or a puddle?
      personal: ★ Why do adults lose the ability to find wonder in tiny things?
    - main: What was a <strong>trivial</strong> childhood worry that felt like the absolute end of the world to you back then?
      personal: ★ Did you ever cry over something that you now find hilarious?
    - main: Would you want to keep your adult memories, or would you prefer to be completely <strong>naïve</strong> and <strong>innocent</strong> again?
      personal: ★ Is adult knowledge more of a gift or a heavy burden?
    - main: How would you spend your pocket money if you didn't have to worry about bills or savings?
      personal: ★ What sweet treat or toy would you buy first?
    - main: Do you think your relationship with your parents would improve if you were a child again, knowing what you know now?
      personal: ★ What childhood secret did you keep from your parents?
    - main: How would you react to being told when to go to bed and what to eat every single day?
      personal: ★ What was your most hated childhood rule?
    - main: If you could send one message from your childhood self to your current adult self, what would it say?
      personal: ★ Would your younger self be proud of the adult you have become?

round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : Speculative Analysis'
  badge: Projections
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: If adults spent one hour a day acting like a <strong>spontaneous</strong> child, global stress and anxiety levels would drop by half.
      personal: ★ Should corporate offices introduce mandatory 'play hours' for employees?
    - main: A child's view of the world is actually more accurate and truthful than an adult's view.
      personal: ★ Do we become less wise as we grow more educated and cynical?
    - main: We should let children stay <strong>naïve</strong> about global crises and politics for as long as possible.
      personal: ★ Is exposing kids to adult news too early a form of emotional harm?
    - main: The best way to <strong>reclaim</strong> our creativity is to throw away our phones and sit in boredom like we <strong>used to</strong> do.
      personal: ★ Did smartphones kill the art of childhood daydreaming?
    - main: Adults are deeply jealous of children because kids live in a state of constant <strong>unburdened</strong> presence.
      personal: ★ Is childhood truly the happiest time of a human life, or is that just nostalgia?
    - main: Modern toys and video games are too structured, destroying a child's natural <strong>imaginative</strong> instincts.
      personal: ★ Was playing with sticks and dirt better for brain development?
    - main: We should allow children to vote in local community decisions that affect their parks and schools.
      personal: ★ Are children capable of making sensible civic choices?
    - main: If you were a child again today, you would struggle to adapt to the highly scheduled, academic lifestyle of modern kids.
      personal: ★ Have we turned modern childhood into a stressful race for future resumes?
    - main: Being <strong>innocent</strong> is a luxury that only rich societies can afford for their children.
      personal: ★ Does survival force kids in struggling areas to grow up too fast?
    - main: The transition from childhood to adulthood is a slow process of losing our authentic self to social expectations.
      personal: ★ Can we ever truly recover the person we were at seven years old?

mistakes:
  - wrong: I <u>was believing</u> that Santa was real.
    right: I <strong>used to believe</strong> that Santa was real.
    note: '(Grammar: State verbs like ''believe'' shouldn''t be used in continuous tenses for past habits; use ''used to'' or past simple)'
  - wrong: I would <u>to spend</u> hours playing inside cardboard boxes.
    right: I would <strong>spend</strong> hours playing inside cardboard boxes.
    note: '(Grammar: Modal ''would'' for past routines takes a bare infinitive)'
