round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : Personal Depth'
  badge: Narrative
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: How would you design your very first day of school to <strong>inspire</strong> your students rather than intimidate them?
      personal: ★ What is the most creative classroom rule you would establish?
    - main: If your students started a small <strong>rebellion</strong> against a boring assignment, how would you handle the situation?
      personal: ★ Would you punish them to protect your authority, or reward their critical thinking?
    - main: How would you maintain a clear professional <strong>boundary</strong> when a student shares a deep, personal problem with you?
      personal: ★ Is it possible to be both an emotional support and an authority figure?
    - main: What is your strategy to keep your <strong>patience</strong> when dealing with a student who is constantly trying to disrupt the class?
      personal: ★ Have you ever lost your temper in a professional setting?
    - main: How would you manage the daily <strong>chaos</strong> of thirty energetic children without raising your voice?
      personal: '★ What is more powerful in a classroom: silence or volume?'
    - main: If your job became physically and mentally <strong>exhausting</strong>, how would you protect your passion for teaching?
      personal: ★ Why do so many young educators leave the profession after just a few years?
    - main: How would you evaluate a student's intelligence if you decided to ban all traditional, standardized testing?
      personal: ★ Can a simple conversation reveal more than a written exam?
    - main: How do you spark genuine <strong>curiosity</strong> in a student who has completely given up on learning?
      personal: ★ Is curiosity a natural talent, or is it a skill that must be trained?
    - main: In what ways would you try to <strong>empower</strong> quiet and shy students to speak up during class debates?
      personal: ★ Do modern schools unfairly reward extroverts while ignoring introverts?
    - main: Do you believe your life would feel more <strong>fulfilled</strong> if you spent it shaping the minds of the next generation?
      personal: ★ What is the legacy you would want your students to remember you for?

round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : Speculative Analysis'
  badge: Projections
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: If you were a teacher, your primary goal would be to <strong>empower</strong> students to question your own <strong>authority</strong>.
      personal: ★ Does true education require teaching children how to disobey?
    - main: The most successful classrooms are those where a controlled amount of <strong>chaos</strong> is allowed.
      personal: ★ Is strict order the enemy of creative breakthroughs?
    - main: Standardized grades do not measure a student's intelligence; they only measure their ability to conform.
      personal: ★ Should we abolish letter grades entirely in favor of qualitative portfolios?
    - main: A teacher's mental health is more important for student success than any modern curriculum or technology.
      personal: ★ Should school weeks be cut to four days to prevent teacher burnout?
    - main: If we want to <strong>inspire</strong> real learning, we must let students decide exactly what topics they want to study.
      personal: ★ Are mandatory subjects like advanced math or history a waste of childhood energy?
    - main: AI tools like ChatGPT will eventually replace teachers as the primary source of factual knowledge.
      personal: ★ Does this shift mean teachers will finally focus purely on human ethics and emotional growth?
    - main: A teacher who is loved by all their students is likely failing to teach them hard truths.
      personal: ★ Should a great educator be willing to be disliked by their class?
    - main: Parents are often the biggest obstacle to a child's education, causing constant <strong>friction</strong> for teachers.
      personal: ★ Should parents be banned from interfering with classroom methods?
    - main: The profession of teaching is so <strong>exhausting</strong> because society expects schools to fix every family and social problem.
      personal: ★ Have we put too much of a moral burden on our school systems?
    - main: The ultimate test of an educational system is whether its graduates retain their childhood <strong>curiosity</strong> as adults.
      personal: ★ Does modern schooling slowly extinguish our desire to learn?

mistakes:
  - wrong: You must <u>to maintain</u> boundaries with parents.
    right: You must <strong>maintain</strong> boundaries with parents.
    note: '(Grammar: Modal verbs take bare infinitives without ''to'')'
  - wrong: Teachers should be <u>allowed doing</u> that.
    right: Teachers should be <strong>allowed to do</strong> that.
    note: '(Collocation: ''be allowed'' takes a full infinitive ''to do'')'
