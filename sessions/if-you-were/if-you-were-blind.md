round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : Personal Depth'
  badge: Narrative
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: How would you <strong>navigate</strong> your city if you had to rely entirely on an <strong>acoustic</strong> map of echoes and footsteps?
      personal: ★ What is the most welcoming sound you would listen for in your neighborhood?
    - main: If you couldn't see your partner or friends, how would you cultivate true emotional <strong>intimacy</strong>?
      personal: ★ Does physical appearance actually get in the way of deep love?
    - main: How would you maintain your home's <strong>orientation</strong> and order if you couldn't visually inspect where items are placed?
      personal: ★ What would be your biggest frustration if a visitor moved a single chair?
    - main: Would you feel more <strong>vulnerable</strong> in public spaces, or would your trust in human <strong>compassion</strong> increase?
      personal: ★ Do you believe strangers are generally willing to guide someone in need?
    - main: How would you experience the <strong>sensation</strong> of a forest walk through your skin, nose, and ears rather than your eyes?
      personal: ★ Is a scent sometimes more vivid and beautiful than a view?
    - main: How would your sense of fashion change if clothing choices were based on <strong>tactile</strong> pleasure instead of visual trends?
      personal: ★ What texture makes you feel safest and most comfortable?
    - main: Do you think your judgment of people would become less <strong>superficial</strong> if you couldn't see their clothes, race, or age?
      personal: ★ How much do visual biases corrupt our initial opinions of others?
    - main: How would you appreciate a crowded market if it sounded like a chaotic <strong>symphony</strong> of voices, steps, and clinking plates?
      personal: ★ Does the eye simplify a room, while the ear makes it more complex?
    - main: How would you keep up with your hobbies and stay independent without a digital screen to look at?
      personal: ★ What analog activity would you miss the most?
    - main: If you were suddenly given your sight back for just one hour, what is the single thing you would want to look at?
      personal: ★ Would you look at a natural landscape or the face of a loved one?

    - main: Blindness cultivates a much richer and more stable sense of self because it is immune to visual comparison.
      personal: ★ Do mirrors do more damage to our self-esteem than they help?

      personal: ★ Does visual panic spread faster than acoustic alarm?

      personal: ★ Why do we feel the need to look at our food before tasting it?
    - main: We would have much better social policies if politicians were forced to draft laws with their eyes closed.
      personal: ★ Does visual evidence sometimes distract leaders from systemic truths?
    - main: A physical environment that is not accessible for non-visual <strong>orientation</strong> is a failed design, regardless of how beautiful it looks.

    - main: If you were blind, your dreams would consist of rich, overlapping <strong>symphonies</strong> and textures that sight cannot comprehend.
