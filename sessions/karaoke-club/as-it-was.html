❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Change
make or become different.
The city has undergone a huge change over the past year.
Opposite: Stability
Isolation
the state of being alone or cut off from others.
He felt a sense of isolation after moving to a new country.
Opposite: Community
Nostalgia
a sentimental longing for the past.
Listening to old records fills her with nostalgia.
Opposite: Modernity
Suburban
characteristic of a quiet residential area outside a city.
They moved to a quiet suburban neighborhood.
Opposite: Urban
Gravity
the force that pulls things down, or extreme seriousness.
She finally understood the gravity of the situation.
Opposite: Levity

Disconnect
a breakdown in communication or emotional connection.
There was a clear disconnect between their expectations.
Opposite: Connection
Melancholy
a feeling of gentle, pensive sadness.
A soft melancholy settled over the autumn evening.
Opposite: Joy
Expectation
a belief about what will happen in the future.
He struggled to meet his family's high expectation.
Opposite: Surprise
Routine
a regular sequence of actions or habits.
Her morning routine includes coffee and a brisk walk.
Opposite: Novelty
Reflection
serious thought or careful consideration.
Quiet reflection helps him process difficult life choices.

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1: Lyric Analysis & Discussion

In 'as it was', Harry Styles sings about how gravity holds him back. How does sudden change in life make us feel heavy or stuck?
★ What is a major change you experienced recently that altered your daily habits?
The lyrics describe sitting alone on the floor while friends worry on the phone. Why can isolation feel comforting yet dangerous?
★ When you feel overwhelmed, do you seek isolation or reach out to friends?
Harry refuses to talk about 'the way that it was'. How can holding onto nostalgia stop someone from moving forward?
★ Is there a past moment in your life that you view with strong nostalgia?
The song mentions internet, moving to America, and routine domestic life. How can a suburban lifestyle feel restricting?
★ Would you prefer living in a quiet suburban town or a busy city center?
The line 'gravity's holdin' me back' suggests emotional weight. What gives a person emotional gravity during tough transitions?
★ What anchor or value gives you personal gravity when life gets chaotic?
When phone calls go unanswered and nobody comes to help, how does an emotional disconnect grow between loved ones?
★ How do you repair a sudden disconnect with an old friend?
Despite its upbeat melody, the song carries a soft melancholy. Why do people enjoy danceable songs with sad lyrics?
★ Do you prefer listening to cheerful music or gentle melancholy when you are down?
The song reflects on how 'it's not the same as it was'. How do high expectations from others create pressure on us?
★ How do you handle it when life fails to meet your original expectation?
Verse 2 describes sitting at home as a daily habit. Why is breaking a comfortable routine so challenging?
★ What positive routine helps keep your mind steady during busy weeks?
The intro begins with a child's voice calling for Harry. How does quiet reflection on childhood memories shape who we become?
★ What childhood memory brings you a moment of peaceful reflection?

🟢 Round 2: Broad Themes & Philosophy Debate

Accepting that life will never be 'as it was' is the most essential step toward personal change and maturity. Discuss.
Modern technology and light-speed internet create more emotional disconnect than real human bonding. Defend or oppose.
✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

Language Focus: Comparative *As... as* with Past Habits

In the song As It Was, the repetitive lyric chunk "You know it's not the same as it was" highlights the use of Comparative *As... as* with Past Habits. This structure supports: Analyzing life changes and temporal transitions.

Let's Practise : Three Examples:
  • Practice using "You know it's not the same as it was" or related forms actively during your discussion rounds today.
  • Notice how the lyric chunk "You know it's not the same as it was" builds rhythmic familiarity and emotional weight.
  • Incorporate the grammatical structure behind "You know it's not the same as it was" in your Stage 6 final speaking challenge.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
It's not the same than it was. → It's not the same as it was. (Use 'as' after 'the same' when making direct comparisons.)
Why you are sitting on the floor alone? → Why are you sitting on the floor alone? (Invert subject and auxiliary verb in direct questions.)
Gravity is holding me behind. → Gravity is holding me back. (Use the phrasal verb 'hold back' to mean restrain or delay.)
