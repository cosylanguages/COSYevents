📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Kernels
small, soft, or edible parts of a nut, seed, or corn; metaphorically, tiny bits.
He only threw her a few kernels of affection, keeping her wanting more.
Opposite: Bulk
Delusional
characterized by or holding idiosyncratic beliefs or impressions that are contradicted by reality.
He was delusional to think he could win without practicing at all.
Opposite: Realistic
Obsession
an unhealthy preoccupation with someone or something.
His obsession with cleanliness became a bit extreme.
Opposite: Indifference
cactus
A succulent plant with thick fleshy stems and spines, adapted to arid conditions.
Living in a harsh emotional desert requires resilience, much like a thriving cactus.
drought
A prolonged period of abnormally low rainfall, leading to a shortage of water.
An emotional drought can leave you feeling drained until genuine connection returns.

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

symbolism
the use of symbols to represent ideas or qualities.
The director used rich visual symbolism to convey the deeper theme.
stockpile
A large accumulated stock or reserve of goods or materials kept for future use.
She built a stockpile of emergency supplies and bottled water just in case of unexpected trouble.
compliment
a polite expression of praise or admiration.
He felt wonderful after receiving a sincere compliment from his teacher.
scarcity
The state of being scarce or in short supply; shortage.
Living with a mindset of emotional scarcity makes people cling desperately to crumbs.
patience
the capacity to accept or tolerate delay or trouble.
Learning to play an instrument requires a lot of patience.
