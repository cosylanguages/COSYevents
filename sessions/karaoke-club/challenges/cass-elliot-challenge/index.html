🎵 Stage 1: Warm-up▲
  • Do you like singing or listening to happy music?
  • Predict: The title is "Make Your Own Kind of Music". Is this song happy or sad?
  • Do you have a favorite singer or band? Who are they?
  • Do you listen to music when you are alone?
📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Be yourself
To act naturally and authentically according to your own values rather than conforming to others' expectations.
Cass Elliot urges listeners to embrace their individuality and be yourself even when walking alone.
Late
Occurring or arriving after the expected, usual, or proper time.
It is never too late to start making your own kind of music and choosing your own direction.
Melody
A sequence of single notes that is musically satisfying; a tune.
The song's uplifting melody stays in your mind long after the track finishes playing.
Choir
An organized group of singers, especially one that performs together in concerts or church services.
Singing in a choir brings people together, but sometimes you must stand out and sing your own tune.
Chorus
A repeated section of a song that contains the primary musical and lyrical message.
The powerful chorus reinforces the theme of remaining true to your personal path.

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

Solo
A musical piece or section performed by a single performer.
Taking a solo moment in life requires courage when everyone else is singing in harmony.
Happy
feeling or showing pleasure or contentment.
Listening to our favorite songs always makes us feel happy.
Friend
kind and pleasant; behaving in a helpful or supporting way.
The new neighbors gave us a very warm and friendly welcome.
Path
A route, track, or course of action taken toward a specific destination or goal.
Forging your own path can feel lonely at first, but it leads to genuine self-fulfillment.
Harmony
The combination of simultaneously sounded musical notes to produce chords and a pleasing effect.
Finding inner harmony allows you to navigate life's challenges with confidence and peace.

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1: Lyric Analysis & Discussion

It is good to be yourself. Do you agree with this?
★ When is it easy to be yourself?
Did you ever arrive late to an important concert or meeting?
★ Do you prefer to arrive early or late?
The song has a beautiful melody. Do you like fast melodies?
★ Can you play a simple melody on an instrument?
The singer performs with a choir. Do you like choir music?
★ Did you ever sing in a school choir?
The chorus of this song is very easy to sing. Do you like it?
★ Can you repeat the main chorus of your favorite song?
She sings a beautiful solo in the middle. Do you like solos?
★ Would you sing a solo in front of people?
Singing makes many people feel very happy. What makes you happy?
★ What is your favorite happy song to listen to?
A good friend always supports your choices. Who is your best friend?
★ Do you have a friend who loves music too?
Everyone chooses their own life path. What is your path?
★ Is your professional path easy or difficult?
Singing in harmony sounds beautiful. Do you like harmonized songs?
★ Is there harmony and peace in your daily routine?
🎵 Interactive Musical Checkpoints (Karaoke Identity)
  • Before Listening: Predict based on the title: Do you expect a slow, sad, acoustic ballad, or a bright, energetic, and highly harmonized dance track?
  • After Verse 1: Pause after 'sing your own special song'. Why does Cass Elliot want us to sing our own song?
  • During the Chorus: Listen to how the upbeat, happy backup vocals contrast with the idea of being alone. Why did they ensure the song this way?
  • After Singing/Listening: Which lyric line remains stuck in your head? Why?

🟢 Round 2: Broad Themes & Philosophy Debate

We must always be yourself and never change for other people. Agree or disagree?
Listening to a happy melody can instantly cure a bad mood. Agree or disagree?
It is better to sing a solo than to sing in a choir. Agree or disagree?

✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

Language Focus: Direct Imperatives for Individualism

In the song Make Your Own Kind Of Music, the repetitive lyric chunk "Make your own kind of music... sing" highlights the use of Direct Imperatives for Individualism. This structure supports: Direct call to authentic self-expression.

Let's Practise : Three Examples:
  • Practice using "Make your own kind of music... sing" or related forms actively during your discussion rounds today.
  • Notice how the lyric chunk "Make your own kind of music... sing" builds rhythmic familiarity and emotional weight.
  • Incorporate the grammatical structure behind "Make your own kind of music... sing" in your Stage 6 final speaking challenge.

🎵 Stage 1: Warm-up▲
  • How important is this discussion theme in your daily life?
  • Look at the title 'It's Getting Better' and the artist 'Cass Elliot'. What do you predict this song is about? (Prediction question)
  • What kind of emotions does this style of music bring to you?
📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Optimism ≠ Pessimism
Hopefulness and confidence about the future or the successful outcome of something.
Her growing optimism helped her believe that things were getting better every single day.
Improvement
The process of making something better or the state of being improved.
Noticing a gradual improvement in your daily life brings renewed energy and purpose.
Relief
A feeling of reassurance and relaxation following release from anxiety, distress, or pain.
Receiving an official medical diagnosis brought unexpected relief by validating her long struggles.
Sunshine
Direct sunlight, or warmth and cheerfulness in one's disposition or life.
Even after a long period of gloom, the return of sunshine brightens the entire horizon.
Bright
Giving off or reflecting much light; shining, intelligent, or full of hope.
Looking forward to a bright future helps banish lingering doubts and fears.

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

Dream
experiencing dreams, or indulging in daydreaming and fantasies.
He spent the entire afternoon dreamin' of a sunny beach vacation.
Path
A route, track, or course of action taken toward a specific destination or goal.
Forging your own path can feel lonely at first, but it leads to genuine self-fulfillment.
Future
The period of time that will come after the present moment.
Investing in your emotional health paves the way for a healthier and happier future.
Cheer
Shouts of encouragement or a mood of joy, enthusiasm, and good spirits.
The warm words of support brought instant cheer to everyone in the room.
Harmony
The combination of simultaneously sounded musical notes to produce chords and a pleasing effect.
Finding inner harmony allows you to navigate life's challenges with confidence and peace.

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1: Lyric Analysis & Discussion

In 'It's Getting Better', Cass Elliot sings about finding joy even when life seems slow. How does maintaining optimism help people get through difficult times?
★ How do you personally cope when you feel a wave of optimism ≠ Pessimism in your daily life?
Cass Elliot describes how life gradually improves day by day. Why is noticing small signs of improvement so crucial during tough seasons?
★ Have you ever found a surprising source of strength while dealing with improvement?
Analyzing the emotional depth of Cass Elliot, how does relief shape the tone of this song?
★ Do you find it easier to express relief through art, music, or quiet conversation?
The song uses warm imagery to describe emotional recovery. How does the feeling of sunshine after a storm change our perspective?
★ What is the most comforting thing someone can do for you when you are feeling warm and hopeful like sunshine?
How does the musical melody of 'It's Getting Better' reflect the heavy joy of brightness?
★ How has your personal way of handling dark moments before finding the bright side changed as you have grown older?
When Cass Elliot sings, does the expression of dream feel like a cry for help or a silent acceptance?
★ Do you believe that sharing our dream makes us feel more connected or more vulnerable?
Does 'It's Getting Better' suggest that we can find comfort in path, or does it always bring pain?
★ Is there a specific place or activity that always helps you heal from path?
How does the imagery in 'It's Getting Better' express anticipation for a brighter future?
★ If you could offer comfort to a friend who is quietly struggling with future, what would you say?
In your opinion, does the protagonist in 'It's Getting Better' overcome their cheer, or is it left unresolved?
★ Do you think that society encourages us to hide our cheer rather than talk about it?
How does 'It's Getting Better' help us understand the universal human experience of harmony?
★ What is the most important lesson you have learned about emotional resilience through harmony?

🟢 Round 2: Broad Themes & Philosophy Debate

The heavy portrayal of optimism ≠ Pessimism in 'It's Getting Better' shows that some emotional wounds never truly heal. Let's debate.
Without a willingness to feel our own improvement, we can never experience true joy and recovery. Discuss.
Cass Elliot's work proves that art is the only genuine way to transform relief into something beautiful. Defend or oppose.
