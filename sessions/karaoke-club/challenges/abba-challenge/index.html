🎵 Stage 1: Warm-up▲
  • Do you ever feel like there are two different 'sides' or voices inside your head competing for attention?
  • Predict: The title is "Me and I". Do you think this represents a peaceful harmony or an internal struggle between two halves?
  • Have you ever reacted strongly to a situation and then immediately regretted how you acted?
  • Do you believe that everyone has a small, quirky 'freak' side to their personality?
📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Scream
give a long, loud, piercing cry, expressing extreme emotion or pain.
The scary movie made her scream in terror.
Opposite: Whisper
Jekyll
representing the good or moral side of a dual personality (from Jekyll and Hyde).
He showed his gentle Jekyll side by helping the elderly lady.
Opposite: Hyde
Hyde
representing the dark, wild, or destructive side of a dual personality.
When he is angry, his aggressive Hyde personality takes over.
Opposite: Jekyll
Freak
a person, animal, or plant that is abnormal or highly unusual.
The heavy snow in summer was a complete freak of nature.
Opposite: Standard
Toyed
moved or handled something idly; played with an idea or concept.
She toyed with the idea of moving to Paris for a year.
Opposite: Ignored

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

Freud
referring to Sigmund Freud, the father of psychoanalysis who analyzed internal conflicts.
Dr. Freud analyzed the patient's dreams to understand their hidden fears.
Opposite: Skeptic
Split
divided into two or more parts, factions, or groups.
She has a split personality, with one side being quiet and the other wild.
Opposite: Unified
Identity
the fact of being who or what a person or thing is.
Studying abroad helped her discover her true personal identity.
Opposite: Anonymity
Combination
a joining or merging of different parts or qualities in which the component elements are individually distinct.
This delicious dessert is a unique combination of sweet and salty flavors.
Opposite: Separation
Gloomy
feeling distressed, sad, or lacking in bright light.
The gloomy autumn weather made him feel like staying in bed all day.
Opposite: Bright

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1: Lyric Analysis & Discussion

Sometimes when we are angry, we scream or act rashly. How do you handle sudden, intense bursts of anger?
★ What is something that makes you want to scream in frustration when you see it happen?
The song references Dr. Jekyll, the calm side of a dual personality. How do you cultivate your own inner calm and reason?
★ In what situations do you feel your gentle, patient jekyll side is most needed?
Mr. Hyde represents the wilder, uncontrolled side of the self. What triggers your more impulsive or impatient side?
★ Have you ever let your impulsive hyde side make a spontaneous decision that you later enjoyed?
The chorus claims that 'everyone's a freak.' Do you find comfort in knowing that nobody is completely normal?
★ What is a funny quirk or 'Freak' habit you have that you are proud of?
ABBA sings that they have 'Toyed' with psychological ideas. Have you ever toyed with a major life change without actually doing it?
★ What is an artistic project or hobby you've toyed with starting recently?
The lyrics mention ideas from 'good old Dr. Freud.' Do you find psychoanalysis helpful for understanding your dreams and behaviors?
★ Have you ever analyzed a dream or feeling using ideas you heard about Dr. Freud or psychology?
The song explores a 'Split identity' or dual nature. Is it possible to be completely consistent in our behavior all the time?
★ Tell us about a time you felt completely split between two different choices or desires.
Discovering our true personal identity is a lifelong journey. How has your identity changed since you were younger?
★ What is one core value or part of your identity that you hope will never change?
We are described as a 'funny combination' of gloomy moods and inspiration. What is your favorite combination of opposite things?
★ What unique combination of skills or experiences makes you great at your job?
When feeling in a gloomy mood, how do you find inspiration and positive energy again?
★ What is your favorite activity to lift your spirits when the weather is cold and gloomy?
🎵 Interactive Musical Checkpoints (Karaoke Identity)
  • Before Listening: Predict based on the title: Do you expect a highly dramatic, dark ballad, or an upbeat synth-pop track with catchy hooks?
  • After Verse 1 (Pause & Predict): Pause after 'Jekyll must have been to Hyde'. Why does she feel Jekyll and Hyde is the perfect metaphor for her internal feelings?
  • During the Chorus (Sun & Rain): How does the metaphor of 'sun and rainy weather' explain the relationship between 'Me' and 'I'? How does the tempo match this dual comparison?
  • After Verse 2 (The Freud Connection): Pause after 'split identity'. Why does she bring up Dr. Freud? Is she being serious, or is it a playful self-deprecation?
  • After Singing/Listening (Lyrical Takeaway): Which lyric line about internal struggle or dual nature resonates with you the most? Why?

🟢 Round 2: Broad Themes & Philosophy Debate

Screaming or expressing anger loudly is a healthy way to release tension. Agree or disagree?
Most people are much more like the reasonable Dr. Jekyll than they are willing to admit. Agree or disagree?
It is important to embrace our inner hyde because too much self-control makes life boring. Agree or disagree?
The fear of being labeled a freak prevents people from pursuing their unique passions. Agree or disagree?
It is dangerous to keep toyed with ideas of leaving your job or relationship if you aren't ready to act. Agree or disagree?
Dr. Freud was right: our unconscious mind and childhood determine almost everything we do. Agree or disagree?
Having a split personality or changing how you act around different people is a necessary social skill, not hypocrisy. Agree or disagree?
Our digital identity on social media is a completely false representation of who we really are. Agree or disagree?
The best partnerships are a combination of opposite personalities who balance each other. Agree or disagree?
A rainy, gloomy afternoon is much more productive for study and work than a bright, sunny day. Agree or disagree?

✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

Reflexive Pronouns (-self / -selves)

We use reflexive pronouns when the subject and the object of a verb are the exact same person. In the song, ABBA explores internal conflicts: 'I am to myself what Jekyll must have been to Hyde' and asks us to 'think about yourself for a minute'.

Let's Practise : Three Examples:
  • I am looking at myself in the mirror and seeing two different people.
  • You should ask yourself if Dr. Freud's split identity theory applies to you.
  • They found themselves caught in a combination of gloomy and bright moods.
🎭 Stage 6: Final Challenge▲
🎭

The Internal Conversation: Role-play a short dialogue between your 'Jekyll' side (responsible, calm) and your 'Hyde' side (spontaneous, emotional) deciding how to spend a Friday evening.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I am looking at me in the mirror → I am looking at myself in the mirror (Use the reflexive pronoun 'myself' when the subject and object refer to the same person.)
She has a split of personality → She has a split personality (Use 'split' as a direct noun modifier before 'personality' without 'of'.)
Everyone are a freak sometimes → Everyone is a freak sometimes (Indefinite pronouns like 'everyone' take a singular verb form 'is'.)

🎵 Stage 1: Warm-up▲
  • Do you believe that some people have an exceptionally charming look or 'gaze' that can easily win trust?
  • Predict: The title is "Angeleyes". Do you think this is a song of pure love, or a cautionary warning about deception?
  • Have you ever been completely charmed by someone, only to find out they were wearing a 'disguise' or pretending to be someone else?
  • What kind of emotional atmosphere do you expect from an ABBA song with such a beautiful, classic title?
📚 Stage 2: Vocabulary▲

❤️ Core Concepts & Feelings

Hypnotised
having been put into a state of hypnosis; highly captivated.
She sat completely hypnotised by the beautiful piano performance.
Opposite: Indifferent
Paradise
an ideal or idyllic place or state; heaven.
The tropical island felt like a peaceful paradise on earth.
Opposite: Hell
Disguise
give a new appearance to hide identity.
She wore a wig as a clever disguise.
Opposite: Reveal
Shiver
shake slightly and uncontrollably as a result of being cold, frightened, or excited.
The cold winter wind made her shiver despite her warm coat.
Opposite: Sultry-glow
Game
an activity or sport played according to rules, or a deceptive trick.
He realized his friend was playing a manipulative game with his feelings.
Opposite: Reality

🗣️ Life, Actions & Connection

Pay
give someone money that is due for work done, or suffer the consequences of an action.
If you treat people badly, you will eventually have to pay the price.
Opposite: Default
Lonely
sad because one has no friends or company.
She felt very lonely during her first week living in the big city.
Opposite: Sociable
Pain
highly distressing physical or emotional sensation.
Time eventually heals the deep pain of a broken heart.
Opposite: Comfort
Disappointment
sadness or displeasure caused by the nonfulfillment of one's hopes or expectations.
Her failing grade was a major disappointment to her parents.
Opposite: Fulfillment
Gaze
look steadily and intently.
He would gaze at the stars for hours in the backyard.
Opposite: Glance

💬 Stage 4: Discussion▲

🔵 Round 1: Lyric Analysis & Discussion

The singer gets completely hypnotised by his look. Why is charisma such a powerful tool in dating and relationships?
★ Tell us about a performer, actor, or person who completely hypnotised or captivated you with their talent.
He makes people feel like they are in paradise before breaking their hearts. Why is the beginning of a romance often so perfect?
★ What is your personal idea of a perfect paradise vacation to escape daily worries?
The chorus warns that 'he wears a disguise.' How can we distinguish between someone's authentic personality and their social disguise?
★ Have you ever had to wear a clever disguise or costume for a themed event or party?
The look that he gave her made her shiver. Why do physical sensations like shivering or getting goosebumps carry such strong emotional meanings?
★ Does the cold winter wind make you shiver more, or do you shiver from excitement before a big event?
The protagonist realizes too late that 'it's a game he likes to play.' Why do some people enjoy playing with other people's emotions?
★ What is your absolute favorite board game or card game to play with friends on a cozy evening?
The song tells us that 'you must pay the price' for falling too fast. What are the emotional costs of trusting the wrong person?
★ Do you prefer to pay with cash, card, or your phone when shopping for daily groceries?
The singer admits she feels deeply lonely when remembering him. Is it better to be lonely and safe, or to take emotional risks?
★ What is a great hobby or routine that keeps you from feeling lonely when you are home by yourself?
Every memory of him brings back all the pain of the betrayal. How does time eventually heal emotional wounds?
★ What is a great physical or mental exercise that helps you relieve neck or back pain after long hours at a desk?
Discovering his lies was a crushing disappointment. How do you handle major disappointments in your personal or professional life?
★ Tell us about a minor disappointment (like a bad movie or cold meal) that you had to laugh off recently.
She warns us not to look too deep into his charming gaze. What can a person's eyes tell you about their true intentions?
★ Do you find it easy or difficult to maintain a steady gaze when giving a public presentation?
🎵 Interactive Musical Checkpoints (Karaoke Identity)
  • Before Listening: Predict based on the title 'Angeleyes': Do you expect a slow, sad, acoustic ballad, or a bright, energetic, and highly harmonized dance track?

In the modern professional world, everyone must wear a disguise of confidence to succeed. Agree or disagree?
Physical reactions like a shiver of intuition are often more reliable than logical reasoning. Agree or disagree?
Dating has inherently become a competitive game of power, rather than a search for genuine connection. Agree or disagree?

✨ Stage 5: Language Focus▲

Relative Clauses with 'Who', 'Which', and 'That'

Relative clauses give us more information about a person or thing. In the song, the singer warns about a man 'who' wears a disguise and uses games 'that' he likes to play.

Let's Practise : Three Examples:
  • The look that he gave her made me shiver with disappointment.
  • Don't look too deep into the angel eyes of a guy who wears a clever disguise.
  • She remembered all the good times which eventually brought her so much pain.
🎭 Stage 6: Final Challenge▲
🎭

The Warning Card: Write a short, anonymous warning card to a young girl who is currently hypnotised by a guy with beautiful angel eyes. Warn her about his disguise and games.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
Look into the eyes of a man which lies → Look into the eyes of a man who lies (Use the relative pronoun 'who' for people, not 'which'.)
He made me to shiver → He made me shiver (Causative 'make' is followed by object + bare infinitive without 'to'.)
I must to pay the price → I must pay the price (Modal auxiliary 'must' is followed directly by bare infinitive without 'to'.)
