☕ The Greatest Quotes Perspective

"If you want others to be happy, practice compassion. If you want to be happy, practice compassion." — Dalai Lama. Examining how altruism, mental discipline, and empathy build resilient happiness.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Altruism ≠ Egoism
the belief in or practice of disinterested and selfless concern for the well-being of others.
Altruism creates strong, supportive communities.
Equanimity ≠ Agitation
mental calmness, composure, and evenness of temper, especially in a difficult situation.
Maintaining equanimity helps you make wise decisions under pressure.
Secular ≠ Religious
not connected with religious or spiritual matters.
Secular ethics focus on universal human values like kindness.
Perspective ≠ Narrow-mindedness
a particular attitude toward or way of regarding something; a point of view.
Changing your perspective can solve seemingly impossible problems.
Contentment ≠ Discontent
a state of happiness and satisfaction.
True contentment comes from inner peace, not material goods.
Interdependence ≠ Independence
the dependence of two or more people or things on each other.
Recognizing global interdependence encourages international cooperation.
Resilience ≠ Fragility
the capacity to recover quickly from difficulties; toughness.
Mental resilience helps people bounce back after failures.
Aspiration ≠ Apathy
a hope or ambition of achieving something.
Her highest aspiration is to help reduce human suffering.
Mindfulness ≠ Mindlessness
the quality or state of being conscious or aware of something.
Daily mindfulness reduces stress and increases clarity.
Empathy ≠ Indifference
the ability to understand and share the feelings of another.
Cultivating empathy fosters deep human connections.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up (10 mins)▲

Welcome! Share your initial thoughts on this topic. What personal experiences or observations come to mind right away?

⚡ Grammar Practice : First and Second Conditionals for Ethical Debates (B1)▲
Use conditionals to discuss ethical choices: "If everyone practiced daily empathy, society would experience much less conflict."
🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)▲
Why does the Dalai Lama emphasize that altruism leads to personal contentment?
★ How do you feel after doing something selfless for a stranger?
How can practicing mindfulness help us maintain equanimity during hard times?
★ What technique helps you stay calm during stressful moments?
Why are secular ethics essential for promoting peace in a diverse world?
★ What universal human value matters most to you?
How does understanding global interdependence foster greater empathy?
★ In what ways do you feel connected to people in other countries?
How does shifting your perspective build mental resilience?
★ What difficult experience in your past eventually taught you a valuable lesson?
🟢 Round 2: Deep Speculation & Future Horizon (Units 6–10)▲
Can secular mindfulness training in schools transform future society?
★ Would you support daily quiet reflection periods in schools?
How does personal aspiration for material success contrast with seeking inner contentment?
★ How do you define success for yourself?
How can recognizing interdependence help humanity tackle climate change?
★ What small eco-friendly choice do you practice daily?
Why is cultivating emotional resilience more effective than avoiding discomfort?
★ How do you encourage yourself when facing a daunting task?
What advice from the Dalai Lama provides the clearest guidance for modern life?
★ What quote or piece of wisdom guides your daily decisions?
