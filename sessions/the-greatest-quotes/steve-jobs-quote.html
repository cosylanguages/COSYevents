Steve Jobs is the person who revolutionized technology and design by merging cold engineering with the warm, rebellious spirit of art, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "Stay hungry, stay foolish." Why is it that we are so eager to settle for safety and predictability? Does the fear of looking foolish prevent us from taking the very risks that could redefine our lives? Let us explore how keeping a hungry mind can change our personal path.

🧭

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

📖 Session Vocabulary

Hungry
having a strong, continuous desire or craving for something (like learning or goals).
To achieve great milestones, you must stay hungry for knowledge and never settle.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What is one thing you are "hungry" to learn right now?
  • What is the "most foolish" (riskiest) thing you have ever done?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Gerunds and Infinitives▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Certain verbs and expressions are followed strictly by a gerund (-ing, like avoid, enjoy, keep, risk) or an infinitive (to + verb, like choose, decide, refuse, manage).

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: The expression 'avoid' and the preposition 'instead of' require a gerund (e.g., _____ risks), whereas 'manage' and 'choose' require an infinitive (e.g., _____ a path).

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. Steve Jobs encouraged students _____ their heart and intuition.
  2. Many traditional business managers refuse _____ their corporate stability.
  3. Instead of conforming, he chose to keep _____ forward on his own path.
  4. Are you keen on _____ unconventional risks in your current career?
  5. We managed _____ deep inspiration in very unexpected places.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

Do you enjoy taking unconventional paths, or do you prefer to keep playing it safe in your daily career decisions?

🔵 Round 1 : Analysis▲
What do you think Steve Jobs meant by "stay foolish", and why is healthy risk-taking so essential for success?
★ Have you ever made a choice that others thought was foolish, but turned out to be correct?
Why is it important to stay hungry for knowledge even after achieving your initial goals?
★ What is one project or skill that you are currently hungry to master?
How does the fear of failure prevent people from following their gut intuition?
★ When was the last time you ignored logic to follow your own intuition?
Why are highly visionary ideas often rejected by traditional experts at the beginning?
★ Do you consider yourself a dreamer with a big future vision, or a practical person?
In what ways does constant, stubborn perseverance outweigh natural talent in building a business?
★ What is the hardest obstacle you have managed to overcome through pure perseverance?
Why is an unconventional career path often far more rewarding than a traditional, safe one?
★ What is the most unconventional habit or routine that helps you stay productive?
How does intellectual curiosity prevent our daily minds from becoming lazy and ordinary?
★ What narrow, unusual topic has sparked your curiosity recently?
Why does a great technological invention require us to tolerate a high level of uncertainty?
★ If you could create any new invention to make daily life simpler, what would it do?
How does having strong personal ambition help you stay focused during periods of doubt?
★ How has your personal ambition shifted or evolved over the last five years?
Can a person stay true to their own path if they never engage in active risk-taking?
★ If you had to describe your attitude toward risk-taking in one word, what would it be?

🟢 Round 2 : Speculating on the Future▲
In the future, technology will allow us to measure a person's level of curiosity and focus in real time. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How would you adapt if school algorithms began to filter and monitor your individual ambition?
Modern digital screens will eventually destroy our collective capacity for deep perseverance and gut intuition. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you use brain-boosting chips to artificially stay hungry for learning?
Future societies will completely ban standardized exams, letting children learn purely based on unconventional methods. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think future kids will be more willing to take foolish risks than today's children?
As artificial intelligence advances, humans will not need to focus on any traditional invention. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What is one simple human invention you hope we never lose?
The survival of human civilization will depend on our willingness to engage in bold space risk-taking. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How do you personally prepare for a future where traditional careers are fully automated?
In the coming decades, a truly visionary leader will be selected by software predictions rather than voting. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If you could send a single message about the power of intuition to people 100 years in the future, what would it be?
Global digital connectivity will make true unconventional lifestyles incredibly difficult to maintain. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you choose to live in a futuristic city that guarantees physical safety at the cost of your risk-taking?
In the future, social status will be determined by how hungry a person is for growth rather than their family wealth. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What role do you hope personal intuition will play in your own professional future over the next decade?
One day, being passionately curious and foolish will be seen as the single most valuable trait of any worker. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ In what area of your life do you want to show greater ambition right now?
A world fully governed by smart algorithms will have no space left for the quiet mystery of human invention. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If an app could make you very successful, would you still listen to your intuition?
🏁 Closing Circle: Complete the Sentence▲

Finish the session by going in a circle. Each student must complete the following sentence with exactly one adjective or short phrase using the vocabulary learned:

"Staying foolish is..."

Examples: about curiosity, unconventional, visionary living, listening to intuition, necessary for invention, a brave choice.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
He have went to Paris → He has gone to Paris (Present perfect third-person singular 'has' + past participle 'gone'.)
We discussed about the problem → We discussed the problem / had a discussion about the problem ('Discuss' is transitive and takes no preposition, or use 'have a discussion about'.)
