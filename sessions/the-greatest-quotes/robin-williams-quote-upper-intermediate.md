    - main: How is the belief that seeking psychological help is a weakness <strong>detrimental</strong> to society?
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt that asking for emotional help was judged negatively by others?
    - main: Can we truly <strong>simulate</strong> emotional stability, or do our micro-expressions always betray us?
      personal: ★ Are you good at detecting when someone is trying to simulate being calm?
    - main: What causes people to <strong>shroud</strong> their true worries in silence rather than speaking out?
      personal: ★ What worry or dream do you currently shroud from your colleagues?
    - main: Is a sense of poetic <strong>melancholy</strong> essential for producing great art and literature?
      personal: ★ Do you enjoy listening to melancholy music when you are feeling down?
    - main: How does deep <strong>compassion</strong> differ from simply feeling sorry for someone?
      personal: ★ Who in your life has shown you the most genuine compassion?
    - main: How do young adults <strong>grapple</strong> with the sudden weight of professional and social expectations?
      personal: ★ What major expectation are you currently trying to grapple with?
    - main: Why is radical <strong>candor</strong> in friendships sometimes difficult but always necessary?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer friends who tell you the harsh truth with absolute candor?
    - main: In what situations do we use humor to <strong>camouflage</strong> our deepest insecurities?
      personal: ★ Have you ever used a joke to camouflage your embarrassment or anxiety?
    - main: Why does recognizing our own emotional <strong>fragility</strong> actually make us more resilient?
      personal: ★ When did admitting your emotional fragility help you find new inner strength?
