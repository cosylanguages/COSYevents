Quote

"Knowledge isn't free. You have to pay attention."

Richard Feynman is the person who unlocked the mysteries of quantum physics while passionately defending intellectual freedom, playfulness, and honesty, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "Knowledge isn't free. You have to pay attention." Why do we treat learning like a rigid, disciplined chore rather than a wild, original adventure? Is there a deeper wisdom in admitting we do not know everything? Join us as we dismantle formal educational dogmas and celebrate intellectual humility.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Knowledge
information, skills, and understanding that you get through learning.
Reading books is an excellent way to get new knowledge.
Attention
listening or looking carefully at someone or something.
Please pay attention to the teacher when she explains.
Free
costing no money; not needing payment.
This English website has a lot of free exercises.
Focus
to give all your attention to one thing.
I need to focus on my vocabulary list.
Remember
to keep in your mind; not forget.
Do you remember the meaning of the word 'focus'?
Learn
to get knowledge or skill in a new subject.
I want to learn how to speak Spanish.
Simple ≠ Complex
easily understood or done; presenting no difficulty ≠ consisting of many different and connected parts.
The basic idea was wonderfully simple, but the actual execution turned out to be incredibly complex.
Mind
the part of a person that thinks, feels, and remembers.
Meditation helps to calm the busy mind.
Share
to tell other people about your ideas or feelings.
I would like to share my study tips with you.
Difficult
hard to do, understand, or deal with; not easy.
This exercise is too difficult for me.

⚡ Grammar Practice : Inversion for Emphasis▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Beginner / Starter (A2))

Use inversion for emphasis after negative or restrictive adverbials (rarely, seldom, little did I know, not only) to sound formal and emphatic.

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: When we put a negative adverbial at the beginning of a sentence, the subject and the _____ are inverted (swapped). We use _____ for present simple or _____ for past simple.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. Rarely _____ meet someone passionately curious about simple truths.
  2. Not only _____ memorize the book, but he also analyzed it.
  3. Little _____ know that school education would not guarantee wisdom.
  4. Seldom _____ person accept ignorance with humble dignity.
  5. Only when we ask questions _____ achieve deep, authentic insight.

I am study every day→I study every day(Simple present structure)
It depend of your focus→It depends on your focus(Depends + on, singular s)
