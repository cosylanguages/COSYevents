Anonymous is the person who represents the collective, quiet wisdom of observers who find deep meaning in the fabric of everyday life, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "The amount of beautiful things in your life depends on your ability to notice them." How do we find a sense of place and belonging in a fast-paced, shifting world? Is home a geographic space, or is it a specific time frozen in our memory? Let us explore how we perceive time, direction, and the lives of those around us.

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"The amount of beautiful things in your life depends on your ability to notice them." : Anonymous

📖 Session Vocabulary

Appreciate
to recognize the full worth, quality, or value of something deeply.
We must learn to slow down and appreciate the simple, quiet moments of our day.
Mindfulness
the mental state of being fully conscious and aware of the present moment.
Practicing daily mindfulness helps reduce stress and improves our concentration.
To notice
to become aware of or pay active attention to someone or something around you.
If you walk too fast, you will fail to notice the lovely autumn leaves on the street.
Joy
a feeling of great pleasure, delight, and happiness.
Hearing her child's honest laughter filled her mind with pure, genuine joy.
Presence
the state or fact of existing, occurring, or being fully present in a place.
Real presence requires putting away your phone to listen to your partner carefully.
Aesthetic
concerned with beauty, art, or the appreciation of physical and natural beauty.
She loved the quiet, ancient aesthetic of the old coffee shops in Paris.
To ignore
to refuse to take notice of or pay attention to someone or something.
Busy corporate workers often choose to ignore their body's need for relaxation.
Gratitude
the quality of being thankful and showing appreciation for the good things in your life.
Keeping a daily journal of three good things is an excellent way to practice gratitude.
Ordinary
with no special, distinctive, or unusual features; normal or standard.
The quote teaches us that even an ordinary rainy day holds a quiet, simple beauty.
Aware
having knowledge or conscious perception of a situation, fact, or surrounding.
Being aware of your emotional state is essential to prevent feeling overwhelmed.

⚡ Grammar Practice : Gerunds vs. Infinitives▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Use gerunds (verb + -ing) as subjects of sentences or after prepositions. Use infinitives (to + verb) to show purpose or after adjectives like difficult, important, or easy.

🟣 Let's Speak Together : Mindful vs. Rushed Scenarios 🧘▲

Discuss with your partner: In which scenario is it easiest to practice presence and notice the ordinary beauty of life? Rank them:

🍁 Silent Observation
Sitting quietly on a park bench for ten minutes, watching the yellow autumn leaves fall.
🎧 Rushed Walk
Walking quickly to work while passively listening to an upbeat playlist on your headphones.
☕ Morning Ritual
Pausing to drink a warm cup of coffee carefully, focusing fully on the rich aroma and warmth.
📸 Digital Capture
Taking a photo of a beautiful sunset quickly to share online, instead of enjoying the moment.

Use gerunds (watching leaves is...) and infinitives to explain your reasoning!

🏁 Closing Circle: Complete the Sentence▲

Finish the session by going in a circle. Each student must complete the following sentence with exactly one adjective or short phrase using the vocabulary learned:

"Beauty is not far away, it is found by..."

Examples: noticing the ordinary, practicing mindfulness, appreciating simple things, staying fully aware, choosing joy.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I want to study more hard→I want to study harder(Comparative of 'hard' is irregular: 'harder')
I am notice the leaves→I notice the leaves / I am noticing the leaves(Simple present or present continuous structure)
It depend of your mind→It depends on your mind(Verb 'depend' takes preposition 'on', and singular agreement 's')
