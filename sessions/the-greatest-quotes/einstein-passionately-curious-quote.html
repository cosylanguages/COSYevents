"I have no special talents, I am only passionately curious.": Albert Einstein

05 November 2026

Duration

60 minutes

Languages

🇬🇧 English

Level

Elementary (A2)

Theme

Learning & Passion

Albert Einstein is the person who reconfigured our entire understanding of the cosmos while holding onto a childlike sense of wonder, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "I have no special talents, I am only passionately curious." Why does formal education so often put out the very fire of curiosity it is meant to feed? If special talent is a myth, how do we keep our passion for learning alive in a world obsessed with quick answers? Let us rediscover the beauty of the passionately curious mind.

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"Let us see how many interpretations exist! There is never only one correct answer to any profound human question." : Oscar Wilde

📖 Session Vocabulary

Talent
a natural ability to do something well.
She has a great talent for learning languages.
Passion
a strong feeling of excitement or interest in something.
He has a deep passion for science and nature.
Special
different from what is normal or usual.
You do not need special skills to begin your learning journey.
Curious
wanting to know or learn about something.
Young children are always curious about the world.
Skill
an ability to do something well because you practiced it.
Speaking another language is an important skill today.
Ordinary
normal or usual; not special.
He was an ordinary boy who worked very hard every day.
Knowledge
information and understanding that you get from study.
Reading books is a great way to get knowledge.
Simple
easy to understand or do; not complex.
The teacher gave a simple explanation of the grammar rule.
Focus
to give all your attention to one activity.
It is important to focus on your personal goals.
Humble
not proud; not thinking you are better than others.
Despite his great success, the scientist remained very humble.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What is something you are very curious about right now?
  • Do you believe that anyone can learn anything with enough curiosity?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Adverbs of Manner▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Elementary (A2))

Use adverbs of manner (usually ending in -ly, like passionately, curiously, easily, patiently) to describe how someone performs an action.

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: We make adverbs of manner by adding -ly to adjectives (e.g. passionate becomes _____, and humble becomes _____).

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. He worked _____ to discover the laws of physics.
  2. The child stared _____ at the stars in the night sky.
  3. The scientist explained his very complex theory _____.
  4. She waited _____ for her experiment to finish.
  5. Albert Einstein _____ said that he was only passionate.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

How do you learn best? Do you learn languages easily, or do you have to study patiently?

🟣 Let's Speak Together : Talent vs. Curiosity 📊▲

Discuss with your partner: Which quality is most important for achieving great success in these different fields? Rank them:

🎨 Fields of Art & Music
Does a great artist need natural talent, or do they just need to practice patiently?
🔬 Fields of Science & Logic
Is deep curiosity the most important skill for a scientist, or do they need a special brain?
🗣️ Learning Foreign Languages
Do you need a special talent for grammar, or do you just need to focus passionately?
💼 Building a Successful Business
Is focus and passion more important, or do you need special wealth and connections?

Use adverbs of manner (passionately, easily, patiently) to explain your views!

🟢 Round 2 : Speculating on the Future▲
In the future, technology will allow us to measure a child's natural talent at birth. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How would you feel if your future career was decided by your early special talents?
Modern digital screens will eventually destroy our collective capacity for deep passion and focus. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you use smart apps to help you stay focus on your personal projects?
Future schools will completely ban exams, letting children learn purely based on their curious interests. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think future kids will be more curious than today's children?
As artificial intelligence advances, humans will not need to learn any complex skill. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What is one simple skill you hope humans never lose?
The survival of human civilization will depend on our willingness to live ordinary, simple lives. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How do you personally prepare for a future where ordinary lives are seen as boring?
In the coming decades, human knowledge will double every single week. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If you could send one piece of knowledge to people 100 years in the future, what would it be?
Global digital connectivity will make true simple living incredibly difficult to maintain. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you choose to live in a futuristic city that guarantees a simple lifestyle at the cost of your freedom?
In the future, social status will be determined by a person's capacity for focus rather than wealth. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What role do you hope focus will play in your own personal future over the next decade?
A future world governed fully by smart technology will leave no space left for the quiet beauty of being humble. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If an app could make you very successful, would you still remain humble?
One day, being passionately curious will be seen as the single most valuable trait of any worker. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ In what area of your life do you want to show greater passion right now?
🏁 Closing Circle: Complete the Sentence▲

Finish the session by going in a circle. Each student must complete the following sentence with exactly one adjective or short phrase using the vocabulary learned:

"Curiosity is..."

Examples: special, a passion, a key skill, a simple joy, a focus, humble, greater than talent.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
He have went to Paris → He has gone to Paris (Present perfect third-person singular 'has' + past participle 'gone'.)
We discussed about the problem → We discussed the problem / had a discussion about the problem ('Discuss' is transitive and takes no preposition, or use 'have a discussion about'.)
I am agree with you → I agree with you (Use 'agree' directly as a verb rather than using it with the auxiliary 'am'.)
