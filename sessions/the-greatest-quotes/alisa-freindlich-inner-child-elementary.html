Theme

Childhood & Personal Gifts

Alisa Freindlich is the person who captivated generations of theatre and cinema lovers with her unparalleled grace and profound dramatic intensity, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "The longer we keep our inner child alive, the longer we keep the natural gifts we were born with." Why is it that growing up so often feels like a slow, quiet betrayal of who we once were? Do we bury our childhood curiosity to survive the expectations of the adult world, or can we choose to keep that early magic alive? Join us as we explore the art of preserving our natural talents.

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"The longer we keep our inner child alive, the longer we keep the natural gifts we were born with." : Alisa Freindlich

📖 Session Vocabulary

Inner child
a person's childhood self, especially regarded as an active part of their adult personality.
Playing with blocks helps adults connect with their inner child.
Natural
existing in or caused by nature; not made by humans.
Her love for singing is a natural ability.
Talent
a natural ability to do something well.
Drawing beautiful pictures is a great artistic talent.
Alive
living, active, or full of energy.
Doing interesting sports makes me feel alive.
Gift
a natural ability or talent.
He has a special gift for learning languages easily.
Preserve
to keep something in its original, safe state.
We must preserve our childhood toys and memories.
Curiosity
a strong desire to know or learn something new.
Childhood curiosity makes learning very easy and fun.
Sincerity
the quality of being honest and real.
Children speak with absolute sincerity and tell the truth.
Imagination
the ability to think of new and creative ideas.
Reading books helps to grow your creative imagination.
Wonder
a feeling of surprise and admiration for beautiful things.
The starry night sky filled the children with wonder.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What was your favorite game or toy when you were seven years old?
  • Do you think adults forget how to have simple fun?
🔵 Round 1 : Analytical Discussion▲
How can adults protect and keep their inner child happy in a busy city?
★ What is one activity from your childhood that you still love doing today?
Why is it important to live in a natural environment to feel relaxed?
★ Do you prefer walking in a quiet forest or visiting a busy shopping mall?
Do you believe that everyone is born with a special talent for something?
★ What is a simple talent you have that you are proud of?
How does playing with children make adults feel happy and alive?
★ When was the last time you felt completely full of energy and alive?
Is a natural gift like drawing more important than practicing hard?
★ What special gift or skill do you want to learn next?
How do we preserve our childhood friendships and good memories?
※ Do you still talk to your friends from elementary school?
Why do some schools stop encouraging curiosity in growing children?
★ What is something you are very curious about learning today?
Why is absolute sincerity so common in young children and rare in adults?
★ Do you find it easy to say exactly what you are thinking to others?
How can we use our imagination to solve daily problems at work?
★ Did you have a very active imagination when you were a child?
How does looking at the ocean fill our minds with a sense of wonder?
★ What was the most beautiful natural view you saw last year?
🟣 Let's Speak Together▲

A comparative reflection on our habits and self-perceptions.

🟢 Round 2 : Philosophical Application▲
In the future, we will have virtual reality games to help seniors re-experience their inner child. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you use a machine to see your childhood house as it was years ago?
Modern high-tech cities will eventually destroy our connection with natural forests. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think we will live in cities with no real trees in 100 years?
Future schools will test babies at birth to identify their genetic talent. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you want to know your baby's genetic skills at birth?
Without art and creative hobbies, human beings are not truly alive. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think practicing art is essential for a happy lifestyle?
A natural gift will always be more valuable than computer-calculated skills. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you value human-made paintings more than AI-made images?
It is impossible to preserve your childhood personality when you have a busy desk job. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Does your current job let you express your true creative self?
As we grow older, our natural curiosity is replaced by social duties. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think adults are less curious than children?
Social media makes it very difficult to speak with absolute sincerity online. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you share your real feelings or only your best moments on social networks?
Artificial intelligence will eventually replace the human imagination in writing books. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you read a novel if it was written entirely by an AI?
A world fully governed by logic will have no space left for the wonder of mystery. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think a world with zero mysteries would be boring?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
We are keep our inner child. → We keep our inner child / are keeping our inner child. (Underlined error: auxiliary 'are' should not be used with base verb 'keep')
I am agree with Voltaire. → I agree with Voltaire. (Underlined error: 'agree' is a verb, do not use it with auxiliary 'am')
Her talent is very beautifully. → Her talent is very beautiful. (Underlined error: use the adjective 'beautiful' after the linking verb 'is' rather than the adverb 'beautifully')
