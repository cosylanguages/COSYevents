Identity & Feedback

Jim Kwik is the person who represents the collective, quiet wisdom of observers who find deep meaning in the fabric of everyday life, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "Don't take criticism from someone you wouldn't take advice from." How do we find a sense of place and belonging in a fast-paced, shifting world? Is home a geographic space, or is it a specific time frozen in our memory? Let us explore how we perceive time, direction, and the lives of those around us.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Criticism
expression of disapproval, or analytical feedback about someone's performance or behavior.
Taking continuous criticism from strangers online can seriously damage your peace of mind.
Advice
guidance, directions, or recommendations offered to help someone make choices.
Before launching his project, he asked his trusted mentor for some professional advice.
Judgment
forming opinions, often critical or biased, about someone's choices or character.
Living with a constant fear of social judgment makes staying true to yourself impossible.
Feedback
helpful information about your performance or actions, used for future improvement.
The teacher gave him some constructive feedback on his essay, helping him study better.
Credibility
the quality of being trusted, respected, and believed.
If someone lacks credibility in a field, we shouldn't take their critical words seriously.
Validation
recognition, approval, or affirmation of a person's worth, choices, or ideas.
Relying on public validation from social media will make you a slave to peer opinions.
Resilience
the capacity to recover quickly from negative feedback, failure, or personal difficulties.
Building emotional resilience helps you listen to difficult criticism without getting upset.
Self-worth
confidence and proud belief in your own value and dignity as an individual.
When you possess strong self-worth, others' superficial opinions can never harm you.
Perspective
a particular way of looking at, understanding, or interpreting a situation.
Having a global perspective helps us see beyond local stereotypes and rules.
Influence
the power or capacity to affect someone's decisions, character, or self-image.
We must carefully choose who has the greatest influence over our life choices.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Whose advice do you trust the most in your life?
  • What was the last piece of criticism you received?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Second Conditional▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Use the second conditional (if + past, would + base verb) to speculate about hypothetical relationships, trust, and vulnerability.

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: In the Second Conditional, the if-clause uses the _____ tense, and the main clause uses _____ or _____ + base verb.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. If we _____ each other more, we would feel less vulnerable.
  2. I _____ my secrets if you promised to remain silent.
  3. If they _____ here, they would offer unconditional support.
  4. We _____ a deep connection if we stopped pretending.
  5. What _____ if someone betrayed your absolute trust?
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

If you could meet someone with absolute empathy and sincerity, what deep thoughts would you share with them?

🔵 Round 1 : Analysis▲
What does Jim Kwik mean when he advises us to never accept criticism from someone we wouldn't take advice from?
★ When was the last time you received a piece of negative criticism that made you question your abilities?
Why do we often react emotionally to the superficial judgment of people who lack any real credibility in our field?
★ How easily can you detach yourself emotionally from critical judgment made by total strangers?
How does receiving constructive feedback from a trusted mentor differ from facing public judgment?
★ Who is the single most trusted person you go to when you need honest, reliable feedback?
Why do we often seek constant public validation online, and how does it affect our internal self-worth?
★ Do you feel a continuous need for validation from your peers or co-workers?
How can a person build the emotional resilience required to filter out negative or biased opinions?
★ What is your go-to strategy for maintaining your self-worth when you face unfair criticism?
Why are we so easily subject to the influence of our close friends' expectations even when they clash with our values?
★ Whose personal perspective or opinion on your life choices has the greatest influence over you today?
How does having a healthy, proud sense of self-worth help people transform critical feedback into positive change?
★ Can you recall a moment where your own resilience helped you recover quickly from a major failure?
Why is an expert's credibility far more important than the simple speed or volume of their advice?
★ How do you evaluate whether someone's opinion on your work has genuine credibility?
How does keeping a broad, global perspective protect us from local peer pressure and rigid stereotypes?
★ How has your personal perspective on success changed over the last five years?
Is it possible to remain completely immune to others' judgment without becoming cold and socially isolated?
★ If you had to describe your relationship with social criticism in one word, what would it be?
🟣 Let's Speak Together : The Feedback Spectrum 📊▲

Discuss with your partner: Whose opinion or criticism is most difficult to filter out and ignore? Rank them:

👥 Core Family & Parents
When their expectations clash directly with your unconventional choices, testing your self-worth.
🏢 Professional Bosses & Clients
When their critical feedback affects your career stability, demanding a high level of professional resilience.
🌐 Online Strangers & Netizens
When random reviews or comments target your creative work, requiring complete emotional detachment.
🎒 Close Peers & Friends
When your social circle subtly judges your unconventional style, tempting you to conform to avoid isolation.

Use the Second Conditional (if my parents criticized... I would feel...) to justify your rankings!

🟢 Round 2 : Speculating on the Future▲
In the future, technology will allow us to measure a critic's credibility and bias with mathematical precision before we read their feedback. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How would you adapt if social media algorithms began to score your individual self-worth?
Modern digital smart devices will eventually destroy our collective capacity for deep resilience to criticism. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you use brain-boosting chips to artificially stay immune to social judgment?
Future school systems will completely eliminate competitive grades, celebrating only a child's unique identity. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think future kids will be more focused on their inner values than seeking peer validation?
As artificial intelligence advances, humans will rely entirely on machine advice for making career and relationship choices. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If you could design a futuristic school, how would you protect kids from online peer influence pressure?
The survival of human civilization will depend on our collective willingness to stay true to our values and reject mass judgment. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How do you personally prepare for a future where career feedback loops are fully automated?
In the coming decades, a truly valuable perspective on science will be determined by software predictions rather than human logic. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If you could send a single message about the power of self-worth to people living 100 years in the future, what would it be?
Global digital connectivity will make it incredibly difficult to protect your child's self-image from continuous online criticism. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you choose to live in a futuristic community that completely guarantees children's safety at the cost of their freedom?
In the future, social status will be determined by a person's capacity for deep resilience rather than wealth or career speed. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What role do you hope your personal perspective will play in your own professional future over the next decade?
One day, being passionately focused and listening to your inner values will be seen as the single most valuable trait of any leader, making external validation obsolete. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ In what area of your life do you feel you need to practice greater emotional detachment right now?
A future world governed fully by smart logic will leave no space left for the beautiful struggle of building self-worth through mistakes. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If an app could guarantee you would never feel insecurity, would you still value human guidance?
🏁 Closing Circle: Complete the Sentence▲

Finish the session by going in a circle. Each student must complete the following sentence with exactly one adjective or short phrase using the vocabulary learned:

"Constructive advice is..."

Examples: invaluable, based on credibility, supportive of self-worth, building resilience, better than raw judgment.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
He have went to Paris → He has gone to Paris (Present perfect third-person singular 'has' + past participle 'gone'.)
We discussed about the problem → We discussed the problem / had a discussion about the problem ('Discuss' is transitive and takes no preposition, or use 'have a discussion about'.)
I am agree with you → I agree with you (Use 'agree' directly as a verb rather than using it with the auxiliary 'am'.)
