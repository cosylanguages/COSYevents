Theme

Childhood & Personal Gifts

Alisa Freindlich is the person who captivated generations of theatre and cinema lovers with her unparalleled grace and profound dramatic intensity, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "The longer we keep our inner child alive, the longer we keep the natural gifts we were born with." Why is it that growing up so often feels like a slow, quiet betrayal of who we once were? Do we bury our childhood curiosity to survive the expectations of the adult world, or can we choose to keep that early magic alive? Join us as we explore the art of preserving our natural talents.

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"The longer we keep our inner child alive, the longer we keep the natural gifts we were born with." : Alisa Freindlich

📖 Session Vocabulary

Inner child
a person's childhood self, especially regarded as an active part of their adult personality.
Connecting with your inner child can provide deep insights during emotional therapy.
Natural
existing in or caused by nature; not made or caused by humankind.
He has a natural ability to make people feel comfortable and safe.
Talent
natural aptitude or skill.
Her musical talent was discovered by her grandmother when she was only five years old.
Alive
living, active, or full of spirit.
Performing on stage is the only activity that makes her feel truly alive.
Gift
a natural ability or talent.
He possessed a rare gift for understanding complex mathematical patterns easily.
Preserve
maintain something in its original or existing state.
It is difficult to preserve our emotional innocence in a highly competitive society.
Curiosity
a strong desire to know or learn something new.
Nurturing childhood curiosity is the most important task of modern education.
Sincerity
the quality of being free from pretense, deceit, or hypocrisy.
The total sincerity in her voice convinced everyone that she was telling the truth.
Imagination
the faculty or action of forming new ideas, or images of external objects.
Children possess a vivid imagination that should never be suppressed by rigid rules.
Wonder
a feeling of surprise mingled with admiration, caused by something beautiful.
The beautiful mountain view filled the young travelers with a sense of wonder.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What childhood memory or game makes you smile immediately when you think of it?
  • Do you agree that adult life forces us to become too serious and predictable?
🔵 Round 1 : Analytical Discussion▲
How does the pursuit of modern success influence our relationship with our inner child?
★ When was the last time you did something purely for spontaneous play without planning any results?
Is it possible to find true balance when our connection with the natural world is completely absent?
★ How do you personally nurture your relationship with nature when you feel mentally exhausted?
Why do some societies struggle to cultivate and appreciate individual talent in children?
★ Who in your childhood best recognized your personal talents, and what did they teach you?
How does embracing childhood playfulness change the way we feel fully alive as adults?
★ What creative or physical activity makes you feel most alive and energized?
Can a natural gift act as a silent shield against the demands of social conformity?
★ Have you ever had to choose between conforming to social rules and protecting your personal gifts?
How do our early school experiences shape our ability to preserve our authenticity as adults?
※ How has your personal understanding of your true self shifted over the last five years?
In what ways does rigid logical thinking challenge our childhood curiosity?
★ What scientific or artistic topic do you feel most curious about right now?
Why do adults often find it difficult to express their emotions with absolute sincerity?
★ Have you ever felt a deep sense of conflict regarding being completely honest in a relationship?
Is our creative imagination a quality of quiet solitude, or does it thrive in busy communities?
★ Where do you find your primary source of creative inspiration when you write or draw?
What is the most profound lesson that maintaining a sense of wonder can teach us about our own nature?
★ If you had to describe your personal experience with beauty and wonder in one word, what would it be?
🟣 Let's Speak Together▲

A comparative reflection on our habits and self-perceptions.

🟢 Round 2 : Philosophical Application▲
In the future, technology will enable us to digitize and share our experience of the inner child directly. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How would you personally adapt if childhood playfulness became entirely obsolete in tomorrow's world?
The rapid speed of modern urbanization will eventually destroy our connection with the natural world. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you be willing to live in a futuristic indoor eco-dome that completely guarantees a safe environment?
Future educational systems will prioritize the cultivation of individual talent over traditional academic skills. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If you could design a futuristic school, how would you teach the value of personal talents to children?
As artificial intelligence advances, machines will exhibit a superior capacity to make humans feel fully alive. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If a machine could perfectly replicate your favorite creative experience, would you still value it?
In the coming decades, possessing a natural gift will become a highly commercialized luxury commodity. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you fear a future where human creativity is fully managed or monitored by digital algorithms?
The survival of human civilization will depend on our collective ability to preserve our emotional authenticity. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How do you want future generations of your family to remember your relationship with your true self?
Future generations will look back at our current suppression of childhood curiosity with deep skepticism. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If you could send a single message about learning to people living 100 years in the future, what would it be?
Global digital connectivity will make true sincerity incredibly difficult to maintain in the future. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you choose to live in a futuristic digital utopia that completely guarantees honesty at the cost of your privacy?
In the future, social status will be determined by a person's capacity for creative imagination rather than wealth. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What role do you hope creative hobbies will play in your own personal future over the next decade?
A world fully governed by logic will have no space left for the mystery of wonder. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you choose to live in a perfectly predictable world if it guaranteed complete safety?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
We discussed about the inner child. → We discussed the inner child / had a discussion about the inner child. (Underlined error: 'discuss' is transitive and does not take the preposition 'about')
I am agree with Alisa Freindlich. → I agree with Alisa Freindlich. (Underlined error: use 'agree' as a main verb rather than with the auxiliary verb 'am')
I feel nostalgic about my childhood. → I feel nostalgic about my childhood / have nostalgia for my childhood. (This is correct! Good usage of 'nostalgic about')
