      personal: ★ Have you ever watched a performance that was technically perfect but lacked emotional warmth?

    right: It's the opposite of making art

  - wrong: I am agree with the quote
    right: I agree with the quote

  - wrong: The machine don't takes risks
    right: The machine doesn't take risks
    note: (Third person singular negative)
