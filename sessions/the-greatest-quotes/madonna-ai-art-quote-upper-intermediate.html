Madonna is the person who redefined pop culture and pushed the boundaries of artistic freedom for decades, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "Algorithms and AI are the opposite of taking risks..." Why do we crave digital perfection yet feel completely untouched by it? If art is born from human error and emotional danger, does a flawless algorithm ultimately rob us of our ability to feel? Let us debate the boundary between automated brilliance and human soul.
