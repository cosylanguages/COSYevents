Anonymous is the person who represents the collective, quiet wisdom of observers who find deep meaning in the fabric of everyday life, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "Do you think so, or were you told to think so?" How do we find a sense of place and belonging in a fast-paced, shifting world? Is home a geographic space, or is it a specific time frozen in our memory? Let us explore how we perceive time, direction, and the lives of those around us.

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"Do you think so, or were you told to think so?" : Anonymous

📖 Session Vocabulary

Influence
the capacity or power to have an effect on the character, development, or behavior of someone.
Social media algorithms have a massive influence on the opinions of young teenagers.
Independent
free from outside control; capable of thinking, deciding, and acting entirely on one's own.
Feynman argued that an independent thinker is highly valuable in a world of standardized education.
Opinion
a view, belief, or judgment formed about something, not necessarily based on objective fact.
In a polarized environment, people often mistake a strong personal opinion for absolute truth.
To shape
to strongly influence, form, or determine the development of a person's beliefs and thoughts.
Our parents and childhood environments do a lot to shape our core ethical values.
Propaganda
biased, misleading, or highly emotional information used to promote a specific political point of view.
Strict governments use media propaganda to prevent citizens from thinking independently.
Critical
expressing analytical, objective, and reasoned judgments regarding facts, data, or opinions.
Developing a critical mind is indispensable if you want to detect biases in daily news feeds.
Conditioning
the process of training, habituating, or influencing a person to behave and think in a certain way.
Many of our consumer choices are the result of continuous, subtle social conditioning.
Authority
the official power or right to give orders, enforce obedience, or make rules.
A nonconformist learner refuses to accept ideas simply because they are declared by an authority.
To conform
to adjust your behavior, views, or lifestyle to match the accepted standards of a social group.
The pressure to conform makes it difficult for individuals to express their original, unorthodox thoughts.
To question
to feel or express doubt about something; to challenge an established assumption or dogma.
It is our duty as active learners to question everything we read on the internet.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • How often do you question the source of your opinions?
  • Is it possible to have a completely independent thought in the digital age?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Passive Voice with Reporting Verbs▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Use passive voice with reporting verbs (it is said, is believed to be, is thought that) to discuss public opinions, social conditioning, and rumors objectively.

Tap a word chip below, then tap any empty bracket [_____] to place it!

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: When using reporting verbs in the passive, we can use 'It is + _____ + that clause' or 'Subject + is/are + past participle + _____'.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. It is _____ that propaganda can easily alter public perception.
  2. The media is often _____ to manipulate societal values.
  3. Many people are _____ to accept opinions without questioning them.
  4. It is _____ that social media algorithms reinforce biases.
  5. Human cognitive biases are _____ to hinder independent logic.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

Is most of what we believe to be true just reported to us by others? How can we separate facts from beliefs?

🟣 Let's Speak Together : The Opinion Matrix 🧠▲

Discuss with your partner: In which scenario is it most difficult to think independently and resist social conditioning? Rank them:

🏢 Corporate Meetings
When your boss and colleagues all share an opinion that you believe is completely wrong.
🌐 Digital Timelines
When social media algorithms continuously flood your feed with highly emotional, biased news.
🏡 Family Traditions
When your parents' traditional values and expectations clash directly with your unorthodox choices.
👥 Core Friendship Circles
When you must hide your authentic views to stay accepted and avoid conflict in your close social group.

Use passive reporting verbs (it is said that..., conformity is believed to be...) to justify your rankings!

🏁 Closing Circle: Complete the Sentence▲

Finish the session by going in a circle. Each student must complete the following sentence with exactly one adjective or short phrase using the vocabulary learned:

"To think for yourself is to..."

Examples: question authority, resist conditioning, express an independent opinion, refuse to conform, seek the real truth.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I studies in an independent way→I study in an independent manner(First-person verb agreement, use 'manner' to match original quote)
He discussed about the opinion→He discussed the opinion('Discuss' is transitive and does not take 'about')
I am agree with Feynman→I agree with Feynman('Agree' is a verb, not an adjective)
