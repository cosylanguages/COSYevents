Theme

Art & Freedom

Voltaire is the person who defended free thought, tolerance, and the simple, harmless pleasures of life against rigid dogmas, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "Let us read, and let us dance, those two amusements will never do any harm to the world." Why does society so often suspect the very activities that bring us harmless joy? If reading and dancing are the ultimate forms of mental and physical freedom, how do they help us resist rigid social dogmas? Let us celebrate the liberating power of simple pleasures.

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"Let us see how many interpretations exist! There is never only one correct answer to any profound human question." : Oscar Wilde

📖 Session Vocabulary

Artistry
creative skill or ability in a fine art or literature.
The novel is praised for its exceptional prose and delicate artistry.
Interpretation
the action of explaining the meaning of something.
There are many different interpretations of this complex, symbolic painting.
Aesthetics
a set of principles concerned with the nature and appreciation of beauty.
The cafe's minimalist aesthetics created a calm, relaxing atmosphere.
Inspiration
the process of being mentally stimulated to do or feel something creative.
Nature has always been the primary source of inspiration for poets.
Expression
the action of making known one's thoughts or feelings.
Music is a powerful form of self-expression for people of all ages.
Imagination
the faculty or action of forming new ideas, or images of external objects.
Children possess a vivid imagination that should never be suppressed.
Paraphrase
express the meaning of something using different words.
To check your understanding, try to paraphrase the author's main argument.
Sincerity
the quality of being free from pretense, deceit, or hypocrisy.
The absolute sincerity of his work made it deeply moving for readers.
Ambiguity
the quality of being open to more than one interpretation.
The poem's rich ambiguity allows each reader to find their own meaning.
Originality
the ability to think independently and creatively.
The artist was celebrated for the fresh originality of her style.

  • How do books and dance help you relax after a stressful week?

Master when to use a gerund (verb-ing) or an infinitive (to + verb) after verbs commonly associated with creative expression (creative, risk, enjoy, continue, stop, recommend).

How do reading and physical movement combine to refine our personal aesthetics and appreciation of life?

🟢 Round 2 : Philosophical Application▲
In the future, digitized entertainment will diminish our capacity to appreciate the simple artistry of reading and dancing. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you participate in virtual reality dancing if it felt identical to real-world artistry?
Future societies will view literary interpretation as a vital defense against algorithmic superficiality. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you rely on AI to summarize books if it saved time for personal interpretation?
Modern life prioritizes digital efficiency over the gentle aesthetics of reading and physical dance. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think your environment's aesthetics encourage you to read more often?
Algorithmic recommendations will eventually replace organic inspiration drawn from books and music. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Have you ever felt that a digital recommendation gave you genuine inspiration?
Physical dance will remain the ultimate unfiltered form of human expression in an automated world. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you feel comfortable dancing in public as a free form of expression?
Excessive screen time actively stifles the natural imagination cultivated by immersive reading. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you worry that modern technology is weakening your own imagination?
Future generations will prefer to paraphrase classic literature rather than reading full original texts. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you read a shorter version of a classic novel if you didn't have time to paraphrase it yourself?
Genuine sincerity in art will become a rare commodity in an era dominated by viral trends. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Can you easily detect a lack of sincerity in popular media today?
Embracing ambiguity in leisure activities leads to greater psychological resilience and open-mindedness. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How do you react when a story ends with complete ambiguity?
True originality is impossible without first immersing oneself in classic literature and culture. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you believe your tastes in music and books reflect true originality?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
He have went to Paris → He has gone to Paris (Present perfect third-person singular 'has' + past participle 'gone'.)
We discussed about the problem → We discussed the problem / had a discussion about the problem ('Discuss' is transitive and takes no preposition, or use 'have a discussion about'.)
I am agree with you → I agree with you (Use 'agree' directly as a verb rather than using it with the auxiliary 'am'.)
