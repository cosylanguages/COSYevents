Duration

Theme

Personality & Non-conformity

Anonymous is the person who represents the collective, quiet wisdom of observers who find deep meaning in the fabric of everyday life, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "I will always prefer weird people with big dreams and poetic personalities." How do we find a sense of place and belonging in a fast-paced, shifting world? Is home a geographic space, or is it a specific time frozen in our memory? Let us explore how we perceive time, direction, and the lives of those around us.

☕

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"Let us see how many interpretations exist! There is never only one correct answer to any profound human question." : Oscar Wilde

📖 Session Vocabulary

Weird
very strange, unusual, or eccentric, but in a highly creative or fascinating way.
He had some weird habits, like writing poetry on train tickets, but everyone loved his spirit.
Dreams
cherished ambitions, great future aspirations, or imaginative goals.
Living without big dreams makes daily life feel incredibly dry and repetitive.
Poetic
having an imaginative, beautiful, and deeply artistic quality or personality.
She had a poetic view of the world, seeing beauty in ordinary rainy afternoons.
Ordinary
normal, standard, or usual; lacking unique, special, or exciting qualities.
Following a highly ordinary routine can feel incredibly safe but boring.
Conformity
behavior in strict accordance with accepted conventions, rules, or social standards.
Society demands complete conformity, which often kills natural artistic talent.
Passionate
showing, having, or caused by intense feelings, excitement, or beliefs.
A passionate dancer will practice for hours purely for the joy of movement.
Creativity
the use of original imagination or unique ideas to create something artistic.
Unusual habits and eccentric thinking are often the true fuel of deep creativity.
Unique
being the only one of its kind; unlike anything or anyone else.
Her voice was completely unique, sounding nothing like standard radio pop singers.
Inspiration
the process of being mentally stimulated to feel, think, or create something artistic.
He found his greatest inspiration in the quiet, weird corners of the old city.
Authentic
genuine, real, and true to one's own eccentric character, desires, and identity.
Being authentic means accepting your weirdness even when others laugh at you.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • How do you define a 'normal' person, and is there actually any such thing?
  • What is a unique or unusual belief that you secretly hold?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Second Conditional▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Use the second conditional (if + past, would + base verb) to speculate about hypothetical relationships, trust, and eccentricity.

🧩 Interactive Word Bank:

Tap a word chip below, then tap any empty bracket [_____] to place it!

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: In the Second Conditional, the if-clause uses the _____ tense, and the main clause uses _____ or _____ + base verb.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. If we _____ each other more, we would feel less vulnerable.
  2. I _____ my secrets if you promised to remain silent.
  3. If they _____ here, they would offer unconditional support.
  4. We _____ a deep connection if we stopped pretending.
  5. What _____ if someone betrayed your absolute trust?
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

If you could meet someone with absolute empathy and sincerity, what deep thoughts would you share with them?

🔵 Round 1 : Analysis▲
Why do you think some people are deeply drawn to those who are labeled as weird or eccentric?
★ Have you ever had a weird habit or hobby that you tried to hide from others?
How does having massive, idealistic dreams change the way a person handles routine struggles?
★ What is a big, poetic dream you have held close to your heart since childhood?
What does it mean to possess a truly poetic view of our daily, often boring world?
★ Do you find beauty in ordinary, quiet places, or do you prefer dramatic events?
Why does modern society place so much pressure on kids to follow strict, ordinary paths?
★ Did your family expect you to follow ordinary careers, or did they support your eccentric ideas?
How does general social conformity act as a silent shield that kills our natural eccentricity?
★ Have you ever chosen conformity over your own unique personality just to avoid an argument?
Can a person build genuine creativity if they are constantly trying to fit in with peers?
★ Where do you go or what do you do when you need to spark your creative inspiration?
Why are highly passionate people sometimes viewed as difficult or exhausting to be around?
★ What topic or hobby can you talk about passionately for hours without getting tired?
How can we remaining authentic to our own eccentric nature when faced with heavy criticism?
★ Can you share a moment where being authentic required you to act in a highly unusual way?
Is having a completely unique style or voice a blessing, or does it bring social isolation?
★ What is the most unique trait or characteristic of your closest friend?
How does finding creative inspiration in weird places help people build an authentic identity?
★ If you had to describe your personal relationship with creativity in one word, what would it be?
🟣 Let's Speak Together : The Eccentricity Spectrum 🎭▲

Discuss with your partner: Which "weird" quality is most valuable for a poetic personality? Rank them:

🌌 The Silent Dreamer
Has massive, impossible dreams, lives quietly in their imagination, and writes beautiful poetry.
🎭 The Loud Eccentric
Rejects conformity loudly, dresses in unique patterns, and openly celebrates their weird habits.
🔬 The Mad Scientist
Possesses intense focus, ignores ordinary social rules, and is passionately curious about narrow topics.
🎒 The Nomadic Explorer
Travels constantly without a plan, seeks inspiration in foreign cultures, and lives an authentic life.

Use the Second Conditional (if I were... I would...) and vocabulary words to explain your views!

🟢 Round 2 : Speculating on the Future▲
In the future, technology will allow us to instantly share our poetic view of the world with others. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How would you adapt if society began to enforce absolute conformity through digital scores?
Modern smart devices will eventually destroy our collective capacity for massive, imaginative dreams. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you use futuristic brain chips to boost your natural creativity?
Future school systems will completely eliminate grades, celebrating only highly unique individual projects. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you think future kids will be more weird and eccentric than today's children?
As artificial intelligence advances, humans will find their greatest inspiration in completely non-logical, poetic ideas. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If you could design a futuristic museum, how would you teach the value of inspiration to children?
In the coming decades, living a highly ordinary, quiet lifestyle will become an expensive luxury. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How do you personally prepare for a future where ordinary routines are fully automated?
The survival of human civilization will depend on our collective willingness to stay authentic and reject mass media. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What is one authentic human trait you hope future generations never lose?
Global digital connectivity will make true unique personalities incredibly difficult to maintain. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you choose to live in a futuristic city that guarantees complete physical safety at the cost of your creativity?
In the future, social status will be determined by how passionate a person is rather than their bank balance. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What role do you hope creativity will play in your own personal future over the next decade?
One day, being a highly weird employee who questions established rules will be seen as the ultimate sign of leadership. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ In what area of your life do you feel you need to practice greater passionate defiance right now?
A future world governed fully by logical logic will have no space left for the quiet mystery of dreams. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ If a machine could perfectly replicate your poetic feelings, would you still value them?
🏁 Closing Circle: Complete the Sentence▲

Finish the session by going in a circle. Each student must complete the following sentence with exactly one adjective or short phrase using the vocabulary learned:

"A weird person is..."

Examples: full of dreams, poetic, passionate, highly unique, authentic, a source of inspiration, far from ordinary.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
He have went to Paris → He has gone to Paris (Present perfect third-person singular 'has' + past participle 'gone'.)
We discussed about the problem → We discussed the problem / had a discussion about the problem ('Discuss' is transitive and takes no preposition, or use 'have a discussion about'.)
I am agree with you → I agree with you (Use 'agree' directly as a verb rather than using it with the auxiliary 'am'.)
