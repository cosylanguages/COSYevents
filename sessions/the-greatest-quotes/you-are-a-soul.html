C.S. Lewis is the person who explored the boundaries of faith, mythology, and the transcendent nature of the human soul, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "You don't have a soul. You are a soul. You have a body." What happens when we stop treating our body as our entire identity and start seeing it as merely a vessel for the soul? Does this shift help us find deeper purpose in a highly material world? Join us for an inspiring metaphysical discussion.

"You don't have a soul. You are a soul. You have a body." : C.S. Lewis

📖 Session Vocabulary

Essence
the intrinsic, core nature or indispensable quality of a living being.
Many spiritual teachers believe that kindness is the true essence of the human soul.
Consciousness
the state of being awake, self-aware, and cognizant of one's own existence.
The mystery of human consciousness continues to baffle materialist scientists.
Materialism
the tendency to value physical comfort and possessions over spiritual or intellectual growth.
Extreme materialism often leads to a sense of inner emptiness, regardless of physical wealth.
Transcendent
beyond or above the limits of normal physical or sensory human experience.
A near-death experience can provide a transcendent glimpse into life beyond the physical body.
Spirituality
the quality of being concerned with the human spirit or soul as opposed to material things.
Personal spirituality can provide a deep source of hope during intense life challenges.
Incarnation
the physical embodiment of a spiritual being or abstract soul in a fleshly form.
According to Lewis, the body is just a temporary incarnation of our eternal self.
Perception
the way in which something is regarded, understood, or interpreted through the senses.
Our limited physical perception makes it difficult to see the intangible aspects of reality.
Existential
relating to human existence, purpose, and the conscious search for meaning.
Realizing that you are a soul can trigger a profound existential shift in your priorities.
Intangible
incapable of being touched or physically grasped; lacking a physical presence.
The most valuable elements of our identity, such as love and memories, are completely intangible.
Ethereal
extremely delicate and light in a way that seems spiritual rather than physical.
She felt a quiet, ethereal peace while contemplating the silent mystery of the night sky.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • When do you feel most "disconnected" from your physical body?
  • Do you think of your mind and your body as two separate things, or as one unit?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Subjunctive Mood for Deep Reflection▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Advanced (C1))

Use the subjunctive mood (I wish I were, it is essential that he be, if only she had) to express hypothetical wishes, urgent recommendations, or regrets about life.

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: After verbs of demanding, recommending or wishing, the subjunctive uses the _____ of the verb (e.g. 'be', 'understand'). For hypothetical regrets about the past, use past modal + _____ + past participle.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. It is essential that we _____ true to ourselves rather than convenient.
  2. I wish our society _____ more focused on spiritual growth.
  3. If only he _____ his vulnerability before it was too late.
  4. They recommended that she _____ the nature of her soul in silence.
  5. We demanded that the organization _____ individual human dignity.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

If you could wish for one permanent shift in human consciousness, what would you wish for? Use the subjunctive mood.

🟣 Let's Speak Together : The Essence of Self: Hypothetical Scenarios 🌌▲

Discuss with your partner: In which scenario is a person closest to experiencing their true "soul"? Rank them from most spiritual to most material:

🪐 Digital Transcendence
Uploading your entire intangible consciousness into a digital, virtual sky, leaving all physical bodies behind.
🌿 Solitary Contemplation
Living in complete, silent isolation, contemplating the ethereal mystery of the universe without physical distractions.
🏛️ Material Perfection
Surrounding yourself with physical beauty, gourmet food, and absolute sensory comfort in a material paradise.
🧬 Temporary Incarnation
Constantly changing physical bodies, experiencing many different human and animal lives over centuries.

Use the subjunctive mood (I wish, it is essential that) to justify your views!

🏁 Closing Circle: Complete the Sentence▲

Finish the session by going in a circle. Each student must complete the following sentence with exactly one adjective or short phrase using the vocabulary learned:

"My body is a container, but I am..."

Examples: an intangible consciousness, an eternal essence, a transcendent being, a quiet spirituality, an ethereal soul.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
You don't have a soul, you are a soul→You don't have a soul; you are a soul(Avoid comma splices by using a semicolon or breaking into two sentences)
I am agree with C.S. Lewis→I agree with C.S. Lewis('Agree' is a verb, not an adjective)
I am not believing in souls→I don't believe in souls(State verbs are not used in continuous forms)
It depend on you→It depends on you(Third person singular requires '-s')
