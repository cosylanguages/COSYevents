Theme

Technology & Cognitive Independence

Anonymous is the person who represents the collective, quiet wisdom of observers who find deep meaning in the fabric of everyday life, and in this session they focus on the following quote: "We should turn off AI for a week to see if people's brains still work." How do we find a sense of place and belonging in a fast-paced, shifting world? Is home a geographic space, or is it a specific time frozen in our memory? Let us explore how we perceive time, direction, and the lives of those around us.

☕ The Philosophers' Ledger: Café Debates

"Let us see how many interpretations exist! There is never only one correct answer to any profound human question." : Oscar Wilde

📖 Session Vocabulary

Artificial
made or produced by human beings rather than occurring naturally.
Relying on artificial intelligence might weaken our natural critical thinking.
Automation
the use of automatic equipment or systems to do work.
The complete automation of writing could make students forget how to compose essays.
Reliance
dependency on or trust in someone or something.
Our heavy reliance on digital navigators has ruined our ability to read physical maps.
Cognitive
relating to the mental processes of perception, memory, and judgment.
Outsourcing simple calculations to apps can affect our cognitive abilities.
Brain
the organ of the body in the head that controls thought and feeling.
The brain is like a muscle that needs regular exercise to stay strong and healthy.
Detachment
the state of being objective, distant, or disconnected.
Taking a week of digital detachment helps us regain mental clarity.
Habits
settled or regular tendencies or practices that are hard to give up.
Checking our phones immediately upon waking is one of the most common habits.
Algorithm
a process or set of rules followed by a computer in calculations.
The search engine algorithm decides what information we see first.
Outsourcing
the practice of obtaining services or solving tasks from an external source.
The outsourcing of simple decisions to software makes life effortless but passive.
Process
a series of actions or steps taken in order to achieve a particular end.
Learning is a slow process that cannot be bypassed by automated summaries.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What is the last thing you asked an AI to write or solve for you?
  • Could you survive a full week without using any internet search engines?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Passive Voice with Reporting Verbs▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Intermediate (B1))

Use passive voice with reporting verbs (it is said, is believed to be, is thought that) to discuss public opinions, social conditioning, and rumors objectively.

📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: When using reporting verbs in the passive, we can use 'It is + _____ + that clause' or 'Subject + is/are + past participle + _____'.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. It is _____ that propaganda can easily alter public perception.
  2. The media is often _____ to manipulate societal values.
  3. Many people are _____ to accept opinions without questioning them.
  4. It is _____ that social media algorithms reinforce biases.
  5. Human cognitive biases are _____ to hinder independent logic.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

Is most of what we believe to be true just reported to us by others? How can we separate facts from beliefs?

In a future world fully governed by artificial agents, human intuition will be completely ignored. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How would you adapt if artificial systems became entirely obsolete in tomorrow's world?
The rapid speed of modern society will eventually make manual labor and the refusal of automation highly respected practices. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Would you use technology to artificially automate your personal creative hobbies?
Future schools will prioritize personal independence over digital reliance. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How do you want future generations of your family to remember your relationship with digital reliance?

★ If you could design a futuristic school, how would you teach cognitive skills to children?

★ Do you fear a future where brain activity is fully managed or monitored by digital algorithms?
The survival of human civilization will depend on our collective ability to practice digital detachment. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ How do you personally prepare for a future where detachment is increasingly rare?

★ If you could send a single message about habits to people living 100 years in the future, what would it be?

★ Would you choose to live in a futuristic utopia that completely guarantees comfort at the cost of your freedom?

★ What role do you hope outsourcing will play in your own personal future over the next decade?

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
He have went to Paris → He has gone to Paris (Present perfect third-person singular 'has' + past participle 'gone'.)
We discussed about the problem → We discussed the problem / had a discussion about the problem ('Discuss' is transitive and takes no preposition, or use 'have a discussion about'.)
I am agree with you → I agree with you (Use 'agree' directly as a verb rather than using it with the auxiliary 'am'.)
