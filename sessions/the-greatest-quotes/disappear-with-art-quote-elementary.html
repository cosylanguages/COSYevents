Quiet Living & Simple Habits

As we grow older, many people prefer quiet hobbies like reading, listening to music, and spending peaceful time at home. In this Elementary (A2) session, we discuss simple pleasures, peaceful daily routines, and how art helps us relax after a busy day.

"The older you get the more you understand why people disappear with art, music, books and quiet living." : Anonymous

📖 Session Vocabulary

Quiet ≠ noisy
making little or no noise; peaceful and calm.
I enjoy a quiet morning with a cup of warm tea.
Disappear ≠ appear
to go away to a place where you cannot be seen or found.
He likes to disappear into his study to read historical novels.
Relax ≠ stress
to rest and become less active or worried.
Soft instrumental music helps me relax after work.
Hobby ≠ duty
an activity that you do for pleasure in your free time.
Painting watercolor landscapes is my favorite weekend hobby.
Peaceful ≠ chaotic
calm and quiet, without violence or noise.
Walking in the park gives me a peaceful feeling.
Prefer
to like one thing or person better than another.
Many adults prefer staying home to going to loud parties.
Routine ≠ chaos
a regular series of movements or actions.
Reading before bed is a healthy evening routine.
Simple ≠ complex
easy to understand, do, or use; uncluttered.
A simple lifestyle often brings great personal happiness.
Comfort ≠ hardship
a state of physical ease and freedom from pain or constraint.
Reading a book on a comfortable sofa gives me great joy.
Solitude ≠ crowd
the state of being alone, especially when this is pleasant.
Artists often need quiet solitude to create good paintings.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • Do you prefer reading books or watching movies in the evening?
  • What is your favorite quiet place in your city or home?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Comparative Structures (The older... the more...)▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Elementary (A2))

Use the + comparative adjective..., the + comparative adjective... to show how two changes happen together (e.g., The older you get, the more you appreciate quiet living).

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I am more old than before. → I am older than before. (Short adjectives take '-er' ending: older, not 'more old'.)
I like quietly places. → I like quiet places. (Use the adjective 'quiet' to modify nouns like 'places', not the adverb 'quietly'.)
It help me to relaxed. → It helps me to relax / It helps me relax. (Use the base verb 'relax' after 'to' or directly after 'help'.)
