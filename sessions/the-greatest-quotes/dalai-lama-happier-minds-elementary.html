☕ The Greatest Quotes Perspective

"Happiness is not something ready made. It comes from your own actions." — Dalai Lama. Discussing how daily choices, kindness, and mindfulness create true joy.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Happiness ≠ Sadness
the state of being happy.
Happiness comes from positive choices.
Compassion ≠ Cruelty
sympathetic pity and concern for the sufferings or misfortunes of others.
Showing compassion makes the world better.
Peace ≠ War
freedom from disturbance; tranquility.
Inner peace begins with a calm mind.
Action ≠ Inaction
the fact or process of doing something.
Kind actions create happy relationships.
Mindful ≠ Careless
conscious or aware of something.
Being mindful helps manage stress.
Kindness ≠ Unkindness
the quality of being friendly, generous, and considerate.
A small act of kindness brightens someone's day.
Wisdom ≠ Foolishness
the quality of having experience, knowledge, and good judgment.
Sharing wisdom helps younger generations.
Joy ≠ Sorrow
a feeling of great pleasure and happiness.
Spending time with family brings deep joy.
Calm ≠ Anxious
not showing or feeling nervousness, anger, or other strong emotions.
Deep breathing keeps your body calm.
Choice ≠ Coercion
an act of selecting or making a decision when faced with two or more possibilities.
Happiness is a daily personal choice.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up (10 mins)▲

Welcome! Share your initial thoughts on this topic. What personal experiences or observations come to mind right away?

⚡ Grammar Practice : Imperatives & Advice Structures (A2)▲
Use imperatives and advice phrases to share positive habits: "Practice compassion every day." / "You should take time to breathe deeply."
🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)▲
The Dalai Lama says happiness comes from our own actions.
★ What simple daily action brings you the most happiness?
Showing compassion to others creates inner peace.
★ How do you feel when you help someone in need?
Practicing kindness every day changes your mental outlook.
★ What is the kindest thing someone did for you recently?
Living a mindful life keeps your thoughts quiet and calm.
★ How do you relax when you feel busy or stressed?
Every morning offers a fresh choice to seek joy.
★ What is your favorite morning routine habit?
🟢 Round 2: Deep Speculation & Future Horizon (Units 6–10)▲
Can teaching compassion in primary schools reduce global conflict?
★ What lesson about kindness did you learn in childhood?
How does true wisdom differ from simply having lots of information?
★ Who is the wisest person you know in your personal life?
Why is inner peace more valuable than physical wealth?
★ What makes you feel peaceful on weekends?
How can modern technology support mindful living instead of distraction?
★ Do you use any meditation or mindfulness apps?
How does making a conscious choice for happiness impact health?
★ What empowering thought helps you start a good day?
