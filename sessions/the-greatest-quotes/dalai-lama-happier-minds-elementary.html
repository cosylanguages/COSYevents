🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)▲
The Dalai Lama says happiness comes from our own actions.
★ What simple daily action brings you the most happiness?
Showing compassion to others creates inner peace.
★ How do you feel when you help someone in need?
Practicing kindness every day changes your mental outlook.
★ What is the kindest thing someone did for you recently?
Living a mindful life keeps your thoughts quiet and calm.
★ How do you relax when you feel busy or stressed?
Every morning offers a fresh choice to seek joy.
★ What is your favorite morning routine habit?
