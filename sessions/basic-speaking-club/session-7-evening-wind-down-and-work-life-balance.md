# Session 7: Evening Wind-Down & Work-Life Balance

After work is when adults actually have time to talk. This session keeps the language small so the conversation can be long.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| evening | a dark sky with a lamp | EEV-ning |
| dinner | a plate at night | DIN-er |
| TV | a television | tee-VEE |
| walk | feet on a path | wawk |
| relax | a person on a sofa | ri-LAKS |
| tired | a yawning face | TIGH-erd |
| book | an open book | buk |
| tea | a cup with steam | tee |
| music | headphones | MYOO-zik |
| bed | a bed | bed |

Warm-up: Mime one thing you do after work. Your partner says it.

Say it: In the evening I watch TV. / I relax at home.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: What do you do in the evening? Is it good for you?

Model: For me, my evening is good because I walk and then relax.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- watch TV or read a book
- walk after dinner or sit on the sofa
- phone in bed or no phone in bed

Example: I prefer a book because the TV is too loud.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: invite a friend for dinner (15 min)

Student A invites Student B for dinner. B says yes and asks what to bring.

- Do you want to come for dinner?
- Yes, thank you. What time?
- Seven o'clock.
- What can I bring?
- Just bread. See you at seven.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Tonight, I will… / Next week, I want to…

Prompts: What will you do tonight? What do you want to do after work next week?

Model: Tonight I will walk for twenty minutes and go to bed early.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you will do tonight.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, my evening is good because I walk and then relax. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer a book because the TV is too loud. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Tonight I will walk for twenty minutes and go to bed early. |

Weaker group: cut the invitation to three lines and let B read them. Stronger group: B is busy tonight, so they have to find another day and time.
