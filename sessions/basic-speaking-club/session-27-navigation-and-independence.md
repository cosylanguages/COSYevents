# Session 27: Navigation & Independence

Left, right, straight, near, far: five words that turn a lost beginner into a person who can ask. Card 4 is a small map and a stranger.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| map | a city map | map |
| left | a hand pointing left | left |
| right | a hand pointing right | right |
| straight | an arrow forward | strayt |
| near | two buildings close | neer |
| far | two buildings with a road | far |
| street | a street with cars | street |
| bus stop | a bus stop sign | bus stop |
| ask | a hand up | ask |
| lost | a person with a map | lost |

Warm-up: Point left. Point right. Say the words. Your partner does the same.

Say it: Go left, then right. / It is near the park.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Do you use a map on your phone? Why?

Model: For me, the map is good because I am often lost.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- ask a person or use your phone
- walk or take a taxi
- go out in the day or at night

Example: I prefer to ask a person because it is faster.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: find the pharmacy (15 min)

Student B is lost. Student A lives in the area and gives directions.

- Excuse me, I am lost. Where is the pharmacy?
- Go straight, then left.
- Is it far?
- No, three minutes.
- Near the bank?
- Yes, next to the bank.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next week, I want to go to… / Tomorrow, I will walk to…

Prompts: Where do you want to go next week? Will you walk or take the bus?

Model: Next week I want to go to the new market, and I will walk there.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One direction word + one place you will go next week.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, the map is good because I am often lost. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer to ask a person because it is faster. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next week I want to go to the new market, and I will walk there. |

Weaker group: draw three streets on paper and let A point while speaking. Stronger group: A gives a longer route with four steps, and B repeats it back.
