# Session 13: Fashion & Identity

Clothes are vocabulary you wear. Card 4 is a changing room, which gives every student a reason to say "too big" and "too small".

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| shirt | a shirt | shert |
| shoes | a pair of shoes | shooz |
| jacket | a jacket | JAK-it |
| dress | a dress | dres |
| size | S, M, L labels | sighz |
| colour | three colour cards | KUL-er |
| wear | a person dressed | wair |
| buy | a bag from a shop | bigh |
| old | an old jumper | ohld |
| new | a new shirt | nyoo |

Warm-up: Look at your partner's shoes. Say the colour.

Say it: I wear black shoes. / This is my size.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Do clothes say something about a person? Why?

Model: For me, clothes say a lot because I wear a uniform.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- new clothes or old clothes
- bright colours or dark colours
- comfortable clothes or beautiful clothes

Example: I prefer comfortable clothes because I walk to work.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: buy a jacket (15 min)

Student A works in a clothes shop. Student B wants a jacket in the right size.

- I want this jacket.
- What size are you?
- Medium, please.
- Do you want to try it on?
- Yes, please. It is too small.
- Here is a large one.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next month, I want to buy… / Tomorrow, I will wear…

Prompts: What do you want to buy next month? What will you wear tomorrow?

Model: Next month I want to buy a warm jacket.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you will wear tomorrow.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, clothes say a lot because I wear a uniform. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer comfortable clothes because I walk to work. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next month I want to buy a warm jacket. |

Weaker group: write the three sizes on cards and let B hand one to A. Stronger group: the jacket is the last one and it is too expensive — B asks for something cheaper.
