Session 2: Work & Career Choices

22 January 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 2: Work & Career Choices

Ten work words, one opinion question, and a small conversation at a shop counter. No grammar lecture — just frames people can use on Monday.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

job office boss colleague money meeting computer email break start

Warm-up: Show your phone. Is your job in the phone? Point to one work app.

Say it: I work in a shop. / I start at nine.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Is your job good for you? Why?

Model: For me, my job is good because the people are nice.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • an office job or a job at home
  • a morning shift or an evening shift
  • a big company or a small company

Example: I prefer a morning shift because I sleep early.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: ask about a job

15 min

Student A has a small shop. Student B wants a job there. Ask about the hours and the money.

  • Hello. I want a job.
  • Are you open on Sunday?
  • What time do you start?
  • We start at nine.
  • How much is the pay?
  • OK, thank you. Goodbye.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: Next year, I want to… / Tomorrow, I will…

Prompts: What do you want to do next year? What will you do after work tomorrow?

Model: Next year I want to work in a bigger office.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: One word from today + one job you want.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

use the rung you are ready for
FunctionA0A0+A1
OpinionGood. Bad.I think it is good.For me, my job is good because the people are nice.
PreferenceThis. That.I prefer this one.I prefer a morning shift because I sleep early.
FutureTomorrow — home.Tomorrow I will go home.Next year I want to work in a bigger office.

Weaker group: use only three questions in Card 4 and write them on the board. Stronger group: the shop says no, so B asks about a different job — "Do you know a shop with a job?"
