# Session 8: Home & Living Preferences

Rooms and furniture give A0 speakers something to point at and name. Card 4 turns that into a phone call about a flat.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| flat | a block of flats | flat |
| house | a house with a door | hows |
| room | a bed in a room | room |
| kitchen | a cooker | KICH-en |
| bathroom | a shower | BATH-room |
| window | a window | WIN-doh |
| garden | grass and a tree | GAR-den |
| street | a street with cars | street |
| neighbour | two doors side by side | NAY-ber |
| keys | a key ring | keez |

Warm-up: Point to three things in this room. Name them.

Say it: There is a kitchen. / My flat is small.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Do you like your home? Why?

Model: For me, I like my home because it is quiet and near the bus.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- a flat or a house
- in the city centre or outside the city
- an old building or a new building

Example: I prefer an old building because the rooms are big.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: call about a flat (15 min)

Student A has a flat to rent. Student B calls and asks three questions.

- Hello. I want to see the flat.
- It has two rooms.
- Is it quiet?
- Yes, very quiet.
- How much is the rent?
- Six hundred euros a month.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next year, I want to… / This weekend, I will…

Prompts: Where do you want to live next year? What will you do at home this weekend?

Model: Next year I want to live in a flat near the park.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you want in your next home.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, I like my home because it is quiet and near the bus. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer an old building because the rooms are big. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next year I want to live in a flat near the park. |

Weaker group: write the rent on a paper card so the numbers are read, not invented. Stronger group: the flat has no lift and B lives on the fifth floor — B must ask about it.
