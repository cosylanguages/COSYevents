# Session 15: Hobbies & Personal Growth

Everyone has one thing they do for free. This session finds it, names it, and puts it in a sentence.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| hobby | a guitar | HOB-ee |
| music | headphones | MYOO-zik |
| guitar | a guitar | gi-TAR |
| sport | a ball | spawrt |
| dance | two people dancing | dans |
| read | an open book | reed |
| draw | a pencil and paper | draw |
| learn | a notebook | lern |
| class | people at desks | klas |
| free time | a clock and a cup | free tighm |

Warm-up: Mime your hobby. The group guesses the word.

Say it: My hobby is music. / I play the guitar.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: What do you do in your free time?

Model: For me, I draw in my free time because it is quiet.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- music or sport
- learn alone or learn in a class
- a free hobby or a hobby you pay for

Example: I prefer a class because I meet people.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: ask about a class (15 min)

Student A teaches a small guitar class. Student B wants to join.

- I want to learn guitar.
- We have a class on Tuesday.
- What time is it?
- At seven in the evening.
- How much is it?
- Twenty euros for one hour.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next year, I want to learn… / This weekend, I will…

Prompts: What do you want to learn next year? What will you do this weekend?

Model: Next year I want to learn to swim.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you want to learn.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, I draw in my free time because it is quiet. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer a class because I meet people. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next year I want to learn to swim. |

Weaker group: give B the three questions written on a card to read aloud. Stronger group: the Tuesday class is full, so A offers Thursday and B must choose.
