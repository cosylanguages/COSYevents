# Session 11: Restaurants & Social Eating

A0–A1 speaking club, 60 minutes, six cards. Every card carries one sentence frame,
and the chunk ladder on the table offers a harder version for anyone ready for it.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

Words: menu, waiter, table, water, order, bill, tip, cheap, expensive, delicious.

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Warm-up: "Look at the menu on your table.
Point to one food. What is it?" Frame: *It is a menu. / This is water.*

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad. / Too expensive.

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.
Question: "Is a restaurant good for you? Why?" Model: "For me, a restaurant is
good. The food is delicious, but it is too expensive."

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

Choices: eat at home or eat in a restaurant; a quiet restaurant or a busy one; pay
by card or pay cash. Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: order a meal (15 min)

A is the waiter, B is the customer. Order one drink and one dish, then ask for the bill.

- A table for two, please.
- Can I see the menu, please?
- I want the soup, please. *(A1: I would like the soup, please.)*
- One water, please. Is it cold?
- The bill, please.
- Here you are. / Thank you.

Demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair
performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Tonight, I will… / Next weekend, I want to eat…

Prompts: "What will you eat tonight? Where do you want to eat next weekend?"

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

Say one word from today + one place you want to eat next week. Board the three best
sentences, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not
interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | I think it is good because the food is cheap. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer a quiet restaurant. | I prefer a quiet restaurant because I can talk. |
| Future | Tonight — pasta. | Tonight I will cook pasta. | Next weekend I want to try the new place near the station. |

Weaker group: two choices in Card 3, and Card 4 may be read word for word.
Stronger group: the dish is finished, so the customer must choose again — "Do you
have soup? No? OK, I want the salad."
