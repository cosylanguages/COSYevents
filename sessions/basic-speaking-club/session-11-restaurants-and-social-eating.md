# Session 11: Restaurants & Social Eating

Ordering food is the most useful A0 conversation there is. Card 4 runs it twice, so both people get to be the waiter.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| menu | an open menu | MEN-yoo |
| waiter | a person with a plate | WAY-ter |
| table | a table with two chairs | TAY-bul |
| water | a glass of water | WAW-ter |
| order | a finger on a menu | OR-der |
| bill | a paper with a total | bil |
| tip | coins on a table | tip |
| cheap | a small price tag | cheep |
| expensive | a big price tag | ik-SPEN-siv |
| delicious | a happy face with a fork | di-LISH-us |

Warm-up: Look at the menu on your table. Point to one food. What is it?

Say it: It is a menu. / This is water. / I like soup.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Is a restaurant good for you? Why?

Model: For me, a restaurant is good because the food is delicious.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- eat at home or eat in a restaurant
- a quiet restaurant or a busy restaurant
- pay by card or pay cash

Example: I prefer a quiet restaurant because I can talk.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: order a meal (15 min)

Student A is the waiter, Student B is the customer. Order one drink and one dish, then ask for the bill.

- A table for two, please.
- Can I see the menu, please?
- I want the soup, please.
- One water, please. Is it cold?
- The bill, please.
- Here you are. Thank you.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Tonight, I will… / Next weekend, I want to eat…

Prompts: What will you eat tonight? Where do you want to eat next weekend?

Model: Tonight I will cook pasta. Next weekend I want to eat in a new restaurant.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word from today + one place you want to eat next week.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, a restaurant is good because the food is delicious. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer a quiet restaurant because I can talk. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Tonight I will cook pasta. Next weekend I want to eat in a new restaurant. |

Weaker group: use two choices in Card 3, and let Card 4 be read word for word. Stronger group: the dish is finished, so the customer must choose again — "Do you have soup? No? OK, I want the salad."
