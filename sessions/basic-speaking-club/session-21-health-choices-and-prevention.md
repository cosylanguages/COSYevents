# Session 21: Health Choices & Prevention

Health talk at A0 is a shopping list of small habits. Ten words, three choices, and a pharmacy conversation everyone will need eventually.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| sleep | a pillow | sleep |
| water | a glass of water | WAW-ter |
| fruit | an apple and a banana | froot |
| vegetables | green vegetables | VEJ-ti-buls |
| walk | feet on a path | wawk |
| doctor | a person in a white coat | DOK-ter |
| medicine | a pill box | MED-i-sin |
| tired | a yawning face | TIGH-erd |
| rest | a person on a sofa | rest |
| every day | a calendar with all days | EV-ree day |

Warm-up: Point to your body: head, stomach, throat. Say the words with your partner.

Say it: I sleep eight hours. / I drink water every day.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: What is good for your health? Why?

Model: For me, walking is good because it is free and easy.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- walk every day or go to the gym
- cook at home or order food
- sleep six hours or sleep eight hours

Example: I prefer to cook because restaurant food has too much salt.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: at the pharmacy (15 min)

Student A is the pharmacist. Student B has a headache.

- Good morning. Can I help you?
- I have a headache.
- Take this, two times a day.
- Before food or after food?
- After food. Drink water.
- How much is it?

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next week, I will… / This month, I want to…

Prompts: What will you do for your health next week? What do you want to change this month?

Model: Next week I will walk thirty minutes every day.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one small health goal.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, walking is good because it is free and easy. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer to cook because restaurant food has too much salt. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next week I will walk thirty minutes every day. |

Weaker group: give the customer a card with the symptom written on it. Stronger group: the pharmacy is out of the medicine, so A suggests a doctor and B books a time.
