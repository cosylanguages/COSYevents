Session 4: Family Roles & Responsibilities

5 February 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 4: Family Roles & Responsibilities

Family words are personal, so this session runs on photos and short answers. Card 4 is a friendly conversation, not a debate.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

mother father sister brother child cook clean help visit home

Warm-up: Show a photo on your phone. Who is in the photo? Say one word.

Say it: This is my sister. / She lives in my city.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Who cooks in your home?

Model: For me, I cook, and my sister cleans.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • a big family or a small family
  • cook at home or eat out
  • live with family or live alone

Example: I prefer a small family because it is quiet.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: ask about a family

15 min

Two friends talk about their families. Ask, answer, then swap.

  • Do you have brothers or sisters?
  • Yes, one sister.
  • Does she live here?
  • No, she lives in another city.
  • Do you visit your family?
  • Yes, every Sunday.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: This weekend, I will… / Next month, I want to…

Prompts: Who will you visit this weekend? What do you want to do with your family next month?

Model: This weekend I will visit my mother.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: One word + one person you will see this week.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

use the rung you are ready for
FunctionA0A0+A1
OpinionGood. Bad.I think it is good.For me, I cook, and my sister cleans.
PreferenceThis. That.I prefer this one.I prefer a small family because it is quiet.

Weaker group: write the six answers on the board so B can read them. Stronger group: add a follow-up — "Who cooks in her home?" — so the answers get longer.
