Session 17: City Life vs Small Town

7 May 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 17: City Life vs Small Town

Comparing two places is the first real opinion a beginner can defend. Card 4 adds a small map and a stranger.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

city town village park shop noisy quiet people walk live

Warm-up: Name one place near your home: a park, a shop, a café.

Say it: There is a park near my home.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Is a city good for you? Why?

Model: For me, the city is good because everything is near.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • a city or a small town
  • a park or a café
  • live near work or live far from work

Example: I prefer a small town because it is quiet.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: ask for directions

15 min

Student B is new in the city. Student A lives here. B wants the park.

  • Excuse me, where is the park?
  • Go left, then right.
  • Is it far?
  • No, five minutes.
  • Thank you very much.
  • You're welcome.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: Next year, I want to live… / This weekend, I will go to…

Prompts: Where do you want to live next year? Where will you go this weekend?

Model: Next year I want to live in a small town near the sea.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: One place word + one place you will go this weekend.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

use the rung you are ready for
FunctionA0A0+A1
OpinionGood. Bad.I think it is good.For me, the city is good because everything is near.
PreferenceThis. That.I prefer this one.I prefer a small town because it is quiet.
FutureTomorrow — home.Tomorrow I will go home.Next year I want to live in a small town near the sea.

Weaker group: draw a simple map with three streets and let A point while speaking. Stronger group: B asks for a second place (a pharmacy) and A must give a longer route.
