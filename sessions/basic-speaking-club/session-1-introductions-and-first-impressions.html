Session 1: Introductions & First Impressions

15 January 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 1: Introductions & First Impressions

Six cards, sixty minutes, ten words. Everyone speaks from the first minute, and every card carries a sentence frame you can read off the table.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

name hello friend country city job family hobby phone photo

Warm-up: Point to your name on your phone. Say it to your partner.

Say it: My name is Ana. / Nice to meet you.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Is your city friendly? Why?

Model: For me, my city is friendly because people say hello.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • a big city or a small town
  • talk to new people or stay with friends
  • a morning person or an evening person

Example: I prefer a small town because it is quiet.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: meet someone new

15 min

Student A is new at the club, Student B is a member. Say your name and ask two questions.

  • Hello. My name is…
  • Nice to meet you.
  • Where are you from?
  • I am from…
  • What is your job?
  • See you next week.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: Tomorrow, I will… / Next week, I want to…

Prompts: Who will you meet tomorrow? What do you want to do next week?

Model: Next week I want to meet a new friend.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: Say your name + one word you liked today.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

use the rung you are ready for
FunctionA0A0+A1
OpinionGood. Bad.I think it is good.For me, my city is friendly because people say hello.
PreferenceThis. That.I prefer this one.I prefer a small town because it is quiet.
FutureTomorrow — home.Tomorrow I will go home.Next week I want to meet a new friend.

Weaker group: let Card 4 be read word for word from the card. Stronger group: add a third question — "Do you live near here?"
