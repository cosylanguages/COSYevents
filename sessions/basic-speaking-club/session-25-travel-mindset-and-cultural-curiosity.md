# Session 25: Travel Mindset & Cultural Curiosity

Travel gives beginners a reason to ask questions. Card 4 is a hotel desk, where every sentence is short and useful.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| travel | a plane and a bag | TRAV-el |
| ticket | a paper ticket | TIK-it |
| hotel | a hotel at night | ho-TEL |
| map | a city map | map |
| food | a plate of food | food |
| photo | a camera | FO-to |
| language | letters from two alphabets | LANG-gwij |
| ask | a hand up | ask |
| city | tall buildings | SI-tee |
| help | two hands together | help |

Warm-up: Show one travel photo on your phone. Say where it is.

Say it: I have a reservation. / My name is Ana Silva.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Do you like travel? Why?

Model: For me, travel is good because I see new food and new streets.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- plan everything or no plan
- a hotel or a flat
- a city or the sea

Example: I prefer a flat because I can cook.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: check in at a hotel (15 min)

Student A works at a hotel. Student B has a reservation.

- Hello. I have a reservation.
- What is your name?
- Ana Silva.
- Yes, room twelve.
- What time is breakfast?
- From seven to ten.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next year, I want to travel to… / In August, I will…

Prompts: Where do you want to travel next year? What will you do there?

Model: Next year I want to travel to Italy and eat in small restaurants.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one place you want to see.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, travel is good because I see new food and new streets. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer a flat because I can cook. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next year I want to travel to Italy and eat in small restaurants. |

Weaker group: give B a reservation card with name, room and dates written on it. Stronger group: the room is not ready, so B has to ask for a place to leave the bags.
