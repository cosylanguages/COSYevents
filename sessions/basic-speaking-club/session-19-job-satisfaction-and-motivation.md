# Session 19: Job Satisfaction & Motivation

Talking about work at A0 means talking about feelings and small requests, not careers. Card 4 is asking for a day off.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| happy | a smiling face | HAP-ee |
| tired | a yawning face | TIGH-erd |
| boss | a person with a list | bos |
| team | people at a table | teem |
| money | notes and coins | MUN-ee |
| help | two hands together | help |
| learn | a notebook | lern |
| change | two arrows | chayn |
| day off | an empty calendar day | day of |
| start | a clock at nine | start |

Warm-up: Say one good thing and one bad thing about your job. One word each.

Say it: My job is good. / The money is bad.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Are you happy at work? Why?

Model: For me, I am happy at work because my team is friendly.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- a good team or good money
- the same job or a new job
- learn new things or do the same work

Example: I prefer a good team because the days are fast.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: ask for a day off (15 min)

Student B wants a day off. Student A is the boss and needs a reason.

- Can I have Friday off, please?
- Why?
- I have a family day.
- OK, but Saturday is busy.
- No problem. Thank you.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next year, I want to… / Next month, I will…

Prompts: What do you want to change next year? What will you do in your next job?

Model: Next year I want to change my job and start later.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you want to change at work.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, I am happy at work because my team is friendly. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer a good team because the days are fast. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next year I want to change my job and start later. |

Weaker group: give the boss three possible answers on the board to choose from. Stronger group: the boss says no, so B has to ask for a different day and give a better reason.
