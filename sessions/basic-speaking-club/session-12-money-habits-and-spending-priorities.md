# Session 12: Money Habits & Spending Priorities

Prices are numbers you already know. This session puts them to work at a shop counter, with no grammar beyond "how much".

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| money | notes and coins | MUN-ee |
| price | a price tag | prighs |
| cheap | a small price tag | cheep |
| expensive | a big price tag | ik-SPEN-siv |
| pay | a card in a machine | pay |
| card | a bank card | kard |
| cash | a banknote | kash |
| save | a jar with coins | sayv |
| spend | a hand with a bag | spend |
| bank | a bank building | bank |

Warm-up: Take out a coin or a note. Say how much it is.

Say it: It is five euros. / I pay by card.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Is it easy to save money? Why?

Model: For me, it is difficult because the city is expensive.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- pay by card or pay cash
- save the money or spend it
- buy the cheap one or buy the good one

Example: I prefer cash because I spend less.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: pay in a shop (15 min)

Student A works in a shop. Student B buys one thing and pays.

- Hello. How much is this?
- It is twelve euros.
- Can I pay by card?
- Yes, of course.
- Here you are.
- Thank you. Have a good day.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next month, I will… / This year, I want to…

Prompts: What will you save for next month? What do you want to buy this year?

Model: Next month I will save fifty euros for a new phone.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you will not buy this month.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, it is difficult because the city is expensive. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer cash because I spend less. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next month I will save fifty euros for a new phone. |

Weaker group: put three prices on the board and let B point to the one they want. Stronger group: the card machine is broken, so B has to pay cash and check the change.
