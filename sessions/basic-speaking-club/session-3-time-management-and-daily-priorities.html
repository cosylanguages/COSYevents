Session 3: Time Management & Daily Priorities

29 January 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 3: Time Management & Daily Priorities

Time words are the easiest words to use at A0: today, tomorrow, nine o'clock. This session turns them into appointments and plans.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

time clock early late busy free work dinner sleep plan

Warm-up: Look at your phone. What time is it now? Say the time to your partner.

Say it: It is nine o'clock. / I am busy today.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Are you busy every day? Why?

Model: For me, I am busy every day because I work and cook.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • a busy day or a free day
  • work in the morning or work in the evening
  • make a plan or no plan

Example: I prefer a free day because I can sleep.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: make an appointment

15 min

Student A works at a reception desk. Student B wants an appointment this week.

  • Hello. I want an appointment.
  • What day?
  • Monday, please.
  • What time?
  • Three o'clock.
  • OK. See you Monday.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: Tonight, I will… / Tomorrow, I want to…

Prompts: What will you do tonight? What do you want to do tomorrow morning?

Model: Tonight I will sleep early.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: One word + one time you will do something tomorrow.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

use the rung you are ready for
FunctionA0A0+A1
OpinionGood. Bad.I think it is good.For me, I am busy every day because I work and cook.
PreferenceThis. That.I prefer this one.I prefer a free day because I can sleep.
FutureTomorrow — home.Tomorrow I will go home.Tonight I will sleep early.

Weaker group: put three times on the board (nine, twelve, three) and let B point. Stronger group: Monday is full, so the receptionist offers Tuesday at four — B must accept or ask for another day.
