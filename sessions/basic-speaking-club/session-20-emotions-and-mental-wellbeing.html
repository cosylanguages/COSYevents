Session 20: Emotions & Mental Wellbeing

28 May 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 20: Emotions & Mental Wellbeing

A welcoming, structured speaking environment for adult beginners focusing on everyday preferences and practical language.

1. Warm-Up & Vocabulary Activation (15 min)

Get comfortable with today's essential high-utility adult vocabulary:

stress anxiety calm overwhelmed motivated lonely supported confident mood cope
Target Grammar Focus: Feel + adjective + when (I feel stressed when…)

2. Round 1: Guided Discussion Questions (20 min)

Use simple frames ("I think...", "I prefer...", "In my opinion...") to answer these questions with your partner:

1. What helps you when you feel stressed?

2. Is it okay to talk about mental health in your culture?

3. Do people talk too much or too little about feelings?

3. Let's Speak Together: Pair Activity (15 min)

Take turns asking your partner questions and building your speaking confidence:

  • Student A: Asks Question 1 & 2. Uses follow-up: "Why do you think so?"
  • Student B: Responds using sentence frame: "I prefer [...] because [...]"
  • Switch roles for Question 3.

4. Round 2: Friendly Debate / Stance (10 min)

Express your opinion on these adult discussion statements:

  • 🗣️ "Talking about stress reduces its effect." — Agree or Disagree?
  • 🗣️ "Modern life is significantly more stressful than in the past." — Agree or Disagree?

5. Facilitator's Correction Note

Remember: Fluency over perfection! Focus on communicating clear ideas and asking basic follow-up questions.
