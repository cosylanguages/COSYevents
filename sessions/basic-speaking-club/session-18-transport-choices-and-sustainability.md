# Session 18: Transport Choices & Sustainability

Transport is the language of getting to work on time. Card 4 is a ticket office, and the clock is the complication.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| bus | a city bus | bus |
| train | a train at a platform | trayn |
| bike | a bicycle | bighk |
| taxi | a taxi | TAK-see |
| ticket | a paper ticket | TIK-it |
| stop | a bus stop sign | stop |
| walk | feet on a path | wawk |
| cheap | a small price tag | cheep |
| fast | a train moving | fast |
| late | a clock, red | layt |

Warm-up: Mime how you came here today. The group guesses: bus, bike, walk.

Say it: I come by bus. / The bus is late.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: How do you go to work? Is it good?

Model: For me, the bike is good because it is cheap and fast.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- the bus or the bike
- a taxi or a walk
- a paper ticket or a ticket on your phone

Example: I prefer the train because the bus is always late.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: buy a ticket (15 min)

Student A works at the ticket office. Student B wants a ticket to the city centre.

- One ticket to the city centre, please.
- Single or return?
- Single, please. How much is it?
- Three fifty.
- Which platform?
- Platform three. It is ten minutes late.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Tomorrow, I will go by… / Next weekend, I want to go to…

Prompts: How will you go to work tomorrow? Where do you want to go next weekend?

Model: Tomorrow I will go by train, and next weekend I want to go to the lake.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One transport word + one place you will go next week.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, the bike is good because it is cheap and fast. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer the train because the bus is always late. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Tomorrow I will go by train, and next weekend I want to go to the lake. |

Weaker group: use three destinations with the prices written on the board. Stronger group: the train is cancelled, so the clerk offers a bus and B must ask about the time again.
