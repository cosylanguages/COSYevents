# Session 16: Sports, Health & Competition

Sport gives beginners verbs they can mime: run, swim, play, win. Card 4 is a conversation at a gym desk.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| run | a running person | run |
| swim | a swimmer | swim |
| football | a football | FOOT-bawl |
| gym | machines in a room | jim |
| team | players together | teem |
| win | a hand up | win |
| lose | a sad face | looz |
| tired | a yawning face | TIGH-erd |
| strong | an arm with a muscle | strong |
| match | a football match | mach |

Warm-up: Mime one sport. Your partner says the word.

Say it: I play football. / I go swimming.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Do you like sport? Why?

Model: For me, sport is good because I sleep better after it.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- play a sport or watch a sport
- exercise alone or in a team
- exercise in the morning or in the evening

Example: I prefer the evening because I am tired in the morning.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: join a gym (15 min)

Student A works at a gym. Student B wants to join.

- I want to join the gym.
- How much is it?
- Thirty euros a month.
- What time are you open?
- From six to ten.
- OK, thank you.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next week, I will… / This year, I want to…

Prompts: What will you do for your body next week? What do you want to try this year?

Model: Next week I will swim two times.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One sport word + one thing you will do next week.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, sport is good because I sleep better after it. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer the evening because I am tired in the morning. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next week I will swim two times. |

Weaker group: write three times (six, eight, ten) on the board for B to choose from. Stronger group: the gym has a cheaper plan without a trainer — B must compare the two.
