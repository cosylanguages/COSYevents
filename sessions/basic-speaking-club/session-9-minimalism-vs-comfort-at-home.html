Session 9: Minimalism vs Comfort at Home

12 March 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 9: Minimalism vs Comfort at Home

"Minimalism" is a big word for a simple action: keep it or give it away. That action is Card 4.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

box shelf old new keep bin tidy full empty buy

Warm-up: Look in your bag. What do you keep and never use? Say one thing.

Say it: I keep this. / I don't need this.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Is your home full of things? Is that good?

Model: For me, my home is full, and I think it is too much.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • many things or few things
  • keep old things or give them away
  • buy new things or use the old ones

Example: I prefer few things because I can clean fast.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: give things away

15 min

Student A is moving to a smaller flat. Student B takes some things.

  • Do you want this?
  • Yes, thank you. What is it?
  • It is a lamp.
  • I don't need this one.
  • OK, I can give it to my sister.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: This weekend, I will… / Next month, I want to…

Prompts: What will you clean this weekend? What do you want to give away?

Model: This weekend I will clean my shelf and give away three books.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: One word + one thing you will give away.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

use the rung you are ready for
FunctionA0A0+A1
OpinionGood. Bad.I think it is good.For me, my home is full, and I think it is too much.
PreferenceThis. That.I prefer this one.I prefer few things because I can clean fast.
FutureTomorrow — home.Tomorrow I will go home.This weekend I will clean my shelf and give away three books.

Weaker group: use only three objects on the table and let B point. Stronger group: B wants the lamp but has no space, so they have to explain why not.
