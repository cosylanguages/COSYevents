Session 10: Food Culture & Personal Taste

19 March 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 10: Food Culture & Personal Taste

Food is the safest topic in any language: everyone has an opinion and a photo. This session uses both.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

rice bread soup salad meat fish sweet salt cook taste

Warm-up: Open your phone. Find one food photo. Show it and say the word.

Say it: In my country we eat rice. / It is very good.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: What food is good for you? Why?

Model: For me, soup is good because it is hot and cheap.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • food from home or restaurant food
  • sweet food or salty food
  • cook at home or buy food

Example: I prefer home food because I know the salt.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: talk about food from your country

15 min

Two people describe one dish from home. Ask, answer, then swap.

  • In my country we eat…
  • It is very good.
  • Is it spicy?
  • No, it is sweet.
  • Do you like it?
  • Yes, I love it.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: This weekend, I want to cook… / Tonight, I will eat…

Prompts: What do you want to cook this weekend? What will you eat tonight?

Model: This weekend I want to cook rice and fish.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: One food word + one dish you will eat this week.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

use the rung you are ready for
FunctionA0A0+A1
OpinionGood. Bad.I think it is good.For me, soup is good because it is hot and cheap.

Weaker group: give each student a food picture card and let them read the sentence from the card. Stronger group: add a comparison — "Which is better, your food or my food?"
