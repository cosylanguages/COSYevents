Session 21: Health Choices & Prevention

4 June 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 21: Health Choices & Prevention

Health talk at A0 is a shopping list of small habits. Ten words, three choices, and a pharmacy conversation everyone will need eventually.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

sleep water fruit vegetables walk doctor medicine tired rest every day

Warm-up: Point to your body: head, stomach, throat. Say the words with your partner.

Say it: I sleep eight hours. / I drink water every day.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: What is good for your health? Why?

Model: For me, walking is good because it is free and easy.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • walk every day or go to the gym
  • cook at home or order food
  • sleep six hours or sleep eight hours

Example: I prefer to cook because restaurant food has too much salt.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: at the pharmacy

15 min

Student A is the pharmacist. Student B has a headache.

  • Good morning. Can I help you?
  • I have a headache.
  • Take this, two times a day.
  • Before food or after food?
  • After food. Drink water.
  • How much is it?

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: Next week, I will… / This month, I want to…

Prompts: What will you do for your health next week? What do you want to change this month?

Model: Next week I will walk thirty minutes every day.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: One word + one small health goal.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder
