# Session 29: Tech Dependence & Digital Habits

Everyone in the room has a phone, which makes this the easiest session to make concrete. Card 4 is asking for help with it.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| phone | a phone in a hand | fone |
| screen | a bright screen | skreen |
| app | a phone with icons | ap |
| message | a speech bubble | MES-ij |
| photo | a camera | FO-to |
| charge | a phone with a cable | chari |
| off | a dark screen | of |
| on | a bright screen | on |
| night | a dark sky | night |
| quiet | a finger on the lips | KWAI-et |

Warm-up: Look at your phone. How many hours yesterday? Say the number.

Say it: I use my phone a lot. / My phone is off.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: How many hours do you use your phone? Is it too much?

Model: For me, four hours is too much because I sleep late.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- phone at dinner or no phone at dinner
- phone in the morning or a walk in the morning
- send a message or make a call

Example: I prefer a call because messages are slow.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: ask for help with a phone (15 min)

Student B has a problem with a phone. Student A helps.

- Can you help me?
- Yes, what is the problem?
- My phone is off.
- Where is your charger?
- Here. Thank you.
- Now it is on.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Tonight, I will… / Next week, I want to…

Prompts: What time will you turn off your phone tonight? What do you want to do without your phone?

Model: Tonight I will turn off my phone at ten.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you will do without your phone.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, four hours is too much because I sleep late. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer a call because messages are slow. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Tonight I will turn off my phone at ten. |

Weaker group: act the problem with the phone in hand, so the words come from the action. Stronger group: the phone needs a new battery, so A explains two options and B chooses.
