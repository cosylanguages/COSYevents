# Session 14: Weather & Lifestyle Choices

Weather is the one topic every stranger shares. Ten words, three choices, and a short conversation about plans that depend on the sky.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| sun | a yellow circle | sun |
| rain | lines falling from a cloud | rayn |
| hot | a person with a fan | hot |
| cold | a person in a coat | kohld |
| wind | a tree bending | wind |
| snow | white ground | snoh |
| coat | a winter coat | koht |
| umbrella | an open umbrella | um-BREL-a |
| summer | a beach | SUM-er |
| winter | a snowy street | WIN-ter |

Warm-up: Look out of the window. Say one word about the weather.

Say it: It is cold today. / I have an umbrella.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Do you like the weather today? Why?

Model: For me, today is good because it is not too hot.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- hot weather or cold weather
- summer or winter
- walk in the rain or stay at home

Example: I prefer winter because I can wear my coat.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: make a plan about the weather (15 min)

Two friends want to go out today. Look out of the window and decide.

- Is it cold today?
- Yes, very cold.
- Do you want to walk?
- No, let's take the bus.
- OK. See you at five.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: This weekend, if it is sunny, I will… / Next summer, I want to…

Prompts: What will you do this weekend if it is sunny? Where do you want to go next summer?

Model: This weekend, if it is sunny, I will walk in the park.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One weather word + one plan for the weekend.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, today is good because it is not too hot. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer winter because I can wear my coat. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | This weekend, if it is sunny, I will walk in the park. |

Weaker group: draw two weather symbols (sun, rain) and let students point. Stronger group: the forecast says rain all weekend, so the pair has to change the plan twice.
