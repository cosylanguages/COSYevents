# Session 5: Appearance & First Judgments

Describing people needs only two patterns: is and has. Ten words, three choices, and a short visit to an optician.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| hair | a head with hair | hair |
| eyes | two eyes | ighz |
| tall | a tall person | tawl |
| short | a short person | short |
| young | a child | yung |
| old | an older person | ohld |
| smile | a happy face | smighl |
| glasses | a person with glasses | GLAS-iz |
| clothes | a shirt and trousers | klohz |
| beard | a face with a beard | beerd |

Warm-up: Look at your partner. Say one true sentence: "You have brown eyes."

Say it: She has long hair. / He is tall.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Do you like your photo? Why?

Model: For me, I don't like my photo because my eyes are closed.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- short hair or long hair
- glasses or no glasses
- formal clothes or comfortable clothes

Example: I prefer comfortable clothes because I walk a lot.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: at the optician (15 min)

Student A works in an optician's shop. Student B wants new glasses.

- Hello. I want new glasses.
- Do you like these?
- Can I try them?
- Yes, of course.
- How much are they?
- They are forty euros.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next month, I want to… / Tomorrow, I will…

Prompts: What do you want to buy next month? What will you wear tomorrow?

Model: Next month I want to buy new glasses.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you will wear tomorrow.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, I don't like my photo because my eyes are closed. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer comfortable clothes because I walk a lot. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next month I want to buy new glasses. |

Weaker group: let A point at the glasses and say only "These? These?" plus the price. Stronger group: the glasses are too small, so B asks for a different pair and a different price.
