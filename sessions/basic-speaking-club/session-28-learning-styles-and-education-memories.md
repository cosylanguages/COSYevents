# Session 28: Learning Styles & Education Memories

The club itself is the topic: how do you learn, and what helps? Card 4 is a short conversation about an English course.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| school | a school building | skool |
| teacher | a person at a board | TEE-cher |
| book | an open book | buk |
| learn | a notebook | lern |
| English | a flag and a letter A | ING-glish |
| class | people at desks | klas |
| homework | a notebook and a pen | HOHM-werk |
| easy | a green tick | EE-zee |
| difficult | a red question mark | DIF-i-kult |
| remember | a hand on the head | ri-MEM-ber |

Warm-up: Say one word you learned in English this month.

Say it: I learn English at the club. / It is difficult but good.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: How do you learn best?

Model: For me, I learn best with music because I remember the words.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- read a book or listen to audio
- learn alone or learn in a class
- study in the morning or study at night

Example: I prefer a class because I can speak with people.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: ask about an English course (15 min)

Student A works at a language school. Student B wants a course.

- I want to learn English.
- We have a class on Monday.
- What time is it?
- From six to seven.
- How many people are in the class?
- Six people.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next year, I want to… / This month, I will…

Prompts: What do you want to do in English next year? What will you practise this month?

Model: Next year I want to speak English at work.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you will practise this week.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, I learn best with music because I remember the words. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer a class because I can speak with people. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next year I want to speak English at work. |

Weaker group: put the three course times on the board and let B choose. Stronger group: the Monday class is full, so A offers a cheaper online class and B must compare.
