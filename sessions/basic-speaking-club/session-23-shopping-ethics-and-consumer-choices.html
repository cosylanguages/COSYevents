Session 23: Shopping Ethics & Consumer Choices

18 June 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 23: Shopping Ethics & Consumer Choices

"Ethics" becomes speakable at A0 when it turns into three questions: where is it from, who made it, and can I return it.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

shop buy local cheap expensive plastic bag worker receipt return choose

Warm-up: Look at one thing on your desk or in your bag. Where is it from?

Say it: It is from Spain. / I buy local food.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Do you buy local food? Why?

Model: For me, I buy local food because it is fresh.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • a local shop or a big supermarket
  • cheap things or good things
  • a new phone or the old phone

Example: I prefer the local shop because the people know me.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: ask where food is from

15 min

Student A works in a small shop. Student B asks about the food and then returns something.

  • Where is this from?
  • It is from Spain.
  • Is it local?
  • No, sorry. The cheese is local.
  • I want to return this bag.
  • Do you have the receipt?

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: Next month, I want to buy… / This weekend, I will…

Prompts: What do you want to buy local next month? What will you do with old things at home?

Model: Next month I want to buy local vegetables at the market.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: One word + one thing you will buy locally.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

use the rung you are ready for
FunctionA0A0+A1
OpinionGood. Bad.I think it is good.For me, I buy local food because it is fresh.
PreferenceThis. That.I prefer this one.I prefer the local shop because the people know me.
FutureTomorrow — home.Tomorrow I will go home.Next month I want to buy local vegetables at the market.

Weaker group: cut the return part and keep only the "where is this from" exchange. Stronger group: B has no receipt, so A has to explain the shop rules politely.
