# Session 22: Time Perception & Punctuality

Being late is a cultural topic you can handle with three words: sorry, bus, wait. Card 4 rehearses the apology until it feels normal.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| minute | a phone clock with minutes | MIN-it |
| hour | a clock face | our |
| early | a clock at seven | ER-lee |
| late | a clock at eleven | layt |
| wait | a person at a bus stop | wayt |
| clock | a wall clock | klok |
| meeting | people at a table | MEE-ting |
| hurry | a running person | HUR-ee |
| sorry | a hand on the heart | SOR-ee |
| on time | a clock at nine | on tighm |

Warm-up: Look at your phone. Say the time. Now say a time you are usually busy.

Say it: I am sorry I am late. / The bus was late.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Are you usually early or late? Why?

Model: For me, I am usually early because I don't like to hurry.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- arrive early or arrive on time
- wait ten minutes or leave
- use a clock or use your phone

Example: I prefer to arrive early because I can relax.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: you are late for a meeting (15 min)

Student B arrives late. Student A is waiting.

- Sorry I am late.
- No problem. What happened?
- The bus was late.
- OK. Let's start now.
- Thank you for waiting.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Tomorrow, I will leave… / Next week, I want to…

Prompts: What time will you leave tomorrow? What do you want to change about your mornings?

Model: Tomorrow I will leave at eight and arrive on time.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One time word + one time you will arrive tomorrow.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, I am usually early because I don't like to hurry. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer to arrive early because I can relax. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Tomorrow I will leave at eight and arrive on time. |

Weaker group: let A accept the apology with one word ("OK") and keep the scene at four lines. Stronger group: the meeting is important, so A is not happy and B has to explain and apologise twice.
