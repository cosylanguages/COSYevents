Session 30: Life Reflections & Future Hopes

6 August 2027

Home / Events / Basic Speaking Club / Session 30: Life Reflections & Future Hopes

The last session of the course, so the language looks backwards and forwards: what was good this year, and what comes next.

Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words

10 min

Point, repeat, then test your partner. Use pictures or real objects on the table.

year change home job family learn happy plan next hope

Warm-up: Say one good thing about this year. One word is enough.

Say it: This year was good. / Next year I want to change my job.

Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks

10 min

Think alone for 30 seconds, tell your partner, then three people tell the room.

Opinion chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: What was good this year?

Model: For me, this year was good because I met new people.

Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks

10 min

Hands up for each side first, then each person says their line.

Preference chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.
  • the same life or a new life
  • stay in this city or move
  • learn something now or learn later

Example: I prefer to learn now because I have time.

Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: tell a friend your plan

15 min

Two friends talk about next year. A asks questions, B answers.

  • What do you want to do next year?
  • I want a new job.
  • Why?
  • The money is not good.
  • That is a good plan.
  • Thank you. And you?

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

Card 5 — Looking ahead

8 min
Future chunks: Next year, I will… / In five years, I want to…

Prompts: What will you do next year? Where do you want to be in five years?

Model: Next year I will start an English course, and in five years I want to work in another country.

Card 6 — Outro & takeaway

5 min

Wrap-up: One word from the course + one thing you will do next year.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

use the rung you are ready for
FunctionA0A0+A1
OpinionGood. Bad.I think it is good.For me, this year was good because I met new people.
PreferenceThis. That.I prefer this one.I prefer to learn now because I have time.
FutureTomorrow — home.Tomorrow I will go home.Next year I will start an English course, and in five years I want to work in another country.

Weaker group: let B answer with one word plus "because" and one noun. Stronger group: A has to give one piece of advice — "You can start with a small course."
