# Session 24: Healthcare Access & Expectations

The first real conversation many newcomers need is booking a doctor's appointment. Card 4 is exactly that phone call.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| doctor | a person in a white coat | DOK-ter |
| appointment | a calendar with a time | a-POINT-ment |
| hospital | a big building with a cross | HOS-pi-tal |
| pharmacy | a green cross | FAR-ma-see |
| wait | people in a queue | wayt |
| medicine | a pill box | MED-i-sin |
| open | an open door | OH-pen |
| closed | a closed door | klohzd |
| hurt | a red spot on an arm | hert |
| help | two hands together | help |

Warm-up: Point to the body words: head, stomach, throat, back. Say them with your partner.

Say it: I want an appointment. / I have a pain here.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Is it easy to see a doctor in your city? Why?

Model: For me, it is difficult because the doctor is very busy.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- a doctor near home or a big hospital
- a morning appointment or an evening appointment
- call first or go in person

Example: I prefer a morning appointment because I work in the evening.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: book an appointment (15 min)

Student A works at a clinic. Student B calls to book a visit.

- Hello. I want an appointment.
- What is your name?
- Ana Silva.
- Are you free on Tuesday?
- Yes, in the morning.
- Ten o'clock is OK?

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next week, I will… / Tomorrow, I want to…

Prompts: When will you go to the doctor next? What do you want to ask about?

Model: Next week I will go to the pharmacy and then to the doctor.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one appointment you need this month.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, it is difficult because the doctor is very busy. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer a morning appointment because I work in the evening. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next week I will go to the pharmacy and then to the doctor. |

Weaker group: write the days and times on the board so B only has to choose. Stronger group: Tuesday is full, so A offers Thursday afternoon and B must check their calendar.
