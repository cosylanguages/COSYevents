# Session 20: Emotions & Mental Wellbeing

Feelings are A0 grammar with a big payoff: "I am tired" is a full conversation opener. Keep the session light and practical.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| happy | a smiling face | HAP-ee |
| sad | a sad face | sad |
| tired | a yawning face | TIGH-erd |
| angry | a red face | ANG-gree |
| worried | a hand on the head | WUR-eed |
| calm | a person with eyes closed | kahm |
| good | a thumb up | gud |
| bad | a thumb down | bad |
| talk | two people with a coffee | tawk |
| friend | two people together | frend |

Warm-up: Show one face: happy, tired or worried. Your partner says the word.

Say it: I am tired today. / I feel good.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: What do you do when you are stressed?

Model: For me, I walk when I am stressed because it helps.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- talk to a friend or stay alone
- walk outside or sleep
- music or silence

Example: I prefer to talk because my friend is a good listener.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: ask a friend how they are (15 min)

Two friends meet after work. B has a difficult week.

- How are you?
- I am tired and worried.
- What happened?
- My job is very busy.
- Do you want to talk?
- Yes, let's have a coffee.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: This weekend, I will… / Next week, I want to…

Prompts: How will you relax this weekend? What do you want to do less next week?

Model: This weekend I will walk and sleep, and next week I want to work less.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One feeling word + one thing you will do to relax.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, I walk when I am stressed because it helps. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer to talk because my friend is a good listener. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | This weekend I will walk and sleep, and next week I want to work less. |

Weaker group: put six faces on the table and let students point instead of naming the feeling. Stronger group: A has the same problem and they have to suggest two solutions to each other.
