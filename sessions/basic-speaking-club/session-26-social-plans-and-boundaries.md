# Session 26: Social Plans & Boundaries

Saying no politely is the hardest A0 skill and the most useful. This session practises it until it is easy.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| plan | a list with times | plan |
| invite | a hand with a phone | in-VIGHT |
| friend | two people together | frend |
| free | an empty page | free |
| busy | a full notebook | BIZ-ee |
| dinner | a plate at night | DIN-er |
| cinema | a cinema screen | SIN-e-ma |
| time | a clock face | tighm |
| yes | a thumb up | yes |
| no | a thumb down | noh |

Warm-up: Say one thing you will do this weekend. One sentence.

Say it: Do you want to come? / Sorry, I am busy.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Is it easy to say no to friends? Why?

Model: For me, it is difficult because I don't want to be rude.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- say yes always or say no sometimes
- plan early or plan at the last minute
- a small group or a big group

Example: I prefer a small group because I can talk to everybody.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: invite and say no politely (15 min)

Student A invites Student B out. B is busy and suggests another day.

- Do you want to come on Saturday?
- Sorry, I am busy.
- Maybe Sunday?
- Yes, Sunday is good. What time?
- Seven o'clock, at the cinema.
- Perfect. See you there.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Next weekend, I want to… / On Sunday, I will…

Prompts: What do you want to do next weekend? Who will you invite?

Model: Next weekend I want to have dinner with two friends.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one plan you will make this week.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, it is difficult because I don't want to be rude. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer a small group because I can talk to everybody. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Next weekend I want to have dinner with two friends. |

Weaker group: give B the phrase "Sorry, I am busy" on a card and let them read it. Stronger group: B is busy on Saturday and Sunday, so they must suggest a third option.
