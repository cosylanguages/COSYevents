# Session 6: Morning Routines & Productivity

The morning is the easiest place to start speaking: everybody has one, and every action has a picture. Card 4 is breakfast at a café counter.

## Card 1 — Intro + 10 core words (10 min)

| Word | Picture cue | Say it |
|---|---|---|
| alarm | a phone alarm | a-LARM |
| shower | water from above | SHOW-er |
| breakfast | bread and coffee | BREK-fast |
| coffee | a cup | KOF-ee |
| bus | a city bus | bus |
| hurry | a running person | HUR-ee |
| brush | a toothbrush | brush |
| work | a desk | werk |
| early | a clock at six | ER-lee |
| tired | a yawning face | TIGH-erd |

Warm-up: Mime one morning action. Your partner says the word.

Say it: I wake up at seven. / I drink coffee.

## Card 2 — Discussion + opinion chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I think… / For me… / It is good. / It is bad.

Question: Is your morning good or bad? Why?

Model: For me, my morning is good because I have time for coffee.

## Card 3 — Which will you choose? + preference chunks (10 min)

Chunks: I prefer… / I like [A] more than [B]. / [A] is better for me.

- early morning or late night
- breakfast at home or breakfast at a café
- walk to work or take the bus

Example: I prefer to walk because the bus is late.

## Card 4 — Real-life mini-action: buy breakfast (15 min)

Student A works at a café. Student B wants breakfast before work.

- One coffee, please.
- And one sandwich, please.
- Here you are.
- How much is it?
- Five euros, please.
- Thank you. Have a good day.

How to run it: demo with one student (2 min) → pairs (5 min) → swap roles (5 min) → one pair performs for the room (3 min).

## Card 5 — Looking ahead (8 min)

Chunks: Tomorrow, I will… / Next week, I want to…

Prompts: What time will you wake up tomorrow? What do you want to change in your morning?

Model: Tomorrow I will wake up at six and walk to work.

## Card 6 — Outro & takeaway (5 min)

One word + one thing you will do tomorrow morning.

Facilitator note: write the three best sentences on the board, then three corrections as "we said / we can say". Fluency first — do not interrupt during the cards.

## Helper sheet — the chunk ladder

| Function | A0 | A0+ | A1 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opinion | Good. Bad. | I think it is good. | For me, my morning is good because I have time for coffee. |
| Preference | This. That. | I prefer this one. | I prefer to walk because the bus is late. |
| Future | Tomorrow — home. | Tomorrow I will go home. | Tomorrow I will wake up at six and walk to work. |

Weaker group: put the three prices on the board and let B point to the total. Stronger group: the sandwich is finished, so B chooses something else and asks the price again.
