mistakes:
  - wrong: We must <u>to keep</u> the peace.
    right: We must <strong>keep</strong> the peace.
    note: '(Modal verb check: ''must'' is followed by the bare infinitive ''keep'' without ''to''.)'
  - wrong: I <u>make</u> an act of kindness.
    right: I <strong>do / perform</strong> an act of kindness.
    note: '(Collocation: ''acts of kindness'' are typically ''done'' or ''performed'', not ''made''.)'
