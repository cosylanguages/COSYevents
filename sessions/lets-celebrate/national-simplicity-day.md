round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : General Discussion'
  badge: Questions
  instruction: Simple discussion about simple living and habits.
  items:
    - main: What <strong>does</strong> it mean to live a <strong>simple</strong> life, and how <strong>do</strong> you start?
      personal: ★ What is one simple thing that makes you very happy?
    - main: Why <strong>is</strong> it important to spend time in a <strong>quiet</strong> place, and what <strong>does</strong> it change?
      personal: ★ Where do you go when you want a quiet moment?
    - main: How <strong>do</strong> we <strong>reduce</strong> our monthly expenses easily?
      personal: ★ What monthly expense did you reduce recently?
    - main: Why <strong>do</strong> people feel more relaxed when they <strong>are</strong> in <strong>nature</strong>?
      personal: ★ How often do you walk in a forest or park?
    - main: What <strong>is</strong> your morning <strong>routine</strong> and how <strong>does</strong> it help you?
      personal: ★ Is your daily routine busy or relaxed?
    - main: How <strong>does</strong> your family <strong>celebrate</strong> traditional holidays?
      personal: ★ How did you celebrate your last birthday?
    - main: Why <strong>does</strong> it feel satisfying when you <strong>tidy</strong> your wardrobe?
      personal: ★ Do you need to tidy your bedroom desk today?
    - main: What <strong>makes</strong> a neighborhood feel <strong>peaceful</strong> and calm?
      personal: ★ Is your current neighborhood peaceful or noisy?
    - main: <strong>Does</strong> a physical <strong>hobby</strong> feel better than a digital one?
      personal: ★ What hobbies do you practice in your free time?
    - main: Why <strong>do</strong> we feel <strong>thankful</strong> for simple things like fresh coffee?
      personal: ★ What simple thing are you most thankful for today?

  title: 'Round 2 : Agree or Disagree: The Future'

  instruction: Simple future predictions about modern lifestyle trends.
  items:
    - main: In the future, will we <strong>reduce</strong> our screen time because we are always online?
      personal: ★ Do you reduce your phone use on weekends?
    - main: Will smart homes do all our physical chores so we <strong>lose</strong> our simple daily <strong>routine</strong>?
      personal: ★ Do you find doing simple tasks like washing dishes to be a good daily <strong>routine</strong>?
    - main: Will governments ask us to pay a tax if we do not live a <strong>simple</strong> life?
      personal: ★ Do you think a <strong>simple</strong> life with only 100 personal possessions is possible?
    - main: As big cities <strong>grow</strong>, will quiet <strong>nature</strong> become too expensive for normal people?
      personal: ★ Do you like quiet weekends in nature?
    - main: Will traditional <strong>hobbies</strong> like gardening <strong>disappear</strong> because of computer games?
      personal: ★ Do you prefer real hobbies to video games?
    - main: Will we <strong>celebrate</strong> free time more than a busy job?
      personal: ★ Do you want more free time or more money?
    - main: Will we live in tiny, clean micro-homes that are easy to <strong>tidy</strong>?
      personal: ★ Do you want to live in a small, tidy home?
    - main: In the future, will we <strong>feel</strong> <strong>thankful</strong> for simple things only when they are expensive?
      personal: ★ Are you happy when you buy simple things?
    - main: Will future schools teach children how to live a <strong>peaceful</strong> and happy life?
      personal: ★ Was your childhood peaceful?
    - main: Will people leave big cities to find a <strong>quiet</strong> and simple life in country villages?
      personal: ★ Do you want to live in a quiet country house?
