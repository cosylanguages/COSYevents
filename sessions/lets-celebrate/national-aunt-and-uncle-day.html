This session celebrates National Aunt and Uncle Day, a heartwarming day to appreciate the special relatives in our lives. We will discuss our favorite childhood memories, family gatherings, and the important role aunts and uncles play in our lives. Join us to learn simple English words to describe your relatives and share cozy family stories.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Uncle
the brother of one's father or mother, or the husband of one's aunt.
My uncle John took me to my very first baseball game when I was ten.
Aunt
the sister of one's father or mother, or the wife of one's uncle.
My aunt Mary always bakes delicious chocolate chip cookies for my birthday.
Nephew
a son of one's brother or sister.
He has one nephew who is studying computer science at university.
Niece
a daughter of one's brother or sister.
My niece is five years old and she loves drawing pictures of cats.
Relative
a person connected by blood or marriage.
Almost all my relatives gather together at my grandparents' house for Christmas.
Generation
all of the people born and living at about the same time.
My grandparents' generation lived in a very different, quieter world.
Gratitude ≠ ungratefulness
the quality of being thankful ≠ a lack of appreciation or thankfulness.
Writing a thank-you note is a simple way to show gratitude instead of ungratefulness.
Gathering ≠ isolation
an assembly or meeting of people ≠ the state of being completely alone.
Our cozy family gathering saved her from a painful sense of winter isolation.
Traditional
existing in or as part of a long-established tradition or custom.
It is traditional to exchange gifts and share a big dinner on New Year's Eve.
Childhood
the state or period of being a child.
She spent her childhood playing outdoors in a beautiful, sunny countryside.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up : Family Circles▲
  • Do you have a large family or a small family?
  • How often do you speak to your aunts or uncles?
🎯 Linguistic Focus (Interactive Grammar)▲

Use comparatives (older, younger, friendlier) to compare your relatives.

Task 1A: Rule Formulation

Complete the rule: For short adjectives ending in 'y', we change 'y' to 'i' and add _____ to make the comparative. For long adjectives, we use 'more' + adjective, e.g. _____.

Task 1B: Structured Practice
  1. My uncle is five years _____ than my father.
  2. My niece is much _____ than my nephew.
  3. My aunt Mary is the _____ person in our family.
  4. Our family gatherings are much _____ than formal office parties.
  5. My grandparents' generation was _____ than ours.
Task 2: Task-Specific Speaking Prompt

Compare your uncle or aunt to your parents. Who is friendlier? Who is older?

🟢 Round 2 : Agree or Disagree: The Future▲
Debate
In the future, digital communication will replace physical family gatherings entirely, leaving us isolated.
★ Do you prefer video calls or physical visits when speaking to your relatives?
The role of an uncle or aunt is less important today than it was in traditional societies.
★ Do you think modern children are too busy with screens to bond with their uncles?
Children who spend their childhood near many relatives grow up to be more confident and resilient.
★ Did you grow up in a big house with many relatives, or in a quiet apartment?
Showing gratitude is a skill that is slowly disappearing in the younger generation.
★ Do you think younger people are ungrateful, or are they just different?
A niece or nephew should always help their elderly relatives with technology and physical work.
★ Have you ever had to help an older relative use a smartphone or computer?
Maintaining traditional family hierarchies is essential to protect young children from moral confusion.
★ Do you think classical family traditions should be strictly followed or modernized?
Living in deep social isolation is better for creative work than living surrounded by a noisy family.
★ Where do you study best: in a quiet room alone, or in a busy café?
A distant relative has absolutely no right to give advice regarding a child's education or career choices.
★ Has a distant relative ever criticized your life decisions? How did you respond?
We should actively celebrate days like Aunt and Uncle Day to strengthen our community bonds.
★ Do you think celebrating minor holidays is fun or is it just a waste of time?
The next generation will have a completely different concept of family, built on shared interests rather than blood.
★ Do you consider your close friends as part of your family circle?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
My uncle John wife is Mary. → My uncle john's wife is Mary. (Underlined error: possessive 's is required to show ownership or relationship)
I am very gratitude for my aunt's help. → I am very grateful for my aunt's help. (Underlined word form error: 'gratitude' is a noun; the adjective form to describe how you feel is 'grateful')
We had a very good family gather. → We had a very good family gathering. (Underlined word form error: use the noun 'gathering' instead of the verb form 'gather')
