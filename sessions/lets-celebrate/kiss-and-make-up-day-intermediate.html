🕊️ Let's Celebrate Perspective

Celebrated annually on August 25th, Kiss and Make Up Day encourages people to set aside grudges, initiate honest conversations, and repair broken connections with friends and family.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Reconciliation ≠ Estrangement
the restoration of friendly relations.
Reconciliation requires willingness from both sides.
Forgiveness ≠ Resentment
the action or process of forgiving or being forgiven.
Forgiveness brings emotional relief and peace of mind.
Grudge ≠ Goodwill
a persistent feeling of ill will or resentment.
Holding a grudge only hurts the person carrying it.
Apology ≠ Defiance
a regretful acknowledgment of an offense or failure.
A sincere apology can heal years of misunderstanding.
Empathy ≠ Apathy
the ability to understand and share the feelings of another.
Empathy helps us see conflict from the other person's point of view.
Compromise ≠ Intransigence
an agreement reached by mutual concession.
Healthy relationships rely on honest compromise.
Rapport ≠ Discord
a close and harmonious relationship.
Building rapport takes time and open communication.
Mediator ≠ Instigator
a person who attempts to make people involved in a conflict come to an agreement.
A neutral mediator can help resolve family disputes.
Vulnerability ≠ Defensiveness
the quality of being open to emotional exposure.
Showing vulnerability is a sign of courage, not weakness.
Harmony ≠ Conflict
agreement or concord.
Restoring harmony in a team improves everyone's wellbeing.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up (10 mins)▲

Welcome! Share your initial thoughts on this topic. What personal experiences or observations come to mind right away?

⚡ Grammar Practice : Expressing Regret & Past Modals (should have, could have) (B1)▲
Use past modals to discuss past conflicts and missed reconciliations: "I should have called earlier." / "We could have resolved the issue much sooner."
🔵 Round 1: Core Exploration (Units 1–5)▲
Why is reconciliation often difficult after a long period of holding a grudge?
★ Looking back at a past argument, what should you have done differently to resolve it sooner?
How does offering a sincere apology help restore emotional harmony?
★ What makes an apology feel genuine to you?
What role does empathy play in reaching a fair compromise during an argument?
★ How do you calm down when an argument gets heated?
Why does showing emotional vulnerability build stronger rapport between people?
★ Who in your life do you feel most comfortable being open with?
When can a neutral mediator help resolve persistent disagreements?
★ Have you ever helped two friends make peace with each other?
🟢 Round 2: Deep Speculation & Future Horizon (Units 6–10)▲
Should society promote designated holidays like Kiss and Make Up Day to encourage forgiveness?
★ Do you think holding a grudge could have been avoided if both parties spoke openly earlier?
How can workplace culture encourage healthier conflict resolution and reconciliation?
★ How are disagreements handled in your workplace or university?
Can true forgiveness exist without a full verbal apology?
★ Have you ever forgiven someone who never apologized?
How does learning to let go of a grudge benefit physical and mental health?
★ What activity helps you let go of stress and anger?
What is the most effective way to rebuild trust and long-term harmony after a betrayal?
★ What advice would you give to a friend trying to repair a relationship?
