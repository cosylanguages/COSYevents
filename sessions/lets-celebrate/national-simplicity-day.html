This session is about National Simplicity Day and the beauty of simple living. We will talk about why people choose to have fewer things and live a quiet life. We will also learn simple words to describe our daily routines and hobbies.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Simple ≠ Difficult
easy to do or understand ≠ not easy to do; needing effort.
The basic idea is simple and easy to understand.
Quiet
with very little noise; calm.
I like to read a book in a quiet room.
Reduce
to make something smaller or less.
We can reduce our screen time on our phones.
Nature
the physical world with trees, plants, and wild animals.
I love walking in nature on sunny days.
Routine
your normal plan or habits for every day.
Eating breakfast is part of my morning routine.
Celebrate
to do something special for a fun day.
We celebrate this holiday with a small family dinner.
Tidy
to clean a room and put things in their correct places.
You should tidy your room every weekend.
Peaceful
calm, quiet, and with no stress.
He found a peaceful spot in the park to rest.
Hobby
an activity that you do for fun in your free time.
Gardening is a very relaxing and simple hobby.
Thankful
feeling or showing thanks; grateful.
I am thankful for my healthy life.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
Short introduction to daily schedules.
  • What is the most relaxing part of your day?
  • Do you have too many things in your bedroom or house?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Simple Present Structure▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Beginner (A1))

The simple present structure is used to talk about regular habits, routines, and permanent facts.

🧩 Interactive Word Bank:

Tap a word chip below, then tap any empty bracket [_____] to place it!

do do not like tidies is has spend does -s/-es
📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: For general habits or routines, we use the Simple Present. For third-person singular (he/she/it), we add _____ to the base verb. We use the auxiliary verb _____ or _____ for questions and negatives.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. She _____ her room every Saturday morning.
  2. They _____ busy city noise.
  3. Do you _____ time in nature often?
  4. He _____ a very simple daily routine.
  5. My neighborhood _____ exceptionally peaceful and quiet.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

Describe your daily morning routine to your partner in 3 simple sentences using the Simple Present tense.

🔵 Round 1 : General Discussion▲
Questions
Simple discussion about simple living and habits.
What does it mean to live a simple life, and how do you start?
★ What is one simple thing that makes you very happy?
Why is it important to spend time in a quiet place, and what does it change?
★ Where do you go when you want a quiet moment?
How do we reduce our monthly expenses easily?
★ What monthly expense did you reduce recently?
Why do people feel more relaxed when they are in nature?
★ How often do you walk in a forest or park?
What is your morning routine and how does it help you?
★ Is your daily routine busy or relaxed?
How does your family celebrate traditional holidays?
★ How did you celebrate your last birthday?
Why does it feel satisfying when you tidy your wardrobe?
★ Do you need to tidy your bedroom desk today?
What makes a neighborhood feel peaceful and calm?
★ Is your current neighborhood peaceful or noisy?
Does a physical hobby feel better than a digital one?
★ What hobbies do you practice in your free time?
Why do we feel thankful for simple things like fresh coffee?
★ What simple thing are you most thankful for today?

🟢 Round 2 : Agree or Disagree: The Future▲
Predictions
Simple future predictions about modern lifestyle trends.
In the future, will we reduce our screen time because we are always online?
★ Do you reduce your phone use on weekends?
Will smart homes do all our physical chores so we lose our simple daily routine?
★ Do you find doing simple tasks like washing dishes to be a good daily routine?
Will governments ask us to pay a tax if we do not live a simple life?
★ Do you think a simple life with only 100 personal possessions is possible?
As big cities grow, will quiet nature become too expensive for normal people?
★ Do you like quiet weekends in nature?
Will traditional hobbies like gardening disappear because of computer games?
★ Do you prefer real hobbies to video games?
Will we celebrate free time more than a busy job?
★ Do you want more free time or more money?
Will we live in tiny, clean micro-homes that are easy to tidy?
★ Do you want to live in a small, tidy home?
In the future, will we feel thankful for simple things only when they are expensive?
★ Are you happy when you buy simple things?
Will future schools teach children how to live a peaceful and happy life?
★ Was your childhood peaceful?
Will people leave big cities to find a quiet and simple life in country villages?
★ Do you want to live in a quiet country house?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I prefer a life more simple→I prefer a simpler life(Comparative form of 'simple' is 'simpler')
I am work in a peaceful place→I work in a peaceful place(Simple present structure)
She tidies always her room→She always tidies her room(Adverbs of frequency come before the main verb)
