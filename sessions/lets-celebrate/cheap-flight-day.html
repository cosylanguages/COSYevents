Celebrate Cheap Flight Day at Starter (A1) level. Learn basic vocabulary for airports, cheap tickets, planes, luggage, and holiday destinations.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Flight ≠ train ride
a journey made by air in a plane.
I booked a cheap flight to Rome.
Cheap ≠ expensive
costing little money or low price.
This airline ticket is very cheap.
Ticket ≠ receipt
a piece of paper giving permission to travel.
Show your flight ticket at the gate.
Plane ≠ car
a vehicle with wings that flies through the air.
The plane landed on time.
Airport ≠ station
a place where aircraft regularly take off and land.
We arrived at the airport two hours early.
Travel ≠ stay
to go from one place to another.
I love to travel during summer holidays.
Luggage ≠ empty hands
bags and suitcases used when traveling.
Keep your luggage small and light.
Seat ≠ aisle
a place to sit in a vehicle or room.
I prefer a window seat on the plane.
Visit ≠ leave
to go to see a person or place.
We plan to visit our cousins in Spain.
City ≠ village
a large town with many buildings.
London is a busy capital city.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What comes to your mind when you hear about this topic?
  • How does this subject relate to your daily habits?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Want to + Base Verb for Travel Wishes (A1)▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Starter (A1))

Use want to + base verb to talk about your travel plans (e.g., I want to fly to Italy).

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I want fly to Spain. → I want to fly to Spain. ('Want' requires 'to' before base verb: 'want to fly'.)
The flight is very much cheap. → The flight is very cheap. (Do not add 'much' before simple adjective 'cheap': use 'very cheap'.)
