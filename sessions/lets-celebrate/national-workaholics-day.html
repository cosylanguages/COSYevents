This session is about National Workaholics Day and the balance between work and life. We will talk about why people work too much and how to rest. We will also learn easy words to describe our daily job and hobbies.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Balance
a state where different things are equal.
A good balance between work and sleep is important.
Career
a job that you do for a long time.
She wants a long career as a school teacher.
Overtime
extra hours that you work at your job.
He works overtime to get more money.
Relaxation
time to rest and feel calm.
Reading a book is my favorite relaxation.
Stress
a feeling of worry or pressure.
Too much work can cause a lot of stress.
Busy
having a lot of work or things to do.
I am very busy with work today.
Holiday
a time when you do not work and can travel.
They are going to Spain for their summer holiday.
Free time
time when you do not have to work or study.
In my free time, I like to play football.
Office
a room or building where people work at desks.
My office is very near the train station.
Task
a small job that you must do.
Cleaning the kitchen is my daily task.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
Introduce your job.
  • What is your job, and do you like it?
  • What time do you start and finish work every day?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Modal Verbs of Obligation▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Beginner (A1))

Modal verbs of obligation (must, have to, should) express rules, necessity, and advice.

🧩 Interactive Word Bank:

Tap a word chip below, then tap any empty bracket [_____] to place it!

have to should take must should not stress must finish must not work has to arrive should
📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: To express a strong duty, we use _____ or _____. For soft recommendations or advice, we use _____.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. You look exhausted; you _____ a holiday.
  2. Employees _____ their daily tasks before leaving.
  3. We _____ on Sundays; it is a strict company rule.
  4. She _____ at the office by 9:00 AM every day.
  5. You _____ too much about this small issue.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

Tell your partner 3 things you "must" or "have to" do in your job, and 2 things you "should" do to relax.

🔵 Round 1 : General Discussion▲
Questions
Simple discussion about work habits.
Why must we maintain a healthy work-life balance to protect our mental health?
★ What is the most important thing for your daily balance?
Why should a person think twice before committing to a demanding career?
★ Do you want to change your career in the future?
How much overtime must employees work in your country's corporate culture?
★ Did you work overtime last week?
Why should deep breathing be your primary method of relaxation?
★ How do you relax after a very busy day?
When work causes too much stress, what immediate steps should you take?
★ What causes you the most stress at work or school?
How busy must your schedule be before you decide you have to take a break?
★ Are you busy this weekend, or do you have free time?
Why should families spend their holiday entirely disconnected from work emails?
★ Where did you go for your last holiday?
What urgent tasks have to be completed before you can enjoy your free time?
★ What free-time activities do you do with your family?
Why must a modern office design quiet relaxation spaces for its employees?
★ Is your current office comfortable for you?
Which difficult task should you delegate to others to reduce your stress?
★ What is your most difficult task today?
🟣 Let's Speak Together : Collaborative Activities▲

⏳ Activity 1: The Work-Life Scale

How do you balance your day? Together with your partner, evaluate these three daily duties. Discuss how much time we should give to each. Practice using modals of obligation (must, have to, should):

💼
Office Job & Tasks

We have to complete our tasks, but we should not work overtime every day.

😴
Sleep & Health

To avoid stress, an adult must sleep 7 to 8 hours every night.

🧘
Relaxation & Hobbies

You should spend some free time in nature to completely recharge.

🚫 Closing: The Digital Detach Promise

As we celebrate National Workaholics Day, we promise to set boundaries. Which promise would help you reduce stress the most?

📵 No work emails after 7:00 PM 🏖️ Taking a full 10-day offline holiday 🚲 No laptop or screens on Sundays 💤 Dedicating 1 hour daily to a creative hobby
🟢 Round 2 : Agree or Disagree: The Future▲
Predictions
Simple future predictions about jobs and rest.
In the future, we must have a good balance between work and rest.
★ How do you keep a good balance when your smartphone is always in your pocket?
In the future, we will not have to be too busy with jobs to have money.
★ If you never had to be busy with jobs, what would you do to feel useful?
When robots do every difficult task, humans should learn to rest more.
★ Have you ever felt anxious during a day without any task to do?
We will not need a physical office, so we must learn to work from home.
★ Do you work better when there is a clear boundary between your office and your bedroom?
Bosses must help workers reduce stress and take more breaks.
★ Would you feel comfortable if your boss could track your emotional stress in real-time?
We should stop working too much overtime because it is bad for us.
★ Have you ever worked overtime to avoid dealing with personal life problems?
We should look at how people enjoy their free time, not just their jobs.
★ What creative activity would you pursue if you had unlimited free time?
Older people must stop working sooner so they can enjoy a long holiday.
★ At what age do you hope to stop working and start your permanent holiday?
A good career should also allow you to have a family.
★ Do you think it is easy to have a good job and a happy family?
We have to get more time for relaxation when machines do our work.
★ If you could redesign the workweek for better relaxation, how many hours would be ideal?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I have a busy job→I have a demanding job(Demanding is better for jobs than busy)
I am work in office→I work in an office(Simple present tense structure)
He work very hard→He works very hard(Third-person singular 's')
