mistakes:
  - wrong: The bee <u>have</u> beautiful wings.
    right: The bee <strong>has</strong> beautiful wings.
    note: '(Subject-verb agreement: ''The bee'' is singular, use ''has''.)'
  - wrong: I saw a bee <u>on yesterday</u>.
    right: I saw a bee <strong>yesterday</strong>.
    note: '(Time adverb: do not use the preposition ''on'' before ''yesterday''.)'
