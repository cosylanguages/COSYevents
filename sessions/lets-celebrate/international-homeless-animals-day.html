Celebrate International Homeless Animals Day at Starter (A1) level. Practice simple vocabulary for pets, shelters, feeding, adoption, and kindness to street animals.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Dog ≠ cat
a common domestic animal kept as a pet.
He feeds his dog every morning.
Cat ≠ dog
a small domesticated carnivorous mammal.
My cat sleeps on the sunny sofa.
Pet ≠ wild animal
an animal kept for companionship.
Dogs and cats are popular house pets.
Home ≠ street
the place where one lives permanently.
Every homeless animal deserves a warm home.
Feed ≠ starve
to give food to an animal or person.
Volunteers feed stray cats in the park.
Adopt ≠ buy
to take an animal into your home as a pet.
We decided to adopt a puppy from the shelter.
Care ≠ neglect
the process of keeping someone or something safe and healthy.
Pets need love, water, and daily care.
Shelter ≠ street
a place that gives protection to homeless animals.
The animal shelter has thirty dogs.
Kind ≠ cruel
friendly, generous, and considerate.
Be kind to street animals in winter.
Water ≠ milk
a clear liquid essential for all living animals.
Always leave clean water for stray dogs.

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What comes to your mind when you hear about this topic?

⚡ Grammar Practice : Can for Ability & Permission (A1)▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Starter (A1))

Use can + base verb to talk about ways we can help animals (e.g., We can adopt pets from shelters).

Complete the rule: Use modal verb 'can' + _____ to talk about basic abilities and actions.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:

  3. They can _____ at the animal shelter.

✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
We can to help street dogs. → We can help street dogs. (Modal verb 'can' is followed by base verb without 'to'.)
The cat have hunger. → The cat is hungry / has food. (Describe state with 'is hungry', or use 3rd person singular 'has'.)
