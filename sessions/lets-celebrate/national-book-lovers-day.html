Books & Reading Habits

Celebrate National Book Lovers Day with basic A1 vocabulary. Learn simple phrases to discuss favorite books, reading places, and peaceful stories.

📖 Session Vocabulary

Book ≠ screen
a written work printed on paper or shown online.
I read a book every night before sleep.
Love ≠ hate
to like something very much.
Children love stories about magic and animals.
Read ≠ write
to look at words and understand their meaning.
She likes to read in the quiet garden.
Story ≠ fact
a description of imaginary or real people and events.
My grandfather told us a funny story.
Library ≠ store
a room or building containing books to borrow.
We get five books from the library each week.
Page ≠ cover
one side of a piece of paper in a book.
Turn to page ten to see the picture.
Quiet ≠ loud
making very little noise.
Please be quiet in the reading room.
Author ≠ reader
a person who writes books.
Who is your favorite author?
Cover ≠ inside
the outside part of a book.
The book has a bright blue cover.
Bookmark
a piece of paper used to mark your place in a book.
I use a colorful paper bookmark.

🎙️ Discussion Structure

🟠 Warm-up▲
  • What comes to your mind when you hear about this topic?
  • How does this subject relate to your daily habits?
⚡ Grammar Practice : Like + Verb-ing (A1)▲

⚡ Grammar Explanation (Starter (A1))

Use like + verb-ing to express your favorite activities (e.g., I like reading printed books).

🧩 Interactive Word Bank:

Tap a word chip below, then tap any empty bracket [_____] to place it!

like reading buying listening verb-ing
📝 Task 1 : Part A: Rule Formulation:

Complete the rule: After the verb 'like', we add _____ to express hobbies.

📝 Task 1 : Part B: Structured Practice:
  1. I like _____ paper books at night.
  2. They like _____ colorful bookmarks.
  3. She likes _____ to audiobooks while walking.
🗣️ Task 2 : Interactive Speaking:

Say two things you like doing with books in your free time.

🔵 Round 1 : Exploration & Analysis▲
What kind of book do you like to read in your free time?
★ What was your favorite childhood story?
Do you love buying paper books or reading digital ebooks?
★ How many books do you read in a month?
Where is your favorite quiet place to read at home?
★ Do you read before going to sleep?
Who is a famous author from your country?
★ Have you ever met a published writer?
How often do you visit a public library or local bookstore?
★ Did you borrow books from libraries as a student?
Do you use a pretty bookmark or fold the corner of a page?
★ Do you collect handmade bookmarks?
Does an attractive book cover make you want to read it?
★ Have you ever judged a book by its cover?
What interesting story did a friend recommend to you recently?
★ Do you share book recommendations with friends?
How many pages can you read in one sitting?
★ Do you read fast or slow?
Why do people enjoy time to read during summer holidays?
★ What book are you reading right now?
🟣 Let's Speak Together▲

A comparative reflection on our habits and self-perceptions.

🟢 Round 2 : Debates & Future Outlook▲
Paper books are much better than digital e-readers. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you own an e-reader device?
Listening to an audiobook is the same as reading a physical book. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you listen to audiobooks while commuting?
Public library buildings will always remain important community centers. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ When did you last visit your neighborhood library?
Every child should learn to love reading at an early age. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Who taught you how to read?
A compelling story in a book is always better than its movie adaptation. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What movie adaptation disappointed you?
A famous author has a duty to teach moral lessons in their books. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Which author inspired you the most?
Reading in a quiet environment is essential for deep focus. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Can you read with background music or noise?
Using a proper bookmark shows respect for physical books. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ What do you use to mark your page?
Designing a striking book cover is as important as writing good text. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Have you bought a book just for its design?
Reading 10 pages every single day can change a person's habits. : Do you agree or disagree?
★ Do you set daily reading goals?
✏️ Teacher's Note (Linguistic Corrections)▲
I like to reading books. → I like reading books. (Use 'like + verb-ing' or 'like + to + infinitive', not both together.)
She read every day. → She reads every day. (Present simple 3rd person singular needs '-s': 'she reads'.)
