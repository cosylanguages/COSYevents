mistakes:
  - wrong: An asteroid impact <u>in Earth</u> is dangerous.
    right: An asteroid impact <strong>on earth</strong> is dangerous.
    note: '(Preposition check: use ''on'' with ''impact'' when referring to the object hit.)'
  - wrong: Scientists can <u>to predict</u> where it goes.
    right: Scientists can <strong>predict</strong> where it goes.
    note: '(Modal verb error: ''can'' is followed by the bare infinitive ''predict'' without ''to''.)'
