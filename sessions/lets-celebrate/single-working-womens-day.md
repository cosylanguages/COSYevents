  instruction: ''
  questions:
    - What is the single greatest advantage of living and working as an independent person?

round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : Career &amp; Society'
  badge: Discussion
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: Why is financial <strong>independence</strong> considered the absolute foundation of modern women's freedom?
      personal: ★ Do you consider yourself fully financially independent, or are you aiming to achieve it soon?
    - main: How does psychological <strong>empowerment</strong> manifest in the workplace among single female professionals?
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt a strong wave of professional <strong>empowerment</strong> after completing a major task?
    - main: Is absolute <strong>self-sufficiency</strong> always positive, or can it lead to a stubborn refusal to ask for help?
      personal: ★ Do you find it easy to delegate tasks, or do you prefer to handle everything on your own?
    - main: Why is achieving a real <strong>work-life balance</strong> uniquely challenging for individuals who live completely alone?
      personal: ★ How do you separate your professional duties from your personal relaxation time?
    - main: What are the most effective strategies to finally shatter the invisible <strong>glass ceiling</strong> in traditional corporate structures?
      personal: ★ Have you ever witnessed or experienced a professional barrier based solely on gender?
    - main: Why should modern companies extend benefits like flexible hours beyond traditional <strong>maternity leave</strong> programs?
      personal: ★ Does your current workplace offer a supportive, flexible schedule for its employees?
    - main: How do personal relationships impact a woman's speed of <strong>career advancement</strong> in competitive fields?
      personal: '★ What is more important to you right now: fast <strong>career advancement</strong> or personal exploration?'
    - main: How can female <strong>solidarity</strong> help overcome systemic biases and support young women entering the workforce?
      personal: ★ Have you ever had a mentor who offered valuable guidance during your early career years?
    - main: Why does modern society still occasionally treat <strong>singlehood</strong> as a problem to be solved rather than a valid choice?
      personal: ★ Do you agree that <strong>singlehood</strong> is a beautiful phase of life that everyone should enjoy and celebrate?
    - main: What is the most stubborn cultural <strong>stereotype</strong> regarding working women that we must actively dismantle today?
      personal: ★ Have you ever had to actively challenge a fixed social <strong>stereotype</strong> in your personal life?

round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : Agree or Disagree: The Future'
  badge: Debate
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: In the future, the concept of a "lifetime career" will disappear, making professional <strong>independence</strong> a basic survival skill for everyone.
      personal: ★ Do you think that having multiple different jobs throughout your lifetime is exciting or stressful?
    - main: True <strong>empowerment</strong> in the corporate world is impossible as long as success is measured solely by the number of hours spent at a desk.
      personal: ★ Would you accept a shorter work week if it meant a slight delay in your promotion path?
    - main: The rise of extreme <strong>self-sufficiency</strong> is slowly destroying traditional family values and creating a lonely, disconnected society.
      personal: ★ Do you think modern city life makes people too individualistic and disconnected from their neighbors?
    - main: Achieving a perfect <strong>work-life balance</strong> is a complete myth created by marketing departments to sell productivity apps.
      personal: ★ Do you use any specific digital tools or habits to protect your personal relaxation time?
    - main: The invisible <strong>glass ceiling</strong> exists primarily because of historic, unconscious bias rather than a lack of qualified female candidates.
      personal: ★ Do you think bias in the workplace is decreasing with the arrival of younger managers?
    - main: Providing generous, paid <strong>maternity leave</strong> should be treated as an essential societal investment rather than an expense for businesses.
      personal: ★ Does your country offer sufficient support for working parents, or is there major room for improvement?
    - main: Prioritizing fast <strong>career advancement</strong> over personal relationships leads to a hollow, unfulfilled middle age.
      personal: '★ What brings you more genuine, long-term satisfaction: a professional achievement or a shared memory?'
    - main: Genuine <strong>solidarity</strong> among coworkers is impossible in highly competitive, bonus-driven corporate environments.
      personal: ★ Have you ever felt that a colleague was actively competing against you instead of cooperating?
    - main: In a few decades, <strong>singlehood</strong> will become the most common lifestyle choice in major global metropolises.
      personal: ★ Do you think cities are being physically redesigned to suit single-person households?
    - main: Media representation still reinforces the harmful <strong>stereotype</strong> that successful working women are cold and unemotional.
      personal: ★ Can you name a film or series that portrays a successful working woman in a realistic and positive way?
mistakes:

    right: We discussed career advancement.
    note: '(Underlined error: ''discuss'' is a transitive verb in English and takes no preposition like ''about'')'
  - wrong: She works very hard to <u>shatter the glass roof</u>.
    right: She works very hard to <strong>shatter the glass ceiling</strong>.
    note: '(Idiomatic word choice improvement: the standard English idiom is ''glass ceiling'', not ''glass roof'')'
