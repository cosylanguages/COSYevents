mistakes:
  - wrong: I am very <u>grateful of</u> my teacher.
    right: I am very <strong>grateful to</strong> my teacher / grateful for my teacher's help.
    note: '(Preposition check: ''grateful'' is followed by ''to'' for people, and ''for'' for actions/things.)'
  - wrong: We discussed <u>about our someone</u> special.
    right: We discussed our special someone.
    note: '(Preposition error: the verb ''discuss'' is transitive and does not take ''about''.)'
