warm_up:
  instruction: ''
  questions:
    - What is the most beautiful natural place or national park you have ever visited?
    - What small environmental habit do you practice every single day?
round_1:
  title: 'Round 1 : General Discussion'
  badge: Questions
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: What is the primary goal of World Nature <strong>conservation</strong>, and why was this global day established?
      personal: ※ Have you ever participated in a voluntary tree planting or park cleanup event?
    - main: Why is every animal and plant species essential to maintain a stable <strong>ecosystem</strong>?
      personal: ※ What wild animals or birds live in the green areas near your home?
    - main: How does global warming affect biological <strong>biodiversity</strong> in cold mountain environments?
      personal: ※ Do you prefer spending your holidays in high mountains or near the ocean?
    - main: What does agricultural <strong>sustainability</strong> look like in countries with very limited rain?
      personal: ※ Do you try to save tap water when washing dishes or taking a shower?
    - main: Why is <strong>preserving</strong> historical rivers and lakes so critical for nearby cities?
      personal: ※ Is the river or lake in your hometown clean enough for swimming?
    - main: What is the main biological threat facing an <strong>endangered</strong> mammal species?
      personal: ※ What is your favorite wild animal, and is it protected by conservation laws?
    - main: How can urban development be planned to protect wild <strong>habitat</strong> corridors?
      personal: ※ Does your city have enough parks, botanical gardens, and green spaces?
    - main: How does chemical and industrial <strong>pollution</strong> alter the temperature of our atmosphere?
      personal: ※ Do you think air pollution in your city is getting better or worse?
    - main: What is required to achieve complete ecological <strong>restoration</strong> after a major forest fire?
      personal: ※ Have you ever witnessed a major natural disaster or extreme weather event?
    - main: Why is protecting natural resources a shared global <strong>responsibility</strong> rather than a local one?
      personal: '※ Who do you think should pay for clean energy: taxpayers or private companies?'

round_2:
  title: 'Round 2 : Agree or Disagree: The Future'
  badge: Predictions
  instruction: ''
  items:
    - main: In tomorrow's world, will global <strong>conservation</strong> efforts be governed by AI satellite networks instead of human rangers?
      personal: ※ Would you trust an automated drone to protect national park boundaries?
    - main: Will every urban <strong>ecosystem</strong> be artificial, with vertical green walls replacing real ground grass?
      personal: ※ Do you think vertical farming can completely replace traditional horizontal fields?
    - main: As we colonize other planets, will earthly <strong>biodiversity</strong> be permanently preserved only as stored genetic databases?
      personal: ※ If you could travel to a colony on Mars, would you go, or would you stay on Earth?
    - main: Will the concept of environmental <strong>sustainability</strong> become obsolete because of synthetic clean energy breakthroughs?
      personal: ※ Do you believe cheap unlimited fusion energy will solve all pollution problems?
    - main: Will <strong>preserving</strong> pristine wild rivers become a luxurious tourist privilege reserved only for the wealthy?
      personal: ※ Should access to wild national parks always remain completely free for everyone?
    - main: Will we clone and restore every <strong>endangered</strong> mammal back to health using advanced DNA editing techniques?
      personal: ※ Would you support cloning a woolly mammoth to release it in modern cold forests?
    - main: Will wild animal <strong>habitat</strong> areas expand dramatically as human populations move into compact, high-rise smart cities?
      personal: ※ Would you prefer to live in a highly dense vertical city to let nature recover around it?
    - main: Will plastic and oil-based <strong>pollution</strong> be completely eliminated by biodegradable vegetable packaging?
      personal: ※ Do you try to avoid buying items wrapped in multiple layers of plastic?
    - main: Will the <strong>restoration</strong> of damaged oceans require hundreds of years of international cooperative funding?
      personal: ※ Do you think countries should work closer together to protect international waters?
    - main: Will individual <strong>responsibility</strong> be replaced by automatic carbon taxes tracked directly through credit card purchases?
      personal: ※ Would you accept a card that limited your purchases based on your personal carbon budget?
mistakes:
  - wrong: We must protect the <u>natures</u>.
    right: We must protect <strong>nature</strong>.
    note: '(Underlined error: ''nature'' is an uncountable noun and does not have a plural form when referring to the environment)'
  - wrong: This species is <u>in danger of extinction</u>.
    right: This species is <strong>endangered / in danger of extinction</strong>.
    note: (Both forms are correct! 'Endangered' is a highly efficient adjective synonym)
