vocabulary:
  - word: regional cuisine
    definition: the traditional cooking style of a particular area.
    example: Ligurian regional cuisine is famous for pesto and fresh seafood.
  - word: seasonal ingredient
    definition: a food that is at its best and most plentiful at a particular time of year.
    example: We try to buy seasonal ingredients at the local market.
  - word: al dente
    definition: cooked until firm when bitten, used especially for pasta and rice.
    example: The pasta should be al dente, not soft.
  - word: antipasto
    definition: a small dish served before the main meal.
    example: We shared an antipasto of olives, cheese, and cured meat.
  - word: primo
    definition: the first course of a traditional Italian meal, often pasta, rice, or soup.
    example: For my primo I ordered a simple tomato pasta.
  - word: secondo
    definition: the main course of a traditional Italian meal, usually meat or fish.
    example: The secondo was grilled fish with lemon.
  - word: contorno
    definition: a side dish, usually vegetables or salad, served with the main course.
    example: We had roasted vegetables as a contorno.
  - word: protected designation of origin
    definition: an official label that protects the name and quality of a regional food product.
    example: Parmigiano Reggiano has a protected designation of origin.
  - word: food waste
    definition: good food that is thrown away instead of being eaten.
    example: Smaller portions can help restaurants reduce food waste.
  - word: recipe
    definition: a set of instructions for preparing a dish.
    example: My grandmother never wrote her recipe down.

mistakes:
  - wrong: 'I am agree with this idea'
    right: 'I agree with this idea'
    note: '(agree is a verb, not an adjective)'
  - wrong: 'The pasta is cook al dente'
    right: 'The pasta is cooked al dente'
    note: '(passive voice: is + past participle)'
  - wrong: 'We discussed about the menu'
    right: 'We discussed the menu'
    note: '(discuss does not take about)'
  - wrong: 'I prefer the region of Tuscany than Sicily'
    right: 'I prefer Tuscany to Sicily'
    note: '(prefer A to B)'
