🔐 Public & Access-Gated Session Policy

All event titles, themes, CEFR levels, and public blurbs are freely accessible to everyone. Full session materials, prompt decks, recordings, and past-session archives are restricted to registered participants and hosts.

How access works: Paid attendees and facilitators receive an unlisted session link (hard-to-guess URL) upon registration to access full session interactive tools and study materials.
